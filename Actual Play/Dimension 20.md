# Dimension 20

**Format**: High-production streaming actual play
**System**: D&D 5E (primarily), various others
**First Episode**: September 19, 2018
**Cast**: Brennan Lee Mulligan (DM) with rotating cast of comedians and performers
**Platform**: Dropout streaming service, YouTube (select content)
**Status**: Ongoing with multiple seasons

## Overview

Dimension 20 is a high-production-value actual play show produced by CollegeHumor (now Dropout) that has become one of the most popular and acclaimed actual play series in the TTRPG space. Premiering in September 2018, the show distinguishes itself through its combination of professional comedy talent, cinematic production values, creative worldbuilding, and Dungeon Master Brennan Lee Mulligan's virtuosic performance.

The show balances genuine comedy with emotional storytelling, featuring elaborate custom sets, professional miniatures and terrain, dynamic lighting, and sophisticated editing. While primarily using D&D 5th Edition, Dimension 20 has explored numerous other systems across its various seasons, each typically running 10-20 episodes as standalone stories.

Dimension 20's significance lies in several key areas:
- **Production standards**: Setting new benchmarks for actual play production quality
- **Comedy and heart**: Balancing laughs with genuine emotional stakes
- **Creative worldbuilding**: Unique, genre-bending settings for each season
- **Accessibility**: Tight editing and standalone seasons lower barrier to entry
- **Business model**: Demonstrating subscription streaming viability for actual play
- **Diverse representation**: Inclusive casting and storytelling

The show has cultivated a passionate fanbase, influenced actual play production standards industry-wide, and demonstrated that actual play content could justify premium streaming service subscription models.

## History and Development

### Origins and Fantasy High (2018)

Dimension 20 was created by Brennan Lee Mulligan, who had been working at CollegeHumor as a writer and performer. Drawing on his experience as a longtime DM and improviser, Brennan pitched an actual play show to CollegeHumor's leadership.

The first season, "Fantasy High," premiered on September 19, 2018, on the newly launched Dropout streaming service (CollegeHumor's subscription platform). The show featured:

**Original Cast ("The Intrepid Heroes")**:
- Brennan Lee Mulligan (DM)
- Emily Axford (Fig Faeth)
- Brian Murphy (Riz Gukgak)
- Siobhan Thompson (Adaine Abernant)
- Zac Oyama (Gorgug Thistlespring)
- Lou Wilson (Fabian Seacaster)
- Ally Beardsley (Kristen Applebees)

**Setting and Concept**: Fantasy High took place in Elmville, exploring "what if high school, but D&D?" - a genre-blending concept mixing high school drama with fantasy adventure.

The season was an immediate success, demonstrating that:
- Professional comedy talent could elevate actual play
- High production values enhanced rather than distracted from gameplay
- Standalone seasons with conclusions appealed to viewers
- Genre-blending settings offered fresh takes on D&D

### Expansion and Sophomore Year (2019-2020)

Following Fantasy High's success, Dimension 20 rapidly expanded:

**Season 2: Escape from the Bloodkeep** (2019): A "villain campaign" featuring a guest cast playing evil characters in a parody of classic fantasy tropes. This established the rotating cast format.

**Season 3: The Unsleeping City** (2019): Modern-day New York City with hidden magic, featuring the Intrepid Heroes in a contemporary fantasy setting. This demonstrated the show's range beyond traditional fantasy.

**Season 4: Tiny Heist** (2020): Heist comedy featuring tiny people, using a guest cast and showing system flexibility.

**Season 5: A Crown of Candy** (2020): Game of Thrones-style political intrigue in a world made of candy and food. This season became legendary for its darkness, emotional weight, and Brennan's willingness to challenge his players with deadly consequences. The finale's emotional conclusion, which reduced Brennan to tears, became one of actual play's most memorable moments.

**Season 6: Fantasy High: Sophomore Year** (2020): The Intrepid Heroes return for their second year at Aguefort Adventuring Academy, continuing the original story.

### COVID Era and Format Innovation (2020-2021)

The pandemic forced production changes:

**Pirates of Leviathan** (2020): First remotely filmed season using digital tools
**The Unsleeping City Chapter II** (2021): Continued remote production with improved technology
**Innovation**: Developed techniques for maintaining energy and production quality remotely

### Post-COVID Expansion (2021-Present)

Return to in-person production with expanded ambitions:

**Seasons Include**:
- **Mice & Murder** (2021): Murder mystery with animal characters
- **Misfits and Magic** (2021): Harry Potter parody with American exchange students
- **The Seven** (2021): All-female adventuring party in the Fantasy High universe
- **A Starstruck Odyssey** (2022): Space opera using Kids on Bikes system
- **A Court of Fey and Flowers** (2022): Regency romance in faerie realm
- **Neverafter** (2022-2023): Horror take on fairy tales
- **Dungeons and Drag Queens** (2023): Guest season with drag performers
- **Burrow's End** (2023): Family of stoats, emotional survival story
- **Fantasy High: Junior Year** (2024): Third season with the Intrepid Heroes

This expansion demonstrated Dimension 20's format versatility and Brennan's range as a DM across genres and tones.

## Format and Production

### Production Values

Dimension 20 sets industry-leading standards for actual play production:

**Set Design**:
- Custom-built sets for each season
- Elaborate dioramas and terrain
- Professional miniatures (often custom-made)
- Dynamic lighting systems
- Practical effects and props

**Technical Quality**:
- Multi-camera setup with professional switching
- Professional audio engineering
- Color grading and post-production
- Motion graphics and effects
- Custom animations for title sequences

**Editing**:
- Sessions edited down to 90-120 minute episodes
- Removing slower moments while preserving story
- Enhanced pacing compared to unedited streams
- Multiple editors ensuring quality

### Episode Structure

Typical Dimension 20 season follows a pattern:

**Format**:
- 10-20 episodes per season
- Episodes run 90-120 minutes (edited from longer sessions)
- "Adventuring Party" after-show discussions (Dropout exclusive)
- Behind-the-scenes content and bonus materials

**Season Structure**:
- Most seasons are standalone, complete stories
- Some seasons share universes (Fantasy High, Unsleeping City)
- Mix of main cast "Intrepid Heroes" seasons and guest cast "sidequest" seasons
- Typically 2-3 seasons per year

### DM Style: Brennan Lee Mulligan

Brennan's DM approach has become iconic:

**Preparation**:
- Extensive worldbuilding and NPC preparation
- Multiple contingency plans for player actions
- Deep system knowledge enabling smooth rulings
- Pre-planned dramatic moments and reveals

**Performance**:
- Virtuosic NPC characterizations with distinct voices
- High-energy descriptions and storytelling
- Physical comedy and dramatic gestures
- Emotional vulnerability and genuine reactions

**Philosophy**:
- "Yes, and" from improv training
- Consequences matter - PC deaths possible
- Rewarding creative player solutions
- Balancing comedy with serious stakes
- Building on player backstories and choices

**Teaching**:
- Clear mechanical explanations
- Making rules accessible to viewers
- Demonstrating good table dynamics
- Showing how to handle difficult situations

Brennan's style has influenced a generation of DMs and become aspirational (sometimes problematically, see "Brennan Effect").

## Influence on DMing and Play Culture

### The "Brennan Effect"

Similar to the "Mercer Effect," expectations created by Brennan's DMing:

**Positive Impacts**:
- Inspiring DMs to improve storytelling
- Demonstrating importance of preparation
- Showing value of character voices and description
- Encouraging emotional investment

**Potential Negatives**:
- Unrealistic expectations for amateur DMs
- Pressure to match professional-level performance
- Comparison causing DM anxiety
- Overlooking that Brennan is professional with staff support

**Community Response**:
- Brennan himself emphasizes "your table is the best table"
- Discussions about managing expectations
- Recognition of professional vs. home game differences
- Celebrating diverse DM styles

### DMing Techniques

Brennan's approaches that DMs emulate:

**Preparation Methods**:
- Detailed NPC preparation
- Flexible planning for player agency
- Building on character backstories
- Creating meaningful choices

**Table Management**:
- Spotlight rotation ensuring all players shine
- Handling rules disputes smoothly
- Maintaining energy and pacing
- Balancing comedy and drama

**Storytelling**:
- Foreshadowing and payoffs
- Thematic coherence
- Emotional beats and catharsis
- Genre awareness and subversion

## Controversies and Criticisms

### Production Accessibility

High production values create concerns:

**Issues**:
- Setting unrealistic expectations for home games
- Implying D&D requires expensive props and sets
- Potential barrier to new players thinking they need equipment
- Class dynamics (wealthy production vs. home games)

**Responses**:
- Cast emphasizes any D&D is good D&D
- Recognition that it's entertainment, not typical game
- Community discussions about home game vs. production

### Representation Debates

Despite commitment to diversity, occasional criticisms:

**Concerns**:
- Handling of certain cultural elements
- Representation vs. stereotypes in some characterizations
- Power dynamics in casting and creative decisions

**Responses**:
- Cast generally receptive to feedback
- Evolution and improvement over seasons
- Transparency about learning and growth

### Combat vs. Roleplay Balance

Some viewers debate mechanical play vs. storytelling:

**Criticisms**:
- Some seasons light on combat
- Rules sometimes bent for story
- Mechanical optimization vs. character choices

**Defense**:
- Different seasons offer different balances
- Story takes priority in this format
- Variety across seasons provides options
