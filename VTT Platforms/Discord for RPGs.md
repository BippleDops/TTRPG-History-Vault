# Discord for RPGs

**Launched**: 2015 (Discord), RPG adoption accelerated 2016+
**Developer**: Discord Inc.
**Platform**: Voice/text chat application
**Pricing**: Free (Nitro optional)

## Overview

Discord, launched 2015 as gaming voice chat platform, became dominant communication tool for online RPG play through free high-quality voice channels, text chat, dice bot integration (Avrae, Sidekick), screen sharing enabling virtual table-tops, and server-based community organization, revolutionizing online gaming by providing superior free alternative to Skype/Google Hangouts while enabling persistent communities through servers, influencing how RPGs transitioned online especially during 2020 pandemic when Discord became essential infrastructure for gaming continuation.

**Widespread Adoption**: Millions of RPG players use Discord

**Free and Robust**: High-quality voice/video/text without payment

**Bot Ecosystem**: Dice rollers (Avrae for D&D, others), character sheet integration, music bots

**Server Communities**: Persistent homes for gaming groups and communities

**Pandemic Essential**: Became crucial when in-person impossible

**Impact**: Transformed online RPG infrastructure. Became default communication platform. Enabled community building at scale.

## Features for RPGs

**Voice Channels**: High-quality voice chat

**Video**: Screen sharing and webcams

**Text Channels**: Separate channels for IC/OOC, dice rolls, rules discussions

**Bots**: Avrae (D&D automation), dice rollers, character sheets, music

**Organization**: Servers with roles, channels, permissions

**Persistent**: Ongoing communities not just session chat

**Mobile**: Apps enabling gaming anywhere

**Screen Share**: Sharing VTT, maps, handouts

## Cultural Impact

**Default Platform**: Became assumed online gaming infrastructure

**Community Building**: Servers creating persistent homes

**Pandemic Gaming**: Essential during COVID for online continuation

**West Marches Revival**: Discord enables large-group coordination

**Living Campaigns**: Persistent servers maintaining ongoing games

**Bot Ecosystem**: Extensive RPG-specific bot development

**Replacing Forums**: Discord servers replacing traditional forums

## Reception

Universally adopted. Some prefer specialized VTT integration but Discord's quality and price (free) made it dominant. Privacy/moderation concerns exist but utility won.

## Legacy

Became essential RPG infrastructure. Enabled online gaming at scale. Community servers proliferated. Standard recommendation for online play.
