# Owlbear Rodeo

**Launched**: 2020
**Developer**: Owlbear Rodeo team
**Platform**: Browser-based VTT
**Pricing**: Free

## Overview

Owlbear Rodeo, launched 2020, provides radically minimalist free browser-based VTT requiring no accounts, no downloads, just upload map and place tokens, demonstrating during pandemic that sometimes simplest solution is best—while Roll20 and Foundry offered comprehensive features, Owlbear succeeded by doing ONE thing well (shared tactical maps) without complexity overhead, attracting users valuing simplicity over features, showing market existed for accessible lightweight VTT alternative to feature-heavy platforms.

**Minimalist Philosophy**: Only essential features—maps, tokens, fog of war

**No Account**: Start using immediately without registration

**Free**: Completely free, donation-supported

**Browser-Based**: No downloads, works anywhere

**Pandemic Success**: Launched perfect timing when online play surged

**Impact**: Demonstrated minimalism viable VTT approach. Provided accessible entry point. Showed free sustainable through donations.

## Features

**Core**:
- Upload maps
- Place tokens
- Move pieces
- Fog of war
- Dice roller
- Drawing tools

**Intentionally Limited**: No character sheets, automation, rules integration

**Simplicity**: Anyone can use immediately

## Cultural Impact

**Pandemic Timing**: Perfect launch during COVID online gaming surge

**Minimalism Validation**: Less can be more

**Free Model**: Sustainable through donations

**Accessibility**: Lowest barrier to entry VTT

## Reception

Praised for elegant simplicity, accessibility, free model. Some want more features but that misses point—it succeeds through minimalism.

## Legacy

Demonstrated minimalist VTT viability. Provided accessible free option. Showed donation model could sustain operations.
