# World Anvil

**Launched**: 2017
**Developer**: World Anvil
**Platform**: Web-based worldbuilding and campaign management
**Pricing**: Freemium (free + subscription)

## Overview

World Anvil, launched 2017, provides comprehensive worldbuilding platform with wiki-style organization, relationship maps, timelines, family trees, and campaign management tools, serving GMs and writers creating elaborate fictional worlds by offering structured templates and interconnected databases for everything from deities to tavern menus, becoming leading worldbuilding platform with hundreds of thousands of users, demonstrating market existed for dedicated world-creation tools beyond simple note-taking, though complexity and feature-richness created steep learning curve requiring significant investment.

**Worldbuilding Focus**: Not VTT but world-creation platform

**Wiki-Style**: Interconnected articles and entries

**Templates**: Structured formats for locations, characters, organizations, etc.

**Relationship Mapping**: Visual connections between elements

**Timelines**: Historical progression tools

**Maps**: Interactive map creation

**Campaign Management**: Session planning and notes

**Community**: Sharing worlds publicly, following others' creations

**Writers + GMs**: Serves both fiction writers and game masters

## Features

**Free Tier**:
- Limited worlds
- Basic templates
- Public worlds

**Subscription Tiers** ($5-$15/month):
- Private worlds
- More worlds
- Advanced features
- Custom styling
- No ads

**Templates**: Pre-structured for:
- Locations (cities, buildings, regions)
- Characters (NPCs, deities, monsters)
- Organizations (guilds, governments, religions)
- Items (artifacts, weapons)
- Vehicles, technologies, magic systems
- Literally everything

**Relationship Maps**: Visual connections

**Timelines**: Historical events

**Interactive Maps**: Clickable locations

**Secrets System**: Information visible only to specific players/GM

**Campaign Tools**: Session planning, quest tracking

## Cultural Impact

**Worldbuilding Platform**: Became leading dedicated tool

**GM Resource**: Essential for elaborate world creators

**Writer Community**: Fiction writers using for non-gaming

**Template Culture**: Structured worldbuilding

**Social Worldbuilding**: Public sharing and following

**Complexity**: Feature-rich but overwhelming for some

## Reception

**Praise**: Comprehensive worldbuilding tools, interconnected organization, relationship mapping, supports elaborate worlds

**Criticism**: Overwhelming complexity, steep learning curve, subscription for full features, can become worldbuilding procrastination

## For RPG Use

**Advantages**: Comprehensive organization, campaign wiki, player access, structured templates

**Disadvantages**: Not VTT (no tactical play), complexity, requires investment, subscription for full features

**Use Cases**: GMs with elaborate detailed worlds, published settings, shared campaign wikis

## Legacy

Established as leading worldbuilding platform. Demonstrated dedicated world-creation tools market. Influenced how GMs organize complex settings. Continues growing and developing.
