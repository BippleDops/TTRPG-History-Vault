## Overview

Shard Tabletop emerged in 2020 as a modern, web-based VTT platform designed to combine the accessibility of browser-based tools with sophisticated character management and rules automation. Founded by developers and tabletop gaming enthusiasts who saw opportunities to improve upon existing platforms, Shard emphasizes seamless integration between character creation, campaign management, and live play, with particular focus on supporting Pathfinder and D&D 5E with deep mechanical implementation.

The platform distinguishes itself through its comprehensive character builder that rivals standalone tools like D&D Beyond, integrated directly into the VTT experience. This integration allows players to create and manage characters, track resources, and execute actions without switching between multiple tools or tabs. Shard's automation handles complex game mechanics, spell effects, and character abilities, reducing GM workload and streamlining gameplay while maintaining the flexibility needed for house rules and customization.

Launched during the COVID-19 pandemic when VTT demand surged, Shard positioned itself as a modern alternative that learned from both the successes and shortcomings of established platforms. While competing in a crowded market against Roll20's massive user base and Foundry VTT's momentum, Shard carved out a niche among players seeking integrated character management with VTT functionality, particularly within the Pathfinder community where official support and deep automation provided significant value.
