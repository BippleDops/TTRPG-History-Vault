# Astral Tabletop

**Launched**: 2018
**Developer**: Astral TableTop LLC
**Platform**: Browser-based VTT
**Pricing**: Freemium (free + subscription tiers)

## Overview

Astral Tabletop, launched 2018, provides feature-rich browser-based VTT with freemium model offering substantial free tier (unlimited players, maps, basic features) plus paid subscription for advanced features (dynamic lighting, fog of war automation, marketplace access), demonstrating browser-based VTT could compete with desktop applications while maintaining accessibility through free tier, though facing intense competition from Foundry VTT and Roll20 in crowded market.

**Browser-Based**: No downloads required

**Freemium**: Free tier with paid upgrades

**3D Dice**: Physics-based dice rolling

**Marketplace**: Content ecosystem

**Character Sheets**: Integrated management

**Competition**: Entered crowded market with Roll20 dominant, Foundry rising

**Challenge**: Differentiating in saturated VTT landscape

## Features

**Free Tier**:
- Unlimited players and maps
- Basic tools
- Character sheets
- Dice rolling

**Paid Tiers**:
- Dynamic lighting
- Fog of war automation
- Marketplace access
- Advanced features

**3D Dice**: Physics simulation for dice

**Automation**: Some rule automation

**Marketplace**: Community/commercial content

## Cultural Impact

Browser-based alternative to Roll20. Free tier lowered barrier. 3D dice appealed to some users. Faced intense competition in maturing VTT market.

## Reception

Praised for 3D dice, free tier generosity, browser accessibility. Struggled against established platforms (Roll20) and rising competitors (Foundry). Maintains dedicated user base but not market leader.
