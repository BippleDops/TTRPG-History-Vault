# D&D Beyond

**Launched**: 2017
**Developer**: Originally Curse/Fandom, acquired by Wizards of the Coast 2022
**Platform**: Browser-based digital tools + VTT features
**Pricing**: Freemium (subscriptions + book purchases)

## Overview

D&D Beyond, launched 2017 and acquired by Wizards of the Coast 2022, provides official D&D 5E digital tools including character builder, digital books, dice roller, and VTT features (maps added later), becoming largest D&D digital platform with millions of users through being official WotC-endorsed solution, demonstrating demand for integrated character management even before full VTT features, with WotC acquisition (reported $146.3M) validating digital tools' importance to D&D's future as company integrated Beyond into core D&D strategy.

**Official**: WotC-endorsed (now owned) D&D digital platform

**Character Builder**: Primary strength—excellent character management

**Digital Books**: Licensed SRD and premium content

**Millions of Users**: Largest D&D digital platform

**WotC Acquisition 2022**: $146.3M purchase price showing strategic importance

**Evolution**: Started as character tools, added VTT features later

**Integration**: Becoming core D&D digital infrastructure

## Features

**Character Builder**:
- Step-by-step creation
- Automatic calculations
- Digital character sheets
- Mobile app

**Digital Books**: Licensed PHB, DMG, adventures

**Dice Roller**: Integrated with character abilities

**VTT Features** (added later):
- Maps and tokens
- Tactical play
- Integration with character data

**Homebrew**: Community content creation

**Campaigns**: Managing groups

## Business Evolution

**2017**: Launched by Curse/Fandom

**Partnership**: WotC licensed content

**Growth**: Millions adopted for character management

## Reception

Praised for excellent character builder, official content, integration. Criticized for paywall (books cost despite physical ownership), subscription model, VTT features lagging dedicated platforms. Massive user base despite criticisms.

## Legacy

Became D&D's official digital platform. WotC acquisition demonstrated digital importance. Character builder set standard. Influencing D&D's digital-first future.
