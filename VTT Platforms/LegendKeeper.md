## Overview

LegendKeeper, launched 2020, provides beautiful worldbuilding platform emphasizing visual appeal and user-friendly interface with interactive maps, wiki organization, and elegant design targeting GMs who want comprehensive worldbuilding tools without World Anvil's complexity or Obsidian's DIY setup, demonstrating market for middle-ground between simple notes and complex databases through gorgeous aesthetics and streamlined features.
