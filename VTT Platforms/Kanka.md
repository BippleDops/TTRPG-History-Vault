# Kanka

**Launched**: 2017
**Developer**: Kanka team
**Platform**: Web-based campaign management
**Pricing**: Freemium (free + subscription)

## Overview

Kanka, launched 2017, offers campaign management platform focusing on simpler more accessible interface than World Anvil—campaign wiki, character tracking, location management, quest tracking, calendar—providing GMs organizational tools for complex campaigns without overwhelming feature-richness, demonstrating market for streamlined campaign management between simple note-taking and comprehensive worldbuilding platforms, though competing in crowded space with World Anvil, Obsidian, Notion, and others.

**Simpler than World Anvil**: More accessible, less overwhelming

**Campaign Wiki**: Organized campaign information

**Free Tier**: Functional free version

**Character/Location/Quest Tracking**: Essential GM tools

**Relationship Mapping**: Visual connections

**Calendar**: Campaign timeline

**Streamlined**: Less features but easier to use

## Features

**Campaign Elements**:
- Characters, locations, organizations
- Quests, items, events
- Timelines and calendars
- Maps, journals

**Free Tier**: One campaign, basic features

**Subscription** (~$5/month): Multiple campaigns, advanced features

**Sharing**: Players can access campaign info

**Templates**: Reusable structures

## Cultural Impact

World Anvil alternative for users wanting simpler tools. Demonstrated spectrum of complexity in campaign management. Part of GM tools ecosystem.

## Reception

Praised for simplicity vs. World Anvil, functional free tier. Some want more features (then choose World Anvil). Serves users wanting middle ground between notes and comprehensive worldbuilding.
