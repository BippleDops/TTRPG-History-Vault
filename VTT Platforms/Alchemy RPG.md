# Alchemy RPG

**Platform Type**: Virtual Tabletop (VTT) - Map-Focused Web Platform
**Launched**: 2020
**Business Model**: Freemium (free tier + premium subscription)
**User Base**: 15,000+ users (estimated)

**Website**: https://alchemyrpg.com
**Status**: Active
**Impact on Industry**: 1/5

## Overview

Alchemy RPG launched in 2020 as a browser-based VTT platform distinguishing itself through an emphasis on integrated map creation and editing tools. While most VTTs require users to create maps externally and import them, Alchemy provides built-in map-making capabilities allowing game masters to design, edit, and play on custom maps without leaving the platform. This integration appeals to GMs who value convenience and iteration, enabling quick adjustments during preparation or even mid-session.

The platform positions itself as a streamlined solution for groups wanting full VTT functionality without complexity or cost barriers. Alchemy's free tier provides core features including the map editor, making it accessible to budget-conscious players while premium subscriptions offer enhanced tools and storage. The browser-based architecture requires no downloads or installations, lowering the barrier to entry for new VTT users or groups making quick transitions to online play.

Launched during the COVID-19 pandemic's peak VTT adoption period, Alchemy entered a crowded market dominated by Roll20's massive user base, Foundry VTT's rapid rise, and numerous other platforms. While never achieving significant market share, Alchemy carved out a small niche among users who prioritized integrated map creation and appreciated having design tools built into their VTT rather than relying on separate applications like Dungeondraft or Inkarnate.
