# Bully Pulpit Games

**Founded**: 2005
**Headquarters**: Chapel Hill, North Carolina, USA
**Founders**: [[Jason Morningstar]], Steve Segedy

## Overview

Bully Pulpit Games, founded 2005 by Jason Morningstar and Steve Segedy, became major indie publisher through Fiasco (2009—breakout GMless game selling 50,000+ copies), Night Witches (2015—ENnie Gold Soviet women pilots PbtA), Grey Ranks (2007—Warsaw Uprising tragedy), and Carolina Death Crawl (2014—Sherman's March), demonstrating indie publishers could achieve mainstream commercial success through GMless frameworks, historical authenticity, high production values, and accessible story game design that welcomed non-gamers alongside hobby veterans.

**Signature**: GMless structured games and deeply-researched historical titles

**Fiasco Success**: 50,000+ copies sold, mainstream breakthrough, introduced thousands to story games

**Production Quality**: Professional standards matching/exceeding mainstream publishers

**Historical Focus**: Morningstar's historical games (Night Witches, Grey Ranks, Carolina Death Crawl) set authenticity standards

**Commercial Achievement**: Proved indie story games viable commercially at scale

## Major Releases

### Fiasco (2009)

GMless Coen Brothers crime comedy creating disasters:
- 50,000+ copies sold (extraordinary for indie)
- 100+ published playsets
- Mainstream media coverage (Wil Wheaton's Tabletop)
- Non-gamer accessible
- Second edition 2018
- Bully Pulpit's flagship defining success

### Night Witches (2015)

PbtA Soviet women pilots WWII:
- ENnie Gold Best Game 2016
- Extensively researched 588th Regiment
- Historical gaming benchmark
- Critical acclaim, strong sales

### Grey Ranks (2007)

Warsaw Uprising teenage resistance fighters:
- Indie RPG Award
- Emotionally devastating historical tragedy
- Demonstrated difficult subjects handled well

### The Shab-al-Hiri Roach (2005)

Academic satire with Sumerian brain-parasites:
- Early Bully Pulpit release
- Dark comedy demonstrating range

### Other Releases

- Carolina Death Crawl (2014): Civil War Sherman's March
- Numerous supplements and playsets
- Consistent quality across catalog
