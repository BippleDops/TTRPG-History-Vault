# Possum Creek Games

**Founded**: 2019  
**Headquarters**: United States
**Founder**: [[Jay Dragon]]

## Overview

Possum Creek Games, founded 2019 by Jay Dragon, achieved breakthrough success with Wanderhome (2021—cozy pastoral animal-folk game with $272K Kickstarter, ENnie Gold 2022) and Sleepaway (2020—queer trans summer camp horror), specializing in Belonging Outside Belonging (No Dice No Masters) games that center queer/trans experiences while establishing "cozy gaming" as major genre, demonstrating explicitly queer-focused indie publisher could achieve mainstream commercial success through beautiful production, thoughtful design, and cultural moment alignment.

**Wanderhome**: Massive hit—$272K Kickstarter, ENnie Gold 2022 Best Game/Writing/Production

**Cozy Gaming**: Practically created/defined genre with Wanderhome

**Queer Trans Centering**: Explicit LGBTQ+ focus throughout catalog

**Belonging Outside Belonging**: Specializing in Avery Alder's No Dice No Masters framework

**Beautiful Production**: Gorgeous books with evocative art

**Cultural Moment**: Released during pandemic when cozy escapism resonated

## Major Releases

### Wanderhome (2021)

Pastoral animal-folk cozy gaming:
- $272K Kickstarter (on $25K goal)
- ENnie Gold 2022: Best Game, Writing, Production
- Best-selling indie 2021-2022
- Defined cozy gaming genre
- No combat, no failure—pure exploration and kindness

### Sleepaway (2020)

Queer trans summer camp horror:
- Breakthrough establishing Dragon as designer
- No Dice No Masters framework
- Queer horror successfully blending community and scares
- Critical acclaim within queer gaming

### Other Releases

Multiple Belonging Outside Belonging games continuing queer/cozy focus
