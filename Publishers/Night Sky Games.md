# Night Sky Games

**Founded**: 2006
**Headquarters**: United States  
**Founder**: [[Joshua AC Newman]], others

## Overview

Night Sky Games, founded 2006 associated with Joshua AC Newman, published Shock: Social Science Fiction (2006—Issue-Shock matrix collaborative worldbuilding), 1001 Nights (2006—Meguey Baker's Arabian Nights storytelling), and Mobile Frame Zero (2012—tactical mecha with narrative RPG), specializing in innovative social science fiction and experimental designs demonstrating literary sci-fi and unusual concepts could find audiences through focused quality publishing.

**Shock**: Literary social sci-fi through collaborative matrix worldbuilding

**1001 Nights**: Meguey Baker's nested-narrative political storytelling

**Mobile Frame Zero**: Tactical mecha-narrative hybrid

**Social Sci-Fi Focus**: Literary science fiction emphasis

**Experimental**: Supporting innovative unusual designs

## Publishing Philosophy

**Innovation Support**: Publishing experimental designs

**Social Science Fiction**: Literary sci-fi focus

**Design Excellence**: Quality over commercial compromise

**Small Operation**: Sustainable focused catalog

## Cultural Impact

Shock influenced social sci-fi gaming. 1001 Nights demonstrated storytelling-game viability. Mobile Frame Zero showed tactical-narrative hybrids. Small but influential catalog.
