# The Gauntlet Publishing

**Founded**: 2015
**Headquarters**: Online community (US-based)
**Founder**: Jason Cordova, others

## Overview

The Gauntlet Publishing, founded 2015 as online community becoming publisher, released Trophy Dark (2019—Jesse Ross's doomed treasure-hunters) and Trophy Gold (2020), while building inclusive welcoming gaming community through Codex zine, podcast, and online play facilitation, demonstrating how community-first approach could build publishing presence through supporting designers and fostering positive gaming culture, influencing inclusive community standards across hobby.

**Community First**: Started as inclusive online community, became publisher

**Trophy**: Jesse Ross's games establishing The Gauntlet as publisher

**Codex**: Community zine with designer contributions

**Inclusive Focus**: Explicitly welcoming, diverse, safe community

**Online**: Digital-native community and publishing
