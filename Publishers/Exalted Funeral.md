# Exalted Funeral

**Founded**: 2017
**Active Era**: Modern Era (2017-Present)
**Historical Significance**: ⭐ 4/5
**Headquarters**: Indianapolis, Indiana
**Status**: Active

## Company History

Exalted Funeral occupies unique position in modern roleplaying game industry as hybrid publisher-distributor whose curatorial vision fundamentally shaped indie and OSR gaming's commercial landscape. Founded by Matthew Combs, the company functions simultaneously as selective publisher of innovative games, distributor providing retail infrastructure for experimental designers, and taste-making curator identifying and promoting exceptional work. The company's journey from small online storefront through publishing Mörk Borg—revolutionary doom metal apocalyptic RPG—and distributing dozens of influential indie titles represents new model for supporting experimental gaming through integrated publishing, distribution, and curation rather than just traditional single-function approaches.

Matthew Combs brought distinctive vision combining appreciation for experimental design, understanding of retail economics, and recognition that indie gaming ecosystem needed better infrastructure for connecting creators with audiences. Traditional RPG distribution models failed experimental publishers—distributors demanded unsustainable discounts, retailers hesitated stocking unknown games, and creators lacked resources for professional distribution. Exalted Funeral addressed these systemic problems through integrated approach.

The company name "Exalted Funeral" evoked death-positive aesthetics, occult imagery, and dark humor pervading much indie gaming. The name signaled company's interest in darker gaming territories—horror, doom, death, apocalypse—while suggesting irreverent approach refusing to take darkness too seriously. This aesthetic positioning immediately communicated values and attracted compatible creators.

Exalted Funeral's founding in 2017 centered on creating better retail infrastructure for indie and OSR games. The initial model combined online storefront selling curated selection of experimental games with direct relationships with creators enabling better terms than traditional distribution. This hybrid approach provided creators viable path to market while offering customers carefully selected quality products.

The curatorial vision proved crucial differentiator. Rather than carrying everything or focusing narrowly on single tradition, Exalted Funeral curated across indie gaming, OSR, story games, and experimental designs united by quality and innovation. This eclectic but selective approach created brand identity—games carried by Exalted Funeral came recommended through selection itself.

Early operations focused on distribution, carrying titles from established indie publishers and innovative designers lacking distribution. This service orientation built relationships with creators while establishing Exalted Funeral as trusted source for experimental gaming. The company became known for responsive service, fair terms, and genuine enthusiasm for games carried.

However, Exalted Funeral's cultural impact accelerated dramatically with publishing Mörk Borg (2020), designed by Pelle Nilsson (Ockult Örtmästare Games) and Johan Nohr. This game presented apocalyptic fantasy world dying in final days, portrayed through radical visual design combining doom metal aesthetics, punk typography, and apocalyptic imagery with innovative layout subverting traditional RPG book design.

Mörk Borg's visual presentation proved immediately revolutionary. Johan Nohr's graphic design abandoned conventional two-column text layouts for dynamic, chaotic compositions where text placement, typography, and imagery created visceral impact. Yellow-and-pink color scheme, distressed textures, and doom metal aesthetics created unforgettable visual identity unlike any previous RPG.

The game's mechanical framework proved elegantly simple: traditional OSR fundamentals streamlined to minimum necessary components. Characters died easily, combat proved lethal, advancement almost nonexistent. This brutal minimalism suited apocalyptic setting where characters survived briefly before inevitable doom rather than building long-term power.

The dying world setting created distinctive campaign structure. Each session brought apocalypse closer through escalating Miseries destroying reality. This built-in countdown ensured campaigns ended definitively when world ended rather than continuing indefinitely. The structure suited OSR's often-short campaign arcs while providing thematic coherence.

Character creation's speed—creating competent character in minutes—enabled play-until-death-then-new-character approach. This reduced emotional investment in individual characters while focusing on experiencing doomed world's final days. The disposable characters suited apocalyptic tone perfectly.

Mörk Borg achieved extraordinary commercial and cultural success. The game won multiple awards including Best Game at ENnies, achieved viral social media spread, introduced thousands to OSR gaming through accessible doom metal package, and spawned extensive third-party content ecosystem. Most significantly, Mörk Borg demonstrated that radical visual design could drive RPG sales as effectively as mechanical innovation.

The game's success validated Exalted Funeral's publishing vision while providing resources for expansion. More importantly, Mörk Borg established company's reputation for identifying and publishing exceptional work, strengthening curator credibility.

Following Mörk Borg, Exalted Funeral published Trophy—anthology of related games designed by Jesse Ross and other contributors. Trophy Dark (2019) presented doomed treasure hunters descending into haunted forest inevitably corrupted or destroyed. The game's structure guaranteed tragic endings while giving players agency over how doom unfolded.

Trophy's mechanics used simple dice pool system where success came at cost of increasing corruption. Characters could accomplish goals but grew progressively darker, eventually succumbing to forest's malevolent influence. This doom spiral created inevitable tragedy while maintaining player choices' meaning.

Trophy Gold (2022) expanded Trophy Dark's framework for longer campaign play, adding advancement and treasure mechanics while maintaining corruption's inevitable pull. The game demonstrated how doom-focused framework could support extended play without losing tragic arc.

Trophy's critical success demonstrated Exalted Funeral could identify and publish excellent story games alongside OSR material. The game achieved awards recognition and built dedicated following appreciating thoughtful horror gaming.

Beyond direct publishing, Exalted Funeral's distribution role profoundly influenced indie gaming landscape. By providing viable distribution for experimental games, the company enabled creators to focus on design rather than retail logistics. This infrastructure support proved crucial for small publishers and individual designers lacking resources for traditional distribution.

The company's retail storefront—both online and eventual physical location in Indianapolis—provided curated destination for indie gaming. Rather than overwhelming selection, Exalted Funeral offered carefully chosen games representing quality and innovation. This curation helped customers navigate indie gaming's overwhelming diversity.

Exalted Funeral's Kickstarter distribution service provided crucial infrastructure for crowdfunding campaigns. Managing fulfillment, international shipping, and retail distribution allowed creators to focus on game development while ensuring professional campaign execution. This service supported numerous successful campaigns that might otherwise struggle with logistics.

The company's taste-making influence extended beyond commercial success to shaping aesthetic and design trends. Games distributed or published by Exalted Funeral achieved visibility and credibility, influencing what gained traction in broader indie gaming culture. This curatorial power carried responsibility exercised through consistent quality focus.

Physical storefront opening in Indianapolis created local gaming hub while demonstrating brick-and-mortar viability for indie gaming retail. The location hosted events, provided demo space, and created community gathering place supporting local gaming culture.

Throughout growth, Exalted Funeral maintained commitment to creator-friendly practices—fair terms, responsive communication, genuine enthusiasm for carried games. This ethical approach built lasting relationships and reputation as publisher-distributor creators wanted to work with rather than merely tolerated.

Today, Exalted Funeral operates as influential force in indie gaming, simultaneously publishing exceptional games, distributing experimental work, curating quality selection, and providing infrastructure enabling indie gaming's continued growth. The company exemplifies how integrated approach combining multiple functions can support ecosystem more effectively than single-function traditional models.

## Company History Expansion

Exalted Funeral's development reflected broader indie gaming's maturation and increasing commercial viability. The company emerged during period when indie and OSR games achieved growing mainstream recognition but lacked adequate distribution infrastructure connecting creators with audiences.

Traditional RPG distribution models proved inadequate for experimental gaming. Major distributors focused on established publishers with proven sales records. Retail stores hesitated stocking unknown indie games. Creators faced choice between poor distribution terms or handling all logistics themselves. This gap created opportunity for alternative models.

Geographic location in Indianapolis provided strategic advantages. Central US position enabled efficient shipping while avoiding coastal cities' high costs. Indianapolis gaming scene provided local community while distance from industry centers encouraged independent thinking.

The company's e-commerce focus suited changing retail landscape. Digital storefronts enabled global reach without physical retail's overhead. Online sales supported niche products viable through scattered customer base but failing geographic concentration requirements for traditional retail.

Matthew Combs' background and vision shaped company development. Understanding both creative and business aspects enabled building sustainable operation. Genuine enthusiasm for experimental gaming ensured curation reflected authentic appreciation rather than purely commercial calculation.

Early financial challenges tested commitment. Distribution required capital for inventory while margins remained modest. However, careful selection and growing reputation enabled gradual sustainable growth. Mörk Borg's success provided breakthrough enabling expansion.

The company's relationship with creator community proved crucial. By treating designers fairly, communicating honestly, and sharing genuine enthusiasm for their work, Exalted Funeral built trust translating to preferred distributor status among quality creators.

## Major Releases

### Mörk Borg (2020)

Mörk Borg revolutionized RPG visual design while creating commercially successful doom metal apocalyptic fantasy game combining brutal OSR mechanics with revolutionary graphic design. The game presented dying world in final days portrayed through radical aesthetics subverting traditional RPG book design.

Johan Nohr's graphic design proved immediately revolutionary. Rather than conventional two-column layouts, Mörk Borg used dynamic compositions where text placement, typography, and imagery created visceral impact. Yellow-pink-black color scheme, distressed textures, doom metal aesthetics, and punk typography created unforgettable visual identity.

The design choices served thematic purposes beyond mere aesthetics. Chaotic layouts reflected world's disintegration, harsh colors conveyed apocalyptic dread, distressed textures suggested corruption and decay. Visual presentation wasn't separate from game content but integral to communicating setting and tone.

Mechanical framework proved elegantly minimal: streamlined OSR fundamentals with brutal lethality and almost nonexistent advancement. Characters died easily, combat proved deadly, power progression barely existed. This minimalism suited apocalyptic setting where survival proved brief before inevitable doom.

The dying world setting provided distinctive campaign structure. Apocalyptic calendar tracked Miseries destroying reality, creating countdown toward inevitable end. This built-in conclusion ensured campaigns ended definitively rather than continuing indefinitely.

Character creation speed—generating competent character in minutes—enabled play-until-death-then-new-character approach. This reduced investment in individual characters while focusing on experiencing doomed world. Disposable characters suited apocalyptic tone where heroic triumph proved impossible.

Third-party content ecosystem emerged around Mörk Borg through Mörk Borg Third Party License enabling compatible content creation. This open approach spawned dozens of compatible adventures, settings, and hacks demonstrating game's cultural impact.

Mörk Borg achieved extraordinary success winning Best Game at ENnies, achieving viral social media spread, and introducing thousands to OSR through accessible doom metal package. Most significantly, the game proved radical visual design could drive sales as effectively as mechanical innovation.

### Trophy Dark (2019)

Trophy Dark presented doomed treasure hunters descending into haunted forest inevitably corrupted or destroyed, creating tragic horror framework guaranteeing terrible endings while giving players agency over how doom unfolded.

The game's structure embraced inevitable doom explicitly. Characters ventured into Forest seeking treasure but would be corrupted by dark forces or die trying. This guaranteed tragedy created different play experience than games promising potential victory.

Mechanics used simple dice pool system where success came at cost of increasing Ruin—corruption track measuring Forest's influence. Characters could accomplish goals but grew progressively darker, eventually succumbing through death or transformation.

Devil's Bargains offered characters advantages in exchange for complications or corruption. Players could choose accepting terrible costs for success or failing safely. This choice created dramatic decisions where every advantage carried price.

The Forest functioned as malevolent character actively corrupting hunters. Rather than passive environment, Forest offered temptations, whispered promises, and provided dark power in exchange for characters' humanity.

Trophy Dark influenced later doom-focused games demonstrating that inevitable tragedy could create compelling play through agency over how doom occurred rather than whether it occurred.

### Trophy Gold (2022)

Trophy Gold expanded Trophy Dark framework for longer campaign play while maintaining corruption's inevitable pull. The game added advancement, treasure mechanics, and extended play structures while preserving tragic arc.

The game maintained Trophy Dark's core doom spiral but stretched over longer timeframe. Characters could survive multiple expeditions, accumulate treasure, and grow more capable—but corruption accumulated across adventures until inevitable tragic end.

Treasure mechanics added material goals beyond mere survival. Characters sought specific artifacts, hoards, or magical items providing tangible objectives and resources for improvement. This traditional adventure gaming element coexisted with doom spiral.

Advancement systems let characters improve through experience while Ruin's accumulation ensured this improvement proved temporary. Characters became more effective treasure hunters but grew progressively corrupted until transformation or death.

The longer campaign structure created tragic arc across multiple sessions. Players developed investment in characters over time, making eventual doom more impactful than single-session character death.

Trophy Gold demonstrated how doom-focused framework could support traditional campaign play without losing tragic inevitability or thematic coherence.
