# Buried Without Ceremony

**Founded**: 2012
**Headquarters**: Canada  
**Founders**: [[Avery Alder]], Benjamin Rosenbaum

## Overview

Buried Without Ceremony, founded 2012 by Avery Alder and Benjamin Rosenbaum, published Monsterhearts (2012—queer teen monster PbtA), The Quiet Year (2013—map-drawing community game with Contempt mechanic), and Dream Askew/Dream Apart (2018—Belonging Outside Belonging creating No Dice No Masters movement), specializing in queer-centered GMless innovative designs establishing Alder as major designer while demonstrating community-focused publishing through itch.io and direct sales could build devoted audiences for sophisticated challenging games.

**Monsterhearts**: Queer teen supernatural PbtA with Strings

**The Quiet Year**: Map-drawing with brilliant Contempt mechanic

**Dream Askew/Dream Apart**: Created Belonging Outside Belonging framework (No Dice No Masters)

**Queer Focus**: Centering LGBTQ+ narratives

**Innovative Mechanics**: Strings, Contempt, No Dice No Masters

**Community**: itch.io and direct relationships with audiences

## Publishing Philosophy

**Queer Centering**: LGBTQ+ perspectives as default

**GMless Innovation**: Specializing in GMless frameworks

**Community-Focused**: Direct audience relationships

**Affordable Accessibility**: itch.io pay-what-you-want options

**Design Excellence**: Innovation and quality

**Small Sustainable**: Modest operation supporting designer vision

## Cultural Impact

**Monsterhearts**: Established queer teen supernatural gaming

**The Quiet Year**: Map-drawing genre creation

**Belonging Outside Belonging**: Created framework spawning movement (Wanderhome, etc.)

**Queer Gaming**: Helped establish LGBTQ+ gaming renaissance

**itch.io Model**: Demonstrated alternative to traditional distribution

## Legacy

Belonging Outside Belonging spawned thriving genre. Monsterhearts established queer teen gaming. The Quiet Year influenced map-drawing games. Alder established as major designer.
