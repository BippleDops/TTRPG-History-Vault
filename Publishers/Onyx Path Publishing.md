# Onyx Path Publishing

**Founded**: 2012
**Active Era**: Modern Era (2012-Present)
**Historical Significance**: ⭐ 4/5
**Headquarters**: Stone Mountain, Georgia
**Status**: Active

## Company History

Onyx Path Publishing emerged from the ashes of White Wolf Publishing's acquisition by CCP Games and subsequent absorption into Paradox Interactive, becoming the primary steward of the World of Darkness and Chronicles of Darkness game lines. Founded in 2012 by Rich Thomas and Matthew McFarland, Onyx Path represents an unusual publishing model: a company operating under license to continue beloved game lines while developing original properties. The company's journey from licensed continuation to independent creative force demonstrates resilience, community engagement, and commitment to preserving gaming legacy while innovating forward.

Rich Thomas brought decades of White Wolf experience, having served in various creative and management roles through the company's tumultuous history. His deep knowledge of World of Darkness properties, relationships with creative community, and understanding of fan expectations made him uniquely positioned to navigate the complex task of continuing these beloved lines under new structure. Matthew McFarland contributed design expertise and editorial leadership, having worked extensively on various World of Darkness products.

The founding circumstances were complex. When CCP Games acquired White Wolf in 2006 to leverage IP for MMO development, traditional tabletop RPG publishing became secondary priority. The merger with Paradox Interactive in 2015 further complicated matters. Throughout these corporate transitions, questions arose about whether classic World of Darkness lines would continue receiving support. Onyx Path emerged as solution: a licensed publisher employing many former White Wolf creators to continue developing properties under oversight of IP holders.

The company name referenced mythological River Styx and journey into underworld—appropriate metaphor for navigating darkness central to World of Darkness games. "Onyx Path" suggested dark beauty and dangerous journey, themes resonating with target audience.

From inception, Onyx Path embraced crowdfunding as primary business model. Traditional RPG publishing involves significant financial risk—printing books before knowing market demand, managing inventory, and working with uncertain sales projections. Kickstarter campaigns provided upfront funding, eliminated inventory risk, validated market interest before production, and built community engagement. This model proved essential for licensed publisher operating without major corporate backing.

Early projects focused on fulfilling community desires for updated editions of Chronicles of Darkness games. The "New World of Darkness" (later rebranded Chronicles of Darkness to distinguish from classic World of Darkness) had languished somewhat during CCP ownership. Fans wanted refined, complete second editions incorporating years of development insights and community feedback.

Vampire: The Requiem 2nd Edition (2013) established Onyx Path's approach. The game streamlined first edition's mechanics, clarified ambiguous rules, tightened clan and covenant concepts, and demonstrated that licensed continuation needn't mean mere reprinting. The second edition incorporated sophisticated design lessons while respecting original vision. The Kickstarter campaign's success validated both market demand and crowdfunding approach.

Werewolf: The Forsaken 2nd Edition (2014) continued this pattern, refining tribal concepts and spirit mechanics while maintaining what worked in first edition. Mage: The Awakening 2nd Edition (2015) similarly evolved ambitious but sometimes unwieldy first edition into more coherent experience. This systematic updating of Chronicles of Darkness line demonstrated Onyx Path's commitment to quality revision rather than rushed cash-grabs.

The Chronicles of Darkness core rulebook (2015, originally titled "The God-Machine Chronicle") rebranded and consolidated New World of Darkness setting. The God-Machine mythology—mysterious cosmic mechanism manipulating reality through infrastructure—provided overarching mystery connecting disparate supernatural types. This central mythology distinguished Chronicles from classic World of Darkness while providing compelling framework.

Simultaneously, Onyx Path negotiated rights to continue classic World of Darkness properties. The 20th Anniversary Editions of Vampire: The Masquerade, Werewolf: The Apocalypse, and other classic games became major projects. These weren't mere reprints but comprehensive compilations incorporating material from entire game line histories, cleaned up and reorganized into definitive editions.

Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary Edition (V20) became publishing phenomenon. The massive book compiled rules, clans, disciplines, and content from twenty years of supplements into single comprehensive volume. The deluxe edition's premium production—high-quality binding, ribbon bookmarks, full-color throughout—created beautiful artifact justifying high price point. The Kickstarter raised over $300,000, demonstrating passionate fan support.

The 20th Anniversary approach applied to other classic games: Werewolf: The Apocalypse 20th Anniversary, Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary, Wraith: The Oblivion 20th Anniversary, and Changeling: The Dreaming 20th Anniversary. Each became major project receiving enthusiastic community support. These editions ensured classic games remained available and introduced new players to historically important properties.

Exalted 3rd Edition (2016) represented Onyx Path's most ambitious project. Exalted—White Wolf's high-powered anime-influenced fantasy game—commanded devoted fanbase but suffered from mechanical issues in previous editions. Third edition development took years, involving complete mechanical overhaul and extensive playtesting. The complexity nearly overwhelmed project, with Kickstarter fulfillment significantly delayed. However, final product demonstrated commitment to quality over rushed completion, eventually delivering mechanically sophisticated and beautifully produced game.

The Exalted 3rd Edition saga taught valuable lessons about project scope management and realistic timeline estimation. Subsequent projects benefited from more conservative planning, though Onyx Path's commitment to quality over speed remained constant.

Trinity Continuum (2018) represented significant original IP development. While Trinity Universe existed under White Wolf, Onyx Path's version substantially reimagined the setting, updating 90s-era assumptions and refining mechanics. The success demonstrated Onyx Path's capability beyond merely continuing licensed properties.

Scion 2nd Edition (2019) similarly updated White Wolf property with substantial mechanical and conceptual improvements. The modern-mythology game gained refined Storypath system mechanics and deeper engagement with diverse cultural mythologies beyond primarily Western focus of first edition.

Throughout its history, Onyx Path expanded beyond World of Darkness properties. The company published Pugmire (anthropomorphic dogs in fantasy post-apocalypse), Cavaliers of Mars (Barsoomian planetary romance), They Came from Beneath the Sea! (1950s B-movie monster horror), and other original properties. These games demonstrated creative range while diversifying beyond licensed dependency.

The relationship with Paradox Interactive evolved over time. When Paradox acquired White Wolf IP, they initially brought tabletop publishing partially in-house before licensing it back to Onyx Path and other publishers. The 5th Edition World of Darkness games (Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition, Werewolf: The Apocalypse 5th Edition) went to other publishers initially, though Onyx Path eventually contributed supplements. This complex licensing landscape required careful navigation.

Onyx Path's community engagement distinguished it from traditional publishers. Regular Monday Meeting blog posts shared development updates, business insights, and behind-scenes information. This transparency built trust and investment. The company treated community as partners rather than mere customers, incorporating feedback and maintaining dialogue.

The "advance PDF" approach offered Kickstarter backers preliminary PDFs during development, allowing community review and feedback before final printing. This crowdsourced editing improved quality while making backers feel invested in development process.

Creator-owned projects became important Onyx Path initiative. The Creators Studio program supported designers developing original games through Onyx Path's infrastructure—crowdfunding expertise, production capabilities, distribution relationships—while maintaining IP ownership. This approach helped new creators while diversifying Onyx Path's catalog.

Today, Onyx Path operates as established publisher with diverse catalog spanning classic World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness, original properties, and creator-owned games. The company survived complex licensing landscapes, corporate IP ownership changes, and crowdfunding challenges to become stable entity preserving gaming heritage while innovating forward.

## Business Model and Philosophy

Onyx Path developed distinctive business model combining licensed stewardship with original development:

**Crowdfunding-First Approach**: Kickstarter campaigns fund virtually all major projects. This model eliminates inventory risk, provides upfront capital, validates market interest, and builds community engagement. Successful campaign delivery record maintains backer trust essential for continued success. The model requires careful project scoping and realistic timeline estimation, lessons learned through occasional fulfillment challenges.

**Licensed Properties Management**: Operating under license to develop others' IP presents unique challenges. Onyx Path must satisfy IP holders' requirements, respect canon and fan expectations, and operate within contractual constraints while maintaining creative vision and financial viability. This balancing act requires diplomatic skill and careful negotiation.

**Premium Production Values**: Onyx Path emphasizes quality production—full-color printing, premium binding, ribbon bookmarks, high-quality art, sophisticated layout design. These production choices justify premium pricing while creating desirable physical objects. The company recognizes that in digital age, physical books must offer tactile and visual appeal beyond mere content.

**Comprehensive Supplementation**: Rather than minimal support, Onyx Path develops extensive supplement lines for successful games. Chronicles of Darkness games receive sourcebooks, setting guides, antagonist books, and player options. This comprehensive approach serves dedicated fans while generating sustained revenue beyond core book sales.

**20th Anniversary Compilation Strategy**: The anniversary editions compile entire game line histories into definitive single volumes. This approach serves multiple purposes: introduces new players without requiring decades of out-of-print supplements, provides veterans comprehensive references, and creates premium products justifying high prices.

**Advance PDF Distribution**: Providing preliminary PDFs to backers during development crowdsources editing and feedback while maintaining engagement during fulfillment. This transparency builds trust and improves final product quality.

**Community Transparency**: Regular Monday Meeting blog posts, social media engagement, and open communication about development challenges, business realities, and creative decisions build community trust. This transparency distinguishes Onyx Path from secretive competitors.

**Creator Support Programs**: Creators Studio helps independent designers develop games through Onyx Path infrastructure while retaining IP ownership. This approach discovers new talent, diversifies catalog, and supports gaming community broadly.

**Diverse Portfolio Strategy**: Balancing licensed properties with original IP reduces dependency on single relationship or property. World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness, Trinity, Scion, and original games create revenue diversification.

**Digital Distribution**: Strong PDF sales through DriveThruRPG supplement print revenue. PDF-only releases for smaller supplements reduce production costs while serving completist fans.

**Storypath System Development**: Creating proprietary system (Storypath) usable across properties provides mechanical consistency and development efficiency. Trinity Continuum and Scion use Storypath, with potential for additional applications.

**Respectful Canon Continuation**: When continuing existing properties, Onyx Path respects established canon, fan expectations, and original visions while incorporating modern design insights. This balance preserves what fans love while improving mechanical and conceptual elements.

**Long-Term Perspective**: The company prioritizes sustainable operations over rapid growth. Conservative planning, realistic timelines, and quality focus create stability despite occasional project challenges.

This multifaceted approach creates viable business model for licensed publisher while supporting creator community and maintaining quality standards.

## Major Releases

### Chronicles of Darkness Core Rulebook (2015)

Chronicles of Darkness (originally God-Machine Chronicle, updating New World of Darkness) represented Onyx Path's vision for modern horror gaming. The game consolidated and refined first edition while introducing compelling central mythology distinguishing it from classic World of Darkness.

The God-Machine mythology provided overarching mystery. This mysterious cosmic mechanism manipulated reality through Infrastructure—mundane objects and locations hiding supernatural function. Angels (inhuman agents), Cover Identities (false lives), and Occult Matrices (reality-warping programs) created surveillance-state horror metaphor. The God-Machine's purposes remained mysterious—was it evil, alien, malfunctioning, or incomprehensible?

This central mystery connected disparate supernatural character types. Vampires, werewolves, mages, and others existed in world where reality itself was compromised. The setting supported cosmic horror and conspiracy themes while maintaining personal horror focus.

Mechanically, the game refined Storyteller System developed over decades. The core resolution—trait + skill dice pool against difficulty—remained familiar, but streamlined subsystems, clarified ambiguous rules, and balanced problematic elements. Conditions system replaced various tracking mechanics with unified approach. Beats and Experiences modified advancement, encouraging story-appropriate character development.

The Morality system evolved beyond simple good-evil tracking. Different templates used different moral frameworks reflecting philosophical perspectives: Humanity for mortals, Integrity for general use. Breaking points occurred when actions violated moral framework, potentially degrading moral trait and transforming character.

Tilts represented temporary environmental or personal conditions affecting scenes—Blizzard, Earthquake, Arm Wrack, Knocked Down. This standardized approach simplified previously ad-hoc modifier systems.

Social maneuvering rules provided structure for social conflict beyond simple dice rolls. Doors represented resistance to influence, overcome through roleplay, investigation, and mechanical actions. This system made social encounters mechanically interesting without reducing them to mere skill checks.

The game supported multiple supernatural types through modular approach. Mortals remained viable protagonists, with rules supporting occult investigators, psychics, and extraordinary ordinary people. This flexibility allowed diverse campaign styles.

Extensive advice for Storytellers covered horror techniques, pacing, mystery design, and maintaining atmosphere. The game recognized that mechanics alone don't create horror—presentation, pacing, and psychological techniques matter equally.

The deluxe edition featured premium production: full-color throughout, beautiful interior art, ribbon bookmark, sturdy binding. The physical object justified collectible status while serving functional reference purpose.

Chronicles of Darkness established foundation for second edition game line. Vampire, Werewolf, Mage, Changeling, and other games built on this core, creating mechanically consistent but thematically distinct experiences.

### Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary Edition (2011)

V20 (though predating Onyx Path's formal founding, it was shepherded by Rich Thomas) became template for anniversary edition approach. The massive book compiled twenty years of Vampire: The Masquerade content into comprehensive single volume.

The game included all thirteen clans from various editions, numerous bloodlines, comprehensive discipline lists, detailed setting information, and rules for various character types. Content previously scattered across dozens of supplements became accessible in one reference.

The production quality set new standards. Premium binding, full-color art throughout, ribbon bookmarks, thick quality paper, and beautiful cover created luxury product. The deluxe Kickstarter edition included leather binding, gilt edges, and premium presentation making it collectible artifact.

Mechanically, V20 synthesized rules from various editions, choosing best implementations and reconciling contradictions. The result wasn't mere reprinting but carefully curated compilation. Revised edition mechanics provided foundation, but elements from other versions appeared where superior.

The setting material balanced various metaplot elements without mandating specific timeline. Storytellers could run games in various periods—Dark Ages, Victorian Age, Modern Nights, or even post-Gehenna. This flexibility served diverse play preferences.

Character creation offered tremendous variety. Beyond thirteen clans, numerous bloodlines provided options. Disciplines, backgrounds, merits, and flaws allowed extensive customization. The sheer options supported decades of play without exhausting possibilities.

The Camarilla, Sabbat, Anarchs, and independent clans received coverage, allowing diverse campaign styles. Political intrigue, religious fanaticism, revolutionary struggle, or independent survival provided different play experiences.

V20 spawned extensive supplement line: Dark Ages 20th Anniversary, Lore of the Clans, Lore of the Bloodlines, Rites of the Blood, and numerous others. These supplements supported comprehensive world exploration.

The success validated market for classic World of Darkness continuation. Fans wanted these games available and supported, willing to pay premium prices for quality presentation. This proved essential for Onyx Path's business model.

V20 introduced new players to Vampire: The Masquerade while serving veterans seeking comprehensive reference. The game's accessibility despite massive size reflected careful organization and presentation.

### Exalted 3rd Edition (2016)

Exalted 3rd Edition represented Onyx Path's most ambitious and troubled project. The anime-influenced high-powered fantasy game commanded devoted fanbase but suffered mechanical issues in previous editions—especially 2nd Edition's combat system, which could extend single fights across entire sessions.

Third edition completely rebuilt mechanics from ground up. The Essence system created unified framework for supernatural abilities. Charms (special powers) used consistent structure while maintaining thematic distinction between Exalt types. The new combat system emphasized cinematic action while resolving faster than predecessors.

The Solar Exalted received complete treatment in core book—twenty-five Abilities with extensive Charm trees each, providing tremendous character customization. Charms emphasized thematic coherence—Solar excellence, perfection, and heroism expressed through mechanical effects.

The Craft system became particularly complex and controversial. Attempting to make crafting epic-scale artifacts mechanically interesting, the system introduced Projects, Objectives, and resource management. While ambitious, complexity sometimes overwhelmed play. This demonstrated challenges of mechanically supporting diverse gameplay elements in unified framework.

Social influence system provided structure for social conflict, building on Chronicles of Darkness approaches. Intimacies (beliefs, relationships, emotions) provided mechanical framework for persuasion and manipulation. This made social scenes mechanically interesting without reducing them to simple opposed rolls.

The setting returned to First Edition roots, discarding much controversial 2nd Edition metaplot. Creation became land of possibility rather than dying world. The Scarlet Empress's disappearance created power vacuum driving conflicts, but player actions determined outcomes rather than predetermined metaplot.

Development took far longer than initially estimated. The Kickstarter launched in 2013, promising 2014 delivery. Final product arrived in 2016, with continued delays on supplementary materials. This taught lessons about conservative timeline estimation and project scope management.

Despite fulfillment challenges, final product demonstrated quality commitment. The massive book featured beautiful art, comprehensive content, and mechanically sophisticated systems. Fans generally agreed the wait produced worthy result, even as they criticized delays.

Subsequent supplements (Dragon-Blooded: What Fire Has Wrought, Lunars: Fangs at the Gate) continued development, each representing major project. The game's complexity demands extensive development time, balancing ambition with realistic scheduling.

Exalted 3rd Edition showed both Onyx Path's ambitions and dangers of scope creep. The game represents remarkable achievement—mechanically complex, thematically rich, beautifully produced. But development challenges highlighted importance of realistic planning.

### Trinity Continuum (2018)

Trinity Continuum reimagined White Wolf's Trinity Universe with contemporary sensibilities. The original 1990s science fiction setting received substantial updating, incorporating modern technological and social awareness while maintaining core concepts.

The Continuum spans multiple eras and power levels. Trinity Continuum: Aeon (far future psionic space opera), Trinity Continuum: Aberrant (near-future superhuman emergence), and Trinity Continuum: Adventure! (1920s pulp action) each provide distinct play experiences while sharing mechanical and setting DNA.

The Storypath System provided unified mechanics across Continuum. The system uses trait + skill + d10 pools, counting successes. Enhancements modify dice or add automatic successes. Paths represent character backgrounds and capabilities. The system emphasizes competence—characters are capable, with failure indicating challenging situations rather than incompetence.

The scaling approach lets system handle normal humans through god-like beings. Trinity Continuum core book focuses on capable but non-superhuman characters—special operatives, investigators, troubleshooters. Aeon adds psionic powers. Aberrant introduces quantum manipulation. The mechanical framework scales appropriately for each power level.

Character Paths define backgrounds and capabilities. Origin Path (childhood/background), Role Path (profession/expertise), and Society Path (organizational affiliation) combine to create competent, well-rounded characters with clear narrative hooks. This approach ensures mechanical effectiveness while building story potential.

The setting embraced diversity and contemporary awareness. Characters from varied backgrounds, LGBTQ+ representation, and international perspectives reflected modern sensibilities. This updating distinguished Continuum from 90s-era Trinity.

The metaplot provided overarching narrative without mandating specific outcomes. Mysterious enemies, factional conflicts, and cosmic mysteries create framework, but player actions determine resolutions. This balance between structure and agency serves various play styles.

The production quality matched Onyx Path standards: full-color throughout, beautiful art, professional layout. The physical books served both functional and collectible purposes.

Trinity Continuum demonstrated Onyx Path's capability developing substantial original IP beyond licensed properties. The game showed the company could create compelling settings and mechanics, not merely continue existing lines.

## Impact on Industry

Onyx Path influenced contemporary gaming significantly:

**Legacy Preservation**: The company preserved and continued beloved game lines that might otherwise have disappeared. World of Darkness and Chronicles of Darkness remained available and supported, introducing new generations to historically significant properties.

**Crowdfunding Validation**: Consistent successful campaigns demonstrated crowdfunding viability for established publishers beyond just startups. Onyx Path's delivery record built industry credibility for Kickstarter publishing.

**Licensed Publisher Model**: The company proved that licensed publishers could operate successfully, creating model for continuing properties under IP holder oversight. This influenced how other companies approached licensed continuation.

**Anniversary Edition Approach**: The comprehensive compilation strategy influenced how other publishers treated classic games. Definitive editions incorporating entire line histories became desirable alternatives to scattered reprints.

**Premium Production Standards**: Onyx Path's deluxe editions raised expectations for quality. Full-color, ribbon bookmarks, premium binding, and beautiful art became aspirational standards.

**Community Transparency**: Regular communication, behind-scenes sharing, and honest discussion of challenges built new model for publisher-community relationships. This transparency influenced other publishers' community engagement.

**Creator Support Programs**: Creators Studio demonstrated how established publishers could support independent designers while benefiting from diversified catalogs.

**Chronicles Development**: The refined second edition Chronicles of Darkness games influenced modern horror gaming design, particularly narrative-focused supernatural RPGs.

**Storypath System**: Creating flexible generic system for diverse properties influenced universal system design, particularly for narrative-focused games.

**Realistic Timeline Learning**: Exalted 3rd Edition's development challenges provided cautionary example about scope management and timeline estimation, influencing project planning industry-wide.

## Current Status and Future

Onyx Path continues operating successfully as specialized publisher:

**Financial Health**: Consistent Kickstarter success, diverse product portfolio, and sustainable operations indicate stable financial position. The company operates conservatively, avoiding overextension.

**Active Product Lines**: Ongoing development across multiple properties—Chronicles of Darkness games, classic World of Darkness support, Trinity Continuum expansion, original properties, and creator-owned projects.

**Licensing Relationships**: Complex but functional relationships with Paradox Interactive (World of Darkness IP) and other rights holders. These relationships evolve as IP holders' strategies change.

**Community Standing**: Strong community loyalty built through transparency, quality products, and respectful continuation of beloved properties. Fan investment ensures continued support.

**Digital Presence**: Robust PDF sales, active social media, regular blog updates, and online community engagement maintain visibility between print releases.

**Leadership Continuity**: Rich Thomas continues steering company with consistent vision across history. Stability in leadership provides continuity.

**Challenges**: Managing multiple licensed relationships, balancing licensed properties with original development, maintaining realistic project scopes and timelines, and adapting to changing IP holder priorities.

**Future Prospects**: Strong position for continued operation through established crowdfunding credibility, loyal community, diverse portfolio, and proven ability to deliver quality products. Challenges include navigating evolving licensing landscape and managing scope for ambitious projects.
