# Adept Press

**Founded**: 1996
**Headquarters**: United States
**Founder**: [[Ron Edwards]]

## Overview

Adept Press, founded 1996 by Ron Edwards, published Sorcerer (2001—demon-binding with Humanity tracking proving narrativist design worked), Sorcerer supplements expanding framework, and ongoing theoretical/design work, becoming foundational narrativist publisher demonstrating indie designer-direct sales viability through PDF distribution and print-on-demand, influencing indie publishing models while establishing Edwards as major theorist (GNS Theory, The Forge founder) and designer, showing single-person operations could achieve critical influence despite limited commercial scale.

**Sorcerer**: Flagship demonstrating narrativist theory in practice

**Supplements**: Sorcerer & Sword, Sex & Sorcery, The Sorcerer's Soul—thematic variations

**Publishing Model**: Direct PDF sales, small print runs, maintaining control

**Theoretical Foundation**: Publishing platform for Edwards's design theory

**The Forge Connection**: Edwards founded The Forge (2001-2012), Adept Press published his designs

**Influence**: Design influence far exceeded sales—studied by designers, foundational for indie movement

## Publishing Philosophy

**Designer Control**: Edwards maintains creative authority

**Direct Sales**: Bypassing traditional distribution

**PDF Primary**: Digital-first before mainstream

**Theory Integration**: Games as theory demonstrations

**Small Scale Sustainability**: Proving solo operations viable

**Ongoing Updates**: Continuous refinement of published games

## Cultural Impact

**Narrativist Publishing**: Template for theory-driven design publication

**Indie Model**: Direct-to-consumer before it was standard

**GNS Theory**: Publishing platform for influential theory work

**The Forge**: Connected to foundational indie community

**Sorcerer's Legacy**: Game studied far beyond played

**Design Education**: Adept Press games as teaching tools

## Legacy

Demonstrated solo designer-publisher viability. Sorcerer influenced generation of narrativist designers. Publishing model template for indie operations. Continues as active small operation.
