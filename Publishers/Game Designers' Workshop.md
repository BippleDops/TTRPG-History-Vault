## Overview

Game Designers' Workshop (GDW) was organised on 22 June 1973 in Normal, Illinois by Frank Chadwick, Rich Banner, [[Marc Miller]] and Loren Wiseman, members of the Illinois State University wargames club. Founded to publish the *Europa* series of World War II board wargames, it expanded into roleplaying games with *En Garde!* (1975) and [[Traveller (1977)]], designed by Marc Miller, and later *Twilight: 2000* (1984), *2300 AD* (1986), *Space: 1889* (1988) and the *Traveller* revisions *MegaTraveller* (1987) and *Traveller: The New Era* (1993). GDW closed on 29 February 1996 after financial difficulties; Marc Miller's Far Future Enterprises subsequently acquired the rights to its roleplaying lines.
