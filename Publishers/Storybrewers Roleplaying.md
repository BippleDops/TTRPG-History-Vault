# Storybrewers Roleplaying

**Founded**: 2016
**Headquarters**: Australia
**Founders**: Hayley Gordon, Vee Hendro

## Overview

Storybrewers Roleplaying, founded 2016 by Hayley Gordon and Vee Hendro, achieved success with Good Society (2018—Jane Austen regency romance with letter-writing and Resolve mechanics), demonstrating niche genres like regency romance viable for gaming through faithful adaptation, thoughtful mechanics, and beautiful production, establishing Australian indie presence while showing how literary genres could successfully translate to tabletop through appropriate mechanical frameworks and production values.

**Good Society**: Flagship Jane Austen-inspired regency romance

**Regency Focus**: Specializing in period social drama

**Letter Writing**: Actual between-session correspondence integrated

**Resolve Mechanics**: Tension-building token system

**Australian**: International indie presence from Australia

**Beautiful Production**: Matching regency aesthetics
