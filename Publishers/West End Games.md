## Overview

West End Games (WEG) was founded in New York City in 1974 by Daniel Scott Palter as a publisher of historical board wargames. Its first roleplaying game, [[Paranoia (1984)]], won an Origins Award, and *Ghostbusters* (1986) introduced the six-sided-dice mechanics that became the D6 System. The licensed [[Star Wars RPG (West End Games) (1987)|Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]] (1987) made WEG one of the leading RPG publishers of the late 1980s and early 1990s; *Torg* (1990) and the *Masterbook* system (1994) followed. The company collapsed into bankruptcy in July 1998 after the failure of its parent company, Bucci Retail Group; Lucasfilm withdrew the Star Wars licence and the *Paranoia* rights reverted to its creators in 2000. The WEG assets later passed through Humanoids, Purgatory Publishing and Nocturnal Media.
