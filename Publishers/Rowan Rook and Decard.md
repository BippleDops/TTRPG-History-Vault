# Rowan, Rook and Decard

**Founded**: 2016
**Headquarters**: United Kingdom
**Founders**: [[Grant Howitt]], [[Chris Taylor]]

## Overview

Rowan, Rook and Decard (RRD), founded 2016 by Grant Howitt and Chris Taylor, achieved major UK indie success through Spire: The City Must Fall (2018—dark elf revolution), Heart: The City Beneath (2020—weird horror dungeon), Honey Heist (2017—viral one-page bears-rob-HoneyCon), and hundreds of one-page RPGs, demonstrating single publisher could excel at both elaborate core games with beautiful production AND brilliant ultra-minimal designs, establishing Resistance System (stress/fallout engine), raising UK indie gaming's international profile, and showing prolific output could maintain quality.

**Dual Focus**: Elaborate hardcovers (Spire/Heart) AND one-page brilliance (Honey Heist, hundreds more)

**Resistance System**: Stress across five tracks, fallout consequences, d10 pools

**UK Weird Fantasy**: Distinctively British grotesque dark humorous aesthetic

**Beautiful Production**: Gorgeous books with professional art/layout

**Prolific**: Constant new releases, especially one-pagers

**Commercial Success**: Strong Kickstarters, retail presence, sustainable operations

Bears robbing HoneyCon one-page game:

- Prolific output
- Consistent quality
- Free and paid
- Demonstrated range
