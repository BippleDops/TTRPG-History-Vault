# Magpie Games

**Founded**: 2012
**Headquarters**: New Jersey, USA
**Founders**: [[Mark Diaz Truman]], Marissa Kelly

## Overview

Magpie Games, founded 2012 by Mark Diaz Truman and Marissa Kelly, became major PbtA publisher through Masks: A New Generation (2016—ENnie Gold teen superheroes), Urban Shadows (2015—political urban fantasy with Debt), Root RPG (2020—licensed adaptation), and Cartel (2018—narco-thriller), demonstrating indie publisher could achieve professional production standards, sustainable business operations, and commercial success while maintaining designer-friendly practices, establishing template for professional indie publishing combining design innovation with business excellence.

**Signature**: PbtA specialization with political focus

**Production Quality**: Professional standards matching/exceeding mainstream

**Commercial Success**: Multiple best-sellers (Masks especially)

**Designer Support**: Fair contracts, development support, professional editing

**Licensed Success**: Root and Avatar adaptations showing licensing capability

## Major Releases

### Masks: A New Generation (2016)

Teen superhero PbtA with Labels instead of stats:
- ENnie Gold Best Game 2017
- Best-selling Magpie title
- Multiple supplements
- Ongoing second edition development
- Mainstream breakthrough

### Urban Shadows (2015, 2019 2E)

Political urban fantasy with Debt mechanics:
- Established Magpie's reputation
- ENnie Awards
- Second Edition refined design
- Commercial and critical success

### Root: The Tabletop Roleplaying Game (2020)

Licensed board game adaptation:
- Successful Kickstarter
- Demonstrated licensing capability
- PbtA woodland animal warfare
- Critical acclaim

### Cartel (2018)

Narco-thriller examining drug war:
- Controversial but thoughtful
- Political gaming
- Safety tools prominent
- Critical recognition

### Avatar Legends (2022)

Licensed Avatar: The Last Airbender PbtA:
- Massive Kickstarter ($10M+)
- Record-breaking campaign
- Mainstream licensed success

### Other Releases

- Pasión de las Pasiones (telenovela PbtA)
- Epyllion (dragon kids)
- Zombie World (GMless zombie survival)
- Consistent quality catalog
