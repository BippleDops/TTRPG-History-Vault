# Mongoose Publishing

**Founded**: 2001
**Active Era**: d20 Era through Modern Era (2001-Present)
**Historical Significance**: ⭐ 4/5
**Headquarters**: Swindon, England
**Status**: Active

## Company History

Mongoose Publishing emerged during the d20 Revolution in 2001, founded by Matthew Sprange in England. The company rode the Open Gaming License wave while carving distinctive niche through reviving classic game properties, extensive d20 supplements, and innovative licensing approaches. Mongoose transformed from opportunistic d20 supplement publisher into steward of gaming's classic properties, particularly becoming synonymous with modern Traveller editions. The company's journey demonstrates how adaptability, license acquisition, and community engagement can build sustainable independent publishing business.

Matthew Sprange founded Mongoose Publishing with clear vision: leverage the d20 System's popularity to build catalog quickly, then diversify into original systems and licensed properties. This pragmatic approach characterized Mongoose's operations—recognize market opportunities, move quickly, adapt as conditions changed, and maintain sustainable operations over explosive growth.

The company's name suggested nimble, aggressive approach—mongooses famously attack creatures much larger than themselves. This metaphor proved apt as small British publisher competed against industry giants through strategic positioning rather than direct confrontation.

Mongoose's early years focused intensively on d20 supplements. The company released numerous products exploring different aspects of d20 System: Slayer's Guides, Quintessential class books, Encyclopedia Arcane magic supplements, and many others. This prolific output built catalog, established market presence, and generated revenue funding future expansion.

Unlike some d20 publishers producing mediocre products quickly, Mongoose maintained quality standards. Books featured solid writing, useful content, and professional production. This quality focus differentiated Mongoose in crowded d20 marketplace, building reputation that sustained company when d20 boom cooled.

The company's strategic shift came through acquiring licenses for classic game properties. Paranoia (2004) represented Mongoose's first major license, reviving the darkly humorous RPG about troubleshooters serving insane Computer in Alpha Complex. Mongoose's edition updated mechanics while maintaining the game's satirical edge, introducing new generation to Paranoia's unique gameplay.

RuneQuest (2006) brought Mongoose deeper into classic game revival. The company acquired license for Chaosium's legendary fantasy RPG, creating new edition using updated mechanics. While Mongoose RuneQuest diverged from Chaosium's approach in some ways, it introduced many to RuneQuest's percentile-based, skill-focused gameplay.

Traveller represented Mongoose's most significant license acquisition. In 2008, Mongoose released new Traveller edition that would become the game's definitive modern version. The company's Traveller maintained the classic science fiction game's core identity while streamlining mechanics and supporting extensive supplement line.

Mongoose Traveller achieved remarkable success, becoming the standard Traveller edition for over a decade. The company released hundreds of supplements exploring the Third Imperium setting, creating new settings, and supporting diverse science fiction campaigns. This extensive support demonstrated Mongoose's commitment to long-term product lines rather than quick cash grabs.

The Traveller license proved so successful that when original edition's sales cooled, Mongoose released Traveller Second Edition (2016), refining mechanics based on years of feedback. This second edition continued strong sales, cementing Mongoose's position as Traveller's modern publisher.

Throughout 2010s, Mongoose balanced classic property revival with original development. The company acquired licenses for Judge Dredd, Conan, and other properties while developing original games. This diversification reduced dependence on any single license while serving diverse markets.

Conan: Adventures in an Age Undreamed Of (2017, later rebranded as Robert E. Howard's Conan) exemplified Mongoose's approach to licensed properties. The game used 2d20 System (licensed from Modiphius Entertainment), demonstrating Mongoose's pragmatism about using appropriate systems rather than forcing properties into house mechanics.

Mongoose's relationship with Marc Miller, Traveller's creator, proved particularly significant. Miller's trust in Mongoose to steward his creation enabled extensive Traveller development. This collaborative relationship demonstrated how good license management could benefit both property owners and publishers.

The company also experimented with innovative publishing approaches. Mongoose used crowdfunding selectively, published through multiple formats (hardcover, softcover, PDF), and maintained direct sales alongside traditional distribution. This flexibility enabled adaptation to changing market conditions.

Judge Dredd & The Worlds of 2000 AD (2018) brought British comics' most famous character to tabletop, using Traveller-derived mechanics. The game demonstrated Mongoose's ability to adapt systems to different settings while maintaining mechanical coherence.

Throughout its history, Mongoose maintained steady operations without dramatic expansion or contraction. The company avoided overextension, maintained sustainable release schedules, and built long-term customer relationships. This conservative approach enabled survival through multiple industry disruptions.

Today, Mongoose Publishing continues as independent British publisher with strong catalog of licensed properties and original games. The company maintains particularly close association with Traveller, having become the game's definitive modern publisher. Mongoose exemplifies sustainable independent publishing through license stewardship, quality focus, and market adaptability.

## Publishing Philosophy

Mongoose Publishing's philosophy centers on several principles:

**Classic Property Stewardship**: Mongoose specializes in reviving and maintaining classic game properties. The company treats licenses respectfully, updating mechanics for modern audiences while maintaining core identity. This stewardship approach builds trust with property owners and existing fans.

**Long-Term Support**: When Mongoose commits to property, they support it extensively. Traveller's hundreds of supplements exemplify this approach. Long-term support builds customer loyalty and creates sustainable revenue through supplement sales.

**Mechanical Pragmatism**: Mongoose demonstrates flexibility about game systems. The company uses d20, percentile, 2d20, and other systems based on appropriateness rather than forcing house system. This pragmatism serves games better than system dogmatism.

**Quality Over Flash**: Mongoose prioritizes solid content over premium components. Books feature good writing, useful information, and professional presentation without excessive component costs. This approach maintains accessible pricing while delivering value.

**Market Responsiveness**: The company adapts quickly to market changes. Mongoose rode d20 boom, pivoted to classic properties as d20 cooled, and embraced digital distribution early. This responsiveness enables survival through market shifts.

**Community Engagement**: Mongoose maintains active community interaction through forums, social media, and convention presence. The company incorporates feedback and supports fan communities around properties.

**Sustainable Operations**: Mongoose avoids overextension, maintaining operations sustainable long-term rather than pursuing explosive growth. This conservative approach enabled continuous operation across two decades.

**Licensing Strategy**: The company targets classic properties with devoted but underserved fanbases rather than chasing mass-market licenses. This niche approach reduces competition while serving passionate audiences.

**Format Flexibility**: Mongoose publishes in multiple formats (hardcover, softcover, PDF) serving different customer preferences and price points. This flexibility maximizes market reach.

## Major Releases

### Traveller (2008) and Second Edition (2016)

Mongoose Traveller became the game's definitive modern edition. The company maintained Traveller's core identity—hard science fiction, dangerous character creation, detailed universe-building, emphasis on trading and exploration—while streamlining mechanics and improving accessibility.

The 2d6 + modifiers core mechanic remained familiar to Traveller veterans. Character creation's life path system risked death (though Mongoose made survival easier than classic Traveller's infamous mortality). Skills remained central to character definition rather than classes or levels.

The Third Imperium setting received extensive support through sector books, alien supplements, ship designs, and adventures. Mongoose also supported alternate settings like Pirates of Drinax campaign and various science fiction variants.

Second Edition (2016) refined mechanics based on eight years of feedback. Changes streamlined combat, clarified skills, and improved character creation while maintaining compatibility with first edition supplements. This evolution demonstrated Mongoose's commitment to continuous improvement.

The extensive supplement line covered starships, vehicles, weapons, aliens, robots, central supply catalog, worlds, and countless other topics. This comprehensive support made Mongoose Traveller the most extensively documented edition.

Traveller's success established Mongoose as the game's modern publisher, association continuing over fifteen years. This longevity demonstrated successful license stewardship benefiting property owner, publisher, and fans.

### Paranoia (2004)

Mongoose's Paranoia revival updated the darkly humorous RPG about troubleshooters serving Alpha Complex's insane Computer. The game's unique premise—players controlled disposable clones in totalitarian dystopia run by malfunctioning AI—created gameplay unlike anything else.

Paranoia's humor came from inherent contradictions: troubleshooters tasked with maintaining order while secretly belonging to treasonous secret societies, following Computer's contradictory orders, and backstabbing teammates while supposedly cooperating. The game satirized bureaucracy, authoritarianism, and paranoia itself.

Mongoose's edition updated mechanics while maintaining tone. The company released multiple editions and variants over the years, experimenting with different mechanical approaches and tones (from lighthearted to darker).

While never achieving mainstream success, Paranoia maintained cult following. Mongoose's support kept the property available and introduced new audiences to Alpha Complex's twisted humor.

### RuneQuest (2006)

Mongoose RuneQuest brought Chaosium's classic fantasy RPG to new audience. The game maintained percentile-based resolution, skill-focused character development, and deadly combat distinguishing RuneQuest from level-based systems.

The Legend variant (later released when license ended) created Mongoose's own fantasy percentile system, demonstrating how licensed properties could inspire original development.

While Mongoose RuneQuest eventually ended when rights returned to Chaosium, the edition introduced many to percentile-based fantasy gaming and demonstrated Mongoose's ability to handle classic properties.

### Conan RPG (2017)

Robert E. Howard's Conan: Adventures in an Age Undreamed Of (later shortened) brought sword-and-sorcery icon to tabletop using 2d20 System. The game emphasized pulp action, dangerous sorcery, and Howardian adventure.

The 2d20 System (developed by Modiphius) provided momentum mechanics, talent-based character development, and cinematic action resolution. Mongoose's decision to license appropriate system rather than force Conan into existing Mongoose mechanics demonstrated pragmatism.

Extensive sourcebooks explored Conan's world, providing campaign frameworks, locations, and adventures. The game achieved success among Conan fans and sword-and-sorcery enthusiasts.

### Judge Dredd & The Worlds of 2000 AD (2018)

This game brought British comics' most famous character to tabletop using Traveller-derived mechanics. Players could control Judge Dredd and other Judges enforcing law in Mega-City One, or explore other 2000 AD properties.

The game demonstrated Mongoose's ability to adapt Traveller mechanics to different settings. The system supported street-level law enforcement, investigation, and action fitting Judge Dredd's tone.

## Cultural Impact

Mongoose Publishing influenced gaming through several channels:

**Classic Property Preservation**: Mongoose kept classic games available and relevant through modern editions. This preservation work maintained gaming history's accessibility.

**Traveller Stewardship**: Becoming definitive modern Traveller publisher introduced new generations to classic science fiction gaming while maintaining devoted community.

**d20 Era Participation**: Mongoose's extensive d20 supplements contributed to system's diversity and demonstrated British participation in primarily American-dominated boom.

**British Gaming Presence**: As prominent British independent publisher, Mongoose helped maintain British gaming industry's international presence.

**License Management Model**: Mongoose demonstrated how to successfully acquire and maintain classic game licenses, influencing how other publishers approached property acquisition.

**Sustainable Independence**: The company showed mid-tier publishers could survive long-term through conservative operations, quality focus, and market adaptation.

## Legacy

Mongoose Publishing's legacy centers on several achievements:

**Traveller Modernization**: Creating definitive modern Traveller edition kept classic game relevant and accessible. This stewardship benefited entire science fiction RPG community.

**Classic Revival**: Demonstrating that classic properties could succeed with modern audiences through respectful updating rather than complete reimagining.

**Sustainable Independence**: Proving mid-tier independent publishers could survive through conservative operations rather than requiring explosive growth or corporate backing.

**Quality d20 Support**: Contributing quality supplements during d20 era rather than exploiting OGL with mediocre products.

**License Stewardship**: Showing how to successfully manage licensed properties benefiting property owners, publishers, and fans simultaneously.

**British Publishing**: Maintaining British independent gaming industry presence competing internationally.

**Comprehensive Support**: Demonstrating value of extensive long-term product line support rather than abandoning games after core releases.

## Business Model and Distribution

Mongoose developed sustainable business model:

**Licensed Properties**: Focus on classic licenses provides built-in audiences while avoiding original IP development risks.

**Extensive Supplements**: Long supplement lines create recurring revenue beyond core rulebook sales.

**Multiple Formats**: Publishing in hardcover, softcover, and PDF serves different price points and customer preferences.

**Direct Sales**: Online store sales maintain higher margins than three-tier distribution while building customer relationships.

**Traditional Distribution**: Maintaining game store presence through distributors provides market reach.

**Conservative Operations**: Avoiding overextension and maintaining sustainable release schedules prevents financial strain.

**Digital Distribution**: Early PDF adoption captured digital markets with low overhead.

**Print-on-Demand**: Using POD for slower-selling titles reduces inventory costs while maintaining availability.

**Crowdfunding**: Selective Kickstarter use funds major projects while reducing risk.

**International Focus**: British base with international distribution expands beyond single market.

This diversified, conservative approach enabled continuous operation across multiple industry disruptions.
