# Half Meme Press

**Founded**: 2003
**Headquarters**: United States
**Founder**: [[Paul Czege]]

## Overview

Half Meme Press, founded 2003 by Paul Czege, published My Life with Master (2003—Diana Jones Award gothic minion tragedy), The Clay That Woke (2014—philosophical minotaur oracles), and Acts of Evil (2018—villain protagonists), specializing in psychologically sophisticated emotionally intense games exploring uncomfortable territory through mechanical constraints, demonstrating indie publishers could pursue ambitious difficult designs, maintain beautiful production values, and build devoted following even with limited commercial scale.

**My Life with Master**: Flagship—Diana Jones Award 2004

**Psychological Focus**: Games exploring difficult emotional/psychological territory

**Beautiful Production**: Especially Clay That Woke's lavish hardcover

**Ambitious Design**: Prioritizing depth over accessibility

**Niche Excellence**: Small dedicated audience for sophisticated design

## Publishing Philosophy

**Psychological Depth**: Games exploring complex emotional territory

**Beautiful Objects**: Books as art objects worthy of content

**Ambitious Design**: Not compromising for accessibility

**Small Operation**: Sustainable through devoted following

**Czege's Vision**: Personal publishing reflecting designer's interests

## Cultural Impact

**My Life with Master**: Canonical indie game, Diana Jones winner

**Psychological Gaming**: Demonstrated emotional depth viable

**Production Standards**: Beautiful books raised expectations

**Niche Success**: Proved specialized content could sustain operation

## Legacy

My Life with Master's foundational influence. Demonstrated beautiful production for indie. Showed psychological sophistication could be sustainable niche.
