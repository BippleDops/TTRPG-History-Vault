# TTRPG History Vault - Research Archive Index

This Research Archive contains 16 comprehensive resource documents covering the most important sources, movements, and developments in TTRPG history. Each document summarizes a major resource or topic, explains its historical value, and describes its utility for expanding the vault.

## Purpose of This Archive

These research documents serve multiple purposes:
1. **Source Documentation**: Identify and summarize major TTRPG history resources
2. **Context Building**: Provide cultural, industry, and design context for vault entries
3. **Research Guidance**: Direct where to find information on specific topics
4. **Gap Identification**: Reveal what areas need more documentation
5. **Educational Value**: Explain why resources matter historically

## How to Use This Archive

**When Creating Vault Entries:**
- Reference relevant research documents for context
- Link to related vault entries mentioned in research docs
- Use quotes and facts to enrich entries
- Apply analytical frameworks from research

**When Researching Topics:**
- Find which resource documents cover your topic
- Check "Related Vault Entries" sections for connections
- Use "Research Value" sections to understand importance
- Follow source URLs (when available) for deeper research

**For Understanding TTRPG History:**
- Read research docs to understand major movements
- See how different resources complement each other
- Recognize patterns across eras and topics
- Build comprehensive historical understanding

## Archive Organization

The archive is organized thematically rather than chronologically, covering:
- **Foundational Histories**: Definitive scholarly works
- **Industry Analysis**: Business and publishing history
- **Design Movements**: Creative and theoretical developments
- **Cultural Phenomena**: Community and social history
- **Platforms and Infrastructure**: How the hobby operates
- **Contemporary Developments**: Modern transformations

## Methodology Notes

**These research documents:**
- Summarize existing resources, not create new research
- Provide context and explain utility
- Link to related vault entries
- Identify key quotes and facts
- Evaluate research value and integration

**They are NOT:**
- Comprehensive on their topics (they point to comprehensive sources)
- Original research (they document existing resources)
- Neutral (they evaluate significance and relevance)
- Complete (ongoing documentation and updates needed)

**Best Practices:**
- Consult multiple resources for any topic
- Triangulate facts across sources
- Recognize bias and perspective
- Update as new resources emerge
- Connect to actual vault entries
