# Zak Smith Controversy (2019)

**Content Warning**: This entry discusses allegations of emotional, physical, and sexual abuse. Reader discretion advised.

**Date**: February 2019
**Key Figures**: Zak Smith (Zak Sabbath), Mandy Morbid, other survivors, industry responders
**Central Issue**: Multiple abuse allegations against prominent RPG creator and industry response
**Resolution Status**: Resolved - Smith de-platformed and removed from industry
**Historical Significance**: 4/5 - Established industry standards for handling abuse allegations

## Overview

In February 2019, the tabletop RPG community was rocked when multiple women, led by Zak Smith's ex-partner Mandy Morbid (Manda Haugland), publicly accused the prominent game designer and consultant of years of sustained emotional, physical, and sexual abuse. Smith, known professionally as Zak Sabbath and Zak S., had been a controversial but influential figure in the OSR (Old School Renaissance) and wider RPG community, author of award-winning products, and consultant on D&D Fifth Edition.

The allegations, posted on social media with detailed accounts from multiple survivors, led to immediate and widespread industry response. Within days, Wizards of the Coast removed Smith from consultant credits, major conventions banned him, DriveThruRPG/RPGNow delisted his products, online communities banned him, and industry professionals publicly condemned his actions and severed professional ties. Smith's fall from prominent creator to industry pariah happened with unprecedented speed.

The controversy transcended Smith as an individual, sparking broader discussions about abuse in gaming communities, the power dynamics between creators and fans, accountability without criminal conviction, the complexity of separating art from artist, the role of industry gatekeepers, and how communities should respond to allegations against prominent members. It demonstrated that the gaming industry, in the MeToo era, would act decisively against credibly accused abusers.

The Zak Smith controversy remains significant as a watershed moment for accountability in the gaming industry and a case study in community response to abuse allegations.

## Zak Smith's Response

### Denial and Counter-Accusations

**Initial Response**:
- Denied all allegations
- Claimed conspiracy against him
- Accused Mandy and others of lying
- Suggested mental health issues of accusers
- Demanded "evidence"
- Portrayed himself as victim

**Tactics**:
- Attempted to discredit survivors
- Invoked prior controversies as "proof" of conspiracy
- Rallied remaining supporters
- Threatened legal action
- Classic DARVO (Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender)

**Community Reception**:
- Most of community sided with survivors
- Pattern-matching with previous online behavior
- Few defenders remained vocal
- Legal threats largely dismissed
- Response itself seen as evidence of manipulative patterns

## Conclusion

The Zak Smith controversy represents a watershed moment for accountability in the tabletop RPG industry. The swift, decisive response to multiple credible allegations of abuse demonstrated that talent, awards, and influence would not protect abusers from consequences. The community's largely unified support for survivors and willingness to believe their accounts marked a cultural shift toward prioritizing safety and accountability over protecting prominent creators.

The controversy forced difficult conversations about power dynamics, community responsibility, complicity, and the relationship between creator and creation. While debates about separating art from artist continue, the industry established clear precedent: credibly accused abusers will be de-platformed, banned, and removed from industry spaces.

Mandy Morbid's courage in coming forward, and the courage of other survivors who corroborated and shared their experiences, catalyzed meaningful change. The gaming community learned to recognize warning signs, believe survivors, and act decisively to protect vulnerable members. While no response is perfect and debates continue about due process and evidence standards, the industry moved decisively toward survivor support and abuser accountability.

The Zak Smith case will be remembered as a moment when the RPG community chose safety, accountability, and survivors over preserving the career of a talented but abusive creator. It established standards and expectations that continue to shape how the industry responds to allegations and how communities protect their members.
