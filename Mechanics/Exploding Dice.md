# Exploding Dice

**First Appeared**: Early RPGs, prominently in [[Savage Worlds]] (2003) as "Aces"
**Category**: Dice Mechanic / Open-Ended Rolling

## Overview

Exploding Dice (called "Aces" in Savage Worlds) create open-ended resolution where rolling maximum value on die lets you reroll and add—rolling 6 on d6 means roll again adding results, potentially infinitely, enabling improbable heroic successes and catastrophic fumbles, adding excitement through unlimited potential while maintaining probability (explosions rare enough to be special), becoming signature Savage Worlds mechanic and appearing across various systems emphasizing pulp action and heroic gameplay.

**Mechanism**: Roll max on die → reroll → add to total → repeat if max again

**Example**: Roll d6, get 6 → roll again, get 5 → total 11 (impossible on normal d6)

**Unlimited**: Theoretically infinite but practically rare

**Excitement**: Every max roll creates anticipation

**Impact**: Savage Worlds signature. Creates memorable unlikely success stories.

## How It Works

**Roll Trait Die**: Roll your skill/attribute die

**Check for Maximum**: Did you roll highest possible number?
- d6: rolled 6?
- d8: rolled 8?
- d10: rolled 10?

**Explode**: If yes, keep that die's result and roll again

**Add Results**: Sum all rolls

**Repeat**: Keep exploding on maximum rolls

**Example Chain**:
- Roll d8: Get 8 (explode!)
- Roll d8: Get 7 (stop)
- Total: 15

**Comparison**:
- Normal d8: Maximum 8
- Exploding d8: Theoretically unlimited, practically 8-16 most common explosions

## Games Using Exploding Dice

**Savage Worlds**: "Aces"—signature mechanic creating pulp heroics

**Deadlands**: Poker-hand mechanics with exploding dice

**7th Sea (2nd Ed)**: Exploding dice for heroic swashbuckling

**Feng Shui**: Hong Kong action with exploding

**Earthdawn**: Exploding step dice

**Numerous Others**: Pulp/heroic games especially

## Design Philosophy

**Heroic Potential**: Anyone can succeed miraculously

**Excitement**: Max rolls create dramatic moments

**Probability Maintained**: Rare enough to feel special

**Open-Ended**: No hard caps on success

**Pulp Feel**: Matches over-the-top action genres

## Statistical Considerations

**Rarity**: Each explosion ~8-17% likely depending on die size

**Average Impact**: Minimal on average results, large on high rolls

**Dramatic Moments**: Creates memorable unlikely successes

**Downside**: Also enables enemy lucky shots

## Reception

**Praise**: Creates exciting dramatic moments, pulp feel, heroic potential

**Criticism**: Adds rolling time, can be swingy, math overhead, unrealistic (endless explosion chains)

## Legacy

Savage Worlds signature element. Appeared in various heroic/pulp games. Influenced probability mechanics broadly.
