# Popcorn Initiative

**First Appeared**: Feng Shui (1996), formalized in various games
**Category**: Initiative System / Turn Order

## Overview

Popcorn Initiative eliminates static turn order by having active player choose who goes next—after your turn, you "pass the popcorn" to any ally who hasn't acted yet, creating dynamic sequencing where tactical positioning determines order, players coordinate through selection rather than rigid rotation, and pacing flows naturally instead of mechanically, demonstrating how player-choice turn order could increase tactical depth while simplifying bookkeeping.

**Basic Structure**:
1. First player acts (determined by circumstances or random)
2. After acting, chooses which ally acts next
3. Chosen ally acts, then chooses next ally
4. Continue until all allies acted
5. Then enemies act similarly

**Player Agency**: Tactical decisions about sequencing

**Coordination**: Enables combo setups

**Simplified**: No tracking complex initiative scores

**Impact**: Appeared in 13th Age, narrative games, and various tactical systems as alternative to traditional initiative

## Mechanics

**Starting**: Some method determines first actor (surprise, roll, negotiation)

**Passing**: After your turn, choose any ally who hasn't acted

**Enemy Turn**: When all PCs acted, enemies act (with own passing or all-at-once)

**Round Structure**: Complete when everyone acted once

**Repeat**: New round, first player acts and begins passing again

**Restrictions**: Typically can't pass to someone who already acted this round

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated initiative could be player-choice not mechanical. Increased tactical coordination. Simplified tracking. Influenced dynamic turn-order thinking.

## Reception

**Praise**: Player agency, tactical coordination, simplified tracking

**Criticism**: Can slow if players debate who goes next, loses surprise element, benefits system-mastery (optimal passing order)

## Notes

- Various implementations across systems
- 13th Age's "flexible initiative" variant
