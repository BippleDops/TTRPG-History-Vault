# Strings (Monsterhearts)

**First Appeared**: [[Monsterhearts]] (2012)
**Designer**: [[Avery Alder]]
**Category**: Influence Mechanic / Social Leverage

## Overview

Strings, created by Avery Alder for Monsterhearts (2012), mechanize social influence and emotional leverage where characters gain Strings on others through manipulation, intimacy, or vulnerability, then spend Strings to mechanically force actions, deny actions, or gain bonuses—creating teen drama power dynamics where "I have a String on you" means "I have leverage/influence over you" that can be cashed for mechanical benefits, revolutionizing how games handle social power and influence beyond simple Charisma checks, demonstrating interpersonal dynamics could be tracked and weaponized mechanically.

**Gaining Strings**: Through moves like "Turn Someone On" (seduction), discovering secrets, creating emotional vulnerability, manipulation

**Spending Strings**: To force NPCs to act, give PCs -1 forward, offer XP for doing what you want

**Teen Drama**: Perfectly captures high school emotional leverage and manipulation

**Influence Economy**: Social power as spendable currency

**Impact**: Influenced Urban Shadows (Debt), various social-manipulation games, demonstrated influence could be mechanical resource

## Mechanics

**Gaining Strings**:
- Turn Someone On (seduce/attract)
- Gaze Into the Abyss (learn secret)
- Shut Someone Down (humiliate)
- Various playbook moves
- GM award for creating vulnerability

**Spending Strings**:
- **On NPCs**: Force them to do what you want (they do it or face consequences)
- **On PCs**: Offer XP to do what you suggest, or give them -1 forward
- Narrative leverage in social scenes

**Multiple Strings**: Can accumulate several on same target

**Asymmetric**: You might have Strings on them; they might have Strings on you

**Drama Generator**: String usage creates complications and drama

## Design Philosophy

**Social Influence Mechanics**: Interpersonal dynamics as spendable resource

**Teen Drama**: Captures manipulation and emotional leverage

**Non-Monetary Power**: Social influence beyond money/violence

**Asymmetric Relationships**: Unequal leverage creating tension

**Weaponized Intimacy**: Vulnerability as exploitable

## Cultural Impact

**Social Mechanics**: Demonstrated influence tracking viability

**Urban Shadows Debt**: Direct inspiration for political favor system

**Teen Drama Gaming**: Perfect mechanical embodiment

**Influence Economy**: Template for social leverage mechanics

## Reception

**Praise**: Brilliant social mechanics, perfect for teen drama, creates authentic manipulation dynamics

**Criticism**: Can enable toxic player behavior if misused, some uncomfortable with weaponized intimacy

## Legacy

Influenced Debt (Urban Shadows), various social-influence mechanics. Demonstrated interpersonal leverage could be mechanical.
