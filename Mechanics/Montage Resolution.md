# Montage Resolution

**First AppearedMenu[[Blades in the Dark]] (2017)
**Designer**: [[John Harper]]
**Category**: Time Compression / Downtime Mechanic

## Overview

Montage Resolution in Blades in the Dark compresses weeks of downtime activities into structured 10-15 minute sequence where each player takes one downtime action (recover, work on project, reduce heat, indulge vice, train) with quick dice resolution, then play immediately moves to next score—creating film-style time compression eliminating session-consuming between-adventure minutiae while maintaining mechanical significance of recovery, advancement, and personal projects, demonstrating how structured menus with single-action limits could preserve downtime importance while preventing pacing problems.

**One Action Per Player**: Each scoundrel gets single downtime activity

**Quick ResolutionMenuRoll and determine outcome rapidly

**Immediate TransitionMenuAfter all resolve, next score begins

**Film MontageMenuLike training montages or time-passage in movies

**Structured Menu**: Clear options preventing analysis paralysis

**Impact**: Became FitD standard. Influenced narrative game downtime handling. Demonstrated compression maintaining importance.

## Downtime Actions

**Recover**: Clear stress, heal harm

**Long-Term Project**: Advance project clock

**Reduce Heat**: Lower law enforcement attention

**Train**: Improve action ratings

**Indulge Vice**: Clear stress through vice (with overindulgence risks)

**Acquire Asset**: Gain temporary resource/contact

**Crafting**: Create items (some playbooks)

Each action has specific mechanics and outcomes.

## Design Philosophy

**Time Compression**: Handle necessary activities without consuming sessions

**Meaningful Choices**: Limited actions force prioritization

**Mechanical Significance**: Downtime provides advancement/recovery

**Structured**: Clear menus prevent aimlessness

**Quick**: Resolve fast, maintain momentum

**Film Technique**: Borrowing cinematic time compression

## Cultural Impact

**FitD Standard**: All Forged in the Dark games include structured downtime

**Downtime Design**: Influenced how narrative games handle between-adventures

**Time Compression**: Template for quick resolution of extended time

**Menu Structure**: Clear option lists preventing paralysis

## Reception

**Praise**: Quick efficient downtime, meaningful choices, prevents session consumption, maintains importance

**Criticism**: Some want more downtime depth, one-action feels limiting, can rush important moments

## Legacy

Became FitD mandatory element. Influenced downtime across narrative gaming. Demonstrated how to compress time while maintaining mechanical significance.
