# Faction Clocks

**First Appeared**: [[Blades in the Dark]] (2017)
**Designer**: [[John Harper]]
**Category**: Faction Management / Off-Screen Events

## Overview

Faction Clocks extend Blades in the Dark's clock mechanics specifically for tracking faction plans and long-term projects of organizations independent of player crew—Lampblacks Clock "Eliminate Red Sashes" advances whether players care, Inspectors Clock "Raid Criminal Hideouts" ticks toward affecting players—creating living world where factions pursue agendas, respond to events, and advance plans off-screen, giving GMs tools for managing complex political landscapes with many organizations having simultaneous goals while maintaining transparency about developing situations.

**Faction Activity**: Organizations have goals tracked via clocks

**Advancement**: Ticks based on downtime, faction rolls, logical progression

**Player Impact**: Crew actions can advance, halt, or reverse faction clocks

**Living World**: City feels alive with organizations acting independently

**Transparency**: Clocks visible to players showing developing situations

## Mechanics

**Creating Faction Clocks**: Each major faction gets 1-3 long-term project clocks

**Advancement Methods**:
- **Downtime Phase**: Faction roll determining progress
- **Logical Consequences**: Player actions affecting faction plans
- **Time Passage**: Some clocks advance with time
- **Rival Action**: Other factions interfering

**Completion**: When faction clock fills, project completes with consequences

**Player Interference**: Crew can deliberately advance or halt faction clocks through scores

## Design Philosophy

**Living World**: Factions don't wait for players

**Political Complexity**: Multiple organizations with agendas

**Transparent Tracking**: Players see what's developing

**Player Agency**: Can interfere with faction plans

**Consequence Web**: Actions affect multiple factions

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated faction management through clocks. Influenced political campaign mechanics. Showed off-screen events could be transparent yet maintain mystery about outcomes.

## Reception

**Praise**: Creates living city, transparent faction tracking, political complexity

**Criticism**: Tracking overhead, can overwhelm with too many clocks
