# Funnel Adventures

**First Appeared**: [[Dungeon Crawl Classics]] (2012)
**Designer**: [[Joseph Goodman]]
**Category**: Character Creation / Adventure Structure

## Overview

Funnel Adventures, pioneered in Dungeon Crawl Classics (2012), start campaigns with each player controlling 3-4 zero-level peasants (farmers, beggars, animal handlers) thrust into deadly adventure where most will die horribly, with survivors becoming 1st-level adventurers whose class is determined by how they survived—creating brutal character-generation-through-play where the meat-grinder "funnel" kills weak/unlucky while strong/lucky emerge as heroes with earned backstories from shared traumatic experience.

**Zero-Level Characters**: Commoners with 1d4 HP, minimal abilities, ridiculous occupations

**High Mortality**: Expect 50-75% death rate

**Survivors Become Heroes**: Who lives becomes 1st-level character with class based on funnel actions

**Shared Trauma**: Party bonds through surviving together

**Instant Investment**: Survivors feel earned through play not chargen choices

## Mechanics

**Start with 3-4 Peasants** each with:
- Occupation (Chicken Butcher, Eel Fisher, Ditch Digger)
- Random gear from occupation
- 1d4 HP (extremely fragile)
- Minimal combat ability

**Deadly Dungeon**: Brutal challenges killing many

**Random Survival**: Luck and player cleverness determine who lives

**Advancement**: Survivors hit 1st-level, choose class based on funnel actions

**Bonding**: Survivors become party with shared history

## Cultural Impact

Popularized 0-level funnels across OSR. Demonstrated character-generation-through-play viability. Created memorable brutal first sessions bonding survivors.

## Reception

Praised for bonding survivors, earned backstories, brutal fun. Some find high mortality frustrating. DCC fans love funnels as signature element.
