# Spotlight Mechanics

**First Appeared**: [[Primetime Adventures]] (2004) as Screen Presence
**Designer**: [[Matt Wilson]]
**Category**: Narrative Structure / Equitable Focus

## Overview

Spotlight Mechanics, pioneered in Primetime Adventures' Screen Presence system (2004), mathematically distribute narrative focus across campaign ensuring every character gets featured episodes—each episode, characters have Screen Presence rating (1-3) determining how central they are to that episode's story, rotating throughout season so everyone gets spotlight, solving ensemble play's perennial problem of unequal attention by mechanically enforcing balanced focus, influencing how games think about ensuring all players get meaningful participation.

**Screen Presence**: Rating 1-3 per episode
- **3**: This is your featured episode, your Issue is central
- **2**: Important supporting role
- **1**: Background/minor role

**Season Distribution**: Across 5-episode season, each player gets 9 total Screen Presence distributed (e.g., 1,2,3,2,1)

**Equitable**: Mathematical guarantee everyone gets spotlight episode

**Television Structure**: Mimicking TV ensemble shows rotating character focus

**Impact**: Demonstrated spotlight could be mechanically enforced. Influenced ensemble gaming thinking.

## Mechanics

**Pre-Season**: Distribute Screen Presence across episodes

**During Episode**: Screen Presence determines:
- Cards drawn in conflicts
- Story centrality
- Issue relevance
- Narrative weight

**Rotation**: Each session different character is featured

**Ensemble**: All participate but focus shifts

## Design Philosophy

**Equitable Spotlight**: Mathematical fairness

**Television Emulation**: TV ensemble structure

**Rotating Focus**: Everyone gets moments

**Predictable**: Knowing when your spotlight episode comes

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated spotlight could be mechanically distributed. Influenced ensemble gaming. Showed mathematical fairness could enhance rather than constrain drama.

## Reception

**Praise**: Ensures everyone gets spotlight, creates ensemble dynamics, fair distribution

**Criticism**: Can feel artificial, some moments want natural focus, mathematical enforcement feels gamey

## Legacy

Influenced ensemble gaming thinking. Some games adopted similar systems. Demonstrated mathematical spotlight distribution viability.
