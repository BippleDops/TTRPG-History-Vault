# Step Dice (Cortex)

**First Appeared**: Cortex Plus (Marvel Heroic Roleplaying, 2012)
**Designer**: Cam Banks, Rob Donoghue, others
**Category**: Dice Mechanic / Trait Rating

## Overview

Step Dice in Cortex systems rate traits/abilities by die size (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12) rather than numerical bonuses, creating resolution where you assemble pool of trait dice (skill d8 + asset d6 + distinction d4) rolling all and taking two highest for total, with larger dice providing better average results without arithmetic overhead, creating flexible modular system where anything can be rated as die and mixed into pools—powers, relationships, assets, complications all rated d4-d12 and contributing to rolls.

**Die Size as Rating**: d4 (worst) to d12 (best) rates trait quality

**Assemble Pools**: Combine multiple relevant trait dice

**Roll and Sum Best Two**: Taking highest two dice for total

**Modular Flexibility**: Anything can be rated as die and added to pool

**Impact**: Cortex signature. Influenced how designers think about trait ratings and modular dice pools.

## Mechanics

**Trait Ratings**:
- d4: Poor/hindrance
- d6: Average
- d8: Good
- d10: Great
- d12: Exceptional

**Building Pools**: Add dice for relevant traits (skill + attribute + distinction + asset + etc.)

**Resolution**: Roll pool, sum two highest dice

**Complications**: Often create penalty dice in opponent pools

## Games Using Step Dice

**Marvel Heroic**: Powers and abilities rated d6-d12

**Leverage RPG**: Roles and skills as dice

**Cortex Prime**: Flexible framework with step dice

**Cam Banks Designs**: Signature across his work

## Impact

Influenced modular dice pool thinking. Demonstrated die size could rate traits. Cortex's signature mechanic.
