# Love Letters

**First Appeared**: [[Apocalypse World]] (2010)
**Designer**: [[D. Vincent Baker]]
**Category**: Session-Start Technique / Between-Session Development

## Overview

Love Letters provide GM technique for handling between-session developments through personalized written prompts given to individual players before session, presenting situation and offering choices with mechanical consequences—"During downtime, you encountered someone from your past. Who were they? Roll+Sharp: 10+ they owe you, 7-9 mutual favors, 6- you owe them"—creating character-specific content advancing personal storylines while mechanically resolving off-screen events, becoming standard PbtA technique for session-start engagement and between-session time jumps.

**Format**: Short letter addressed to specific player/character

**Content**: Situation prompt + choices + mechanical resolution

**Timing**: Given before session (between sessions or at session start)

**Purpose**: Resolve off-screen time, create hooks, personalize content, advance storylines

**Impact**: Became common PbtA technique. Demonstrated personalized prompts could handle time jumps elegantly.

## Structure

**Address Player**: "Dear [Player Name],"

**Describe Situation**: What happened during downtime/between sessions

**Offer Choices**: Usually 2-3 options or roll

**Mechanical Resolution**: Choices have mechanical consequences

**Return to Play**: Session starts with Love Letter outcomes established

**Example**:
"Dear Sarah,

While the party rested, you explored the ancient library. You found a tome that might answer your questions about the curse, but reading it risked something. What did you do?

- Read it despite risks: Roll+Int. 10+: gain knowledge and keep sanity. 7-9: gain knowledge but mark corruption. 6-: the knowledge comes with terrible price.
- Left it sealed: Mark XP for restraint but return empty-handed.
- Took it to show the wizard: They're now interested in you.

See you at the table!"

## Usage Contexts

**Between Sessions**: Handling off-screen time

**Extended Downtime**: Weeks/months/years passing

**Separated Characters**: Individual experiences when party splits

**Personal Storylines**: Character-specific content

**Campaign Shifts**: Major time jumps or situation changes

**Session Start**: Engaging players immediately upon arrival

## Design Philosophy

**Personalization**: Individual attention for each player

**Between-Session Engagement**: Gaming continues between sessions

**Agency**: Players choose outcomes not GM deciding off-screen

**Mechanical Integration**: Choices have mechanical consequences

**Story Advancement**: Personal storylines progress

**Time Compression**: Handle weeks/months narratively

## Cultural Impact

**PbtA Standard**: Common technique in Apocalypse World-descended games

**Personalized Content**: Demonstrated value of individual player attention

**Between-Session Gaming**: Gaming extending beyond table time

**Engagement Tool**: Players excited receiving Love Letters

## Reception

**Praise**: Personal attention, story advancement, engaging start, elegant time-jump handling

**Criticism**: Preparation time, inconsistent if some players get them others don't, can feel like homework

## Legacy

Became common PbtA GM technique. Influenced personalized content in various games. Demonstrated between-session engagement techniques.
