# Metacurrency Systems

**First Appeared**: Various games 1970s-present (formalized differently across systems)
**Category**: Resource Management / Player Agency Currency

## Overview

Metacurrency Systems provide renewable resources (Fate Points, Hero Points, Bennies, Inspiration) enabling players to influence dice, reroll failures, activate abilities, or introduce facts, creating player agency layer where tactical resource management lets you push for success at crucial moments rather than accepting pure randomness, appearing across nearly all modern RPGs in various forms—FATE's Fate Points (earned through compels), Savage Worlds' Bennies (poker chips for rerolls), D&D 5E's Inspiration (advantage for good roleplay)—demonstrating how limited meta-resources enhance player engagement.

**Common Forms**:
- **Fate Points** (FATE): Invoke Aspects, refuse compels
- **Bennies** (Savage Worlds): Reroll, soak wounds
- **Hero Points** (Pathfinder/13th Age): Reroll, cheat death
- **Inspiration** (D&D 5E): Grant Advantage
- **Artha** (Burning Wheel): Bonus dice, introduction of facts
- **Action Points** (Various): Extra actions, special maneuvers

**Purpose**: Let players influence fate at critical moments

**Economy**: Limited pool renewable through play

**Impact**: Became standard game element across modern RPGs

## Common Metacurrency Features

### Earning Currency

**Different Methods Across Games**:

**FATE**: Compels (accept complication for FP)

**Savage Worlds**: Refresh each session, earn for great roleplay

**D&D 5E Inspiration**: GM awards for exceptional roleplay

**Burning Wheel Artha**: Pursuing Beliefs/Instincts

**13th Age**: Automatic per session plus special triggers

**Hero Points**: Level-based or GM awarded

### Spending Currency

**Rerolls**: Most common use—reroll failed die

**Bonuses**: Add bonus to roll

**Special Abilities**: Activate powerful moves

**Cheat Death**: Survive otherwise-fatal

**Introduce Facts**: Add elements to fiction

**Manipulate Dice**: Before or after rolling

**Extra Actions**: Additional turn/action

### Renewal

**Per Session**: Refresh at session start

**Milestone**: Regain at story points

**Through Play**: Earn via roleplay/actions

**Compels/Complications**: Accept problems for currency

**GM Award**: Exceptional play rewarded

## Design Philosophy

Metacurrency serves:

**Player Agency**: Influence randomness

**Dramatic Timing**: Push success at crucial moments

**Heroic Resilience**: Protagonists can push beyond normal limits

**Economy**: Strategic resource management

**Roleplaying Incentive**: Currency rewards good play

**Safety Valve**: Prevent catastrophic bad luck

## Cultural Impact

Became near-universal game element. Demonstrated player-agency resources enhanced engagement. Influenced how designers think about heroic capability and dramatic timing.

## Reception

**Praise**: Player agency, dramatic timing, heroic feel, mitigates bad luck

**Criticism**: Can feel video-gamey, disrupts simulation, varies by implementation quality
