# Lifepath Systems

**First Appeared**: [[Traveller]] (1977)
**Category**: Character Creation / History Generation

## Overview

Lifepath Systems create characters through sequential career choices representing pre-game history—Traveller's terms of service (4-year military/merchant/scout careers with survival rolls and skill acquisition), Cyberpunk's lifepath events (childhood, relationships, enemies made), Burning Wheel's elaborate lifepath burning (cultural origin → settings → skills earned)—generating detailed backstories with mechanical consequences while producing emergent character concepts discovered through process rather than planned, demonstrating how systematic history-building could create investment and depth while raising death-in-chargen possibility (Traveller's infamous character death during creation).

**Structure**: Sequential choices/rolls building history

**Traveller** (1977): 4-year career terms, survival rolls, skill acquisition, possible death

**Cyberpunk** (1988): Childhood → culture → roles → life events → relationships/enemies

**Burning Wheel** (2002): Cultural stock → settings → skills → traits from lifepaths

**Emergent**: History discovered through system

**Mechanical Consequences**: Past determines present capabilities

**Death in Chargen**: Traveller's notorious possibility—character dies before play begins

## Traveller Lifepaths (1977)

### Process

1. **Roll Stats**: Standard attributes
2. **Choose Career**: Navy, Marines, Merchant, Scout, etc.
3. **Survival Roll**: Pass or die/injured
4. **Skill Acquisition**: Gain skills from career tables
5. **Promotion/Benefits**: Advance within career
6. **Reenlist/Muster Out**: Continue or retire
7. **Repeat**: Each 4-year term
8. **Muster Out**: Gain benefits for service
9. **Begin Play**: With career history

### Character Death

**Survival Rolls**: Failed roll can kill character before play

**Controversial**: Creating character only to die in generation

**Simulation**: Realistic—careers have risks

**Beloved**: Many fans love risk/reward

**Infamous**: Traveller known for death-in-chargen

### Skills from History

Career determines skills:
- Navy: Engineering, Gunnery, Vacc Suit
- Scout: Pilot, Navigation, Survival
- Merchant: Trading, Persuasion, Steward

**Emergence**: Character capabilities flow from history

## Cyberpunk Lifepaths (1988)

### Structure

**Cultural Origins**: Where from (Corporate, Street, Nomad)

**Family Background**: Parents' status

**Personality**: Motivations and quirks

**Life Events**: Roll yearly events (1d10 years)
- Made enemies
- Found lovers
- Gained contacts
- Suffered tragedies

**Friends and Enemies**: Specific NPCs created

### Relationships Generated

Lifepaths create:
- Romantic partners
- Enemies and rivals
- Friends and contacts
- Family complications

**Story Hooks**: Built-in adventure seeds

## Burning Wheel Lifepaths (2002)

### Elaborate System

**Born Lifepath**: Cultural origin (City Born, Village Born, Noble Born)

**Setting Lifepaths**: Sequential career paths within settings
- City Dweller → Shopkeeper → Merchant → Magnate
- Peasant → Soldier → Captain → Knight

**Skills and Traits**: Each lifepath grants specific capabilities

**Resources**: Lifepaths determine starting wealth/property

**Years**: Each lifepath takes years, determining age

### Character Burning

Creating character called "burning":
- Choose sequential lifepaths
- Accumulate skills, traits, resources
- Determine age from years spent
- Create psychologically deep character from history

**Investment**: Elaborate process creates attachment

## Design Philosophy

**History Matters**: Past determines present

**Emergence**: Discover character through process

**Investment**: Time creating builds attachment

**Mechanical Integration**: History has mechanical weight

**Story Hooks**: Built-in backgrounds

**Simulation**: Careers realistically grant skills

## Cultural Impact

**Traveller Death**: Infamous character death in chargen

**Emergent Concepts**: History-generated characters

**Investment Creation**: Process builds attachment

**Story Integration**: Mechanical histories providing hooks

**Burning Wheel**: Modern sophisticated implementation

## Reception

**Advocates**: Love emergent characters, depth, investment from process

**Critics**: Time-consuming, death in chargen frustrating, random history limits concept realization

**Hybrid Fans**: Appreciate when done well (Burning Wheel) but not always want complexity

## Legacy

Continues in various forms. Traveller maintained lifepaths. Burning Wheel refined to sophistication. Cyberpunk's relationship generation influential. Demonstrates viable alternative to stat-array character creation.
