# Hakan Seyalıoğlu

**Active Years**: 2016-present
**Birth Year**: ~1986
**Nationality**: United States

Hakan Seyalıoğlu co-designed Dialect (2018) and Sign (2020) with Kathryn Hymes, creating language-creation games where isolated communities develop unique languages that die when communities end, winning ENnie Gold 2019 for Best Writing and establishing language creation as recognized gaming genre, demonstrating how linguistics and communication could be compelling gameplay through collaborative world-building and poignant inevitable language death.

## Major Works

**Dialect (2018)**: Language creation game with inevitable death—ENnie Gold 2019

**Sign (2020)**: Deaf community creating sign language

**Partnership**: Collaboration with Kathryn Hymes producing innovative linguistic games

## Design Philosophy

Emphasizes language as gameplay, collaborative linguistics, community through communication, poignant inevitability.

## Cultural Impact

Co-created language gaming genre. ENnie Gold recognition. Demonstrated linguistic focus viable. Influenced communication-focused design.
