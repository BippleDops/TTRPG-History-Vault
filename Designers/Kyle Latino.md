# Kyle Latino

**Active Years**: 2012-present
**Birth Year**: ~1980
**Nationality**: United States

Kyle Latino designed Swords Without Master (2013), creating poetic sword & sorcery game with phases (Perilous, Rogueish, Discovery) using dice pools and tone dice, demonstrating how genre emulation could work through structured pacing and atmospheric framework, influencing minimalist sword & sorcery design though remaining cult classic due to unusual structure requiring adaptation.

**Swords Without Master** (2013): Minimalist Sword & Sorcery

**Phases**: Perilous (action), Rogueish (clever), Discovery (mystery)

**Tone Dice**: Determining scene atmosphere

**Poetic**: Evocative minimalist language

**Impact**: Influenced sword & sorcery indie gaming. Demonstrated phase-based structure viability.
