# Nils Karlén

**Active Years**: 2014-present
**Birth Year**: ~1975
**Nationality**: Sweden

Nils Karlén designed Tales from the Loop (2017) and Things from the Flood (2018), adapting Simon Stålenhag's iconic retrofuturist artwork into RPGs using Year Zero Engine where players portray 1980s/90s kids investigating mysteries in small Swedish/American towns filled with abandoned robots and strange technology, capturing Stranger Things-style kid adventures with melancholic Scandinavian atmosphere, winning multiple ENnie Golds and demonstrating how art-to-game adaptations could succeed when respecting source material's tone.

## Major Works

### Tales from the Loop (2017)

80s kids mystery in retrofuturist setting:

**Stålenhag's Art**: Based on artist's evocative paintings

**Kid Protagonists**: Playing 10-15 year olds investigating mysteries

**The Loop**: Particle accelerator creating weird technology/phenomena

**80s Nostalgia**: Bikes, cassette tapes, small-town life

**No Kids Die**: Explicit rule—kids can't permanently die

**Melancholic Tone**: Swedish melancholy amid adventure

**Year Zero Engine**: Adapted for kid mysteries

**ENnie Gold 2017**: Won Best Game, Best Writing, Best Production

**Impact**: Established retrofuturist gaming. Kids-as-protagonists gaming. Stålenhag's aesthetic in tabletop.

### Things from the Flood (2018)

90s sequel to Loop where kids are older, stakes higher:

**Teenage Protagonists**: 13-18 year olds

**Kids CAN Die**: Raising stakes from Loop

**Darker Tone**: 90s grunge and cynicism

**The Flood**: Loop's successor project

**Coming of Age**: Teenage experiences and maturation

**Impact**: Showed how to continue/darken earlier game

## Design Philosophy

Karlén emphasizes:

**Art Inspiration**: Adapting visual art to gaming

**Kid Protagonists**: Children's perspectives and experiences

**Nostalgic 80s/90s**: Period-specific atmosphere

**Retrofuturism**: Abandoned futures aesthetic

**Melancholic Beauty**: Swedish sensibilities

**Year Zero Adaptation**: Using engine for different genres

## Innovation

**Retrofuturist Gaming**: Stålenhag aesthetic in tabletop

**Kid Protagonist Rules**: Mechanical considerations for child characters

**No Death Rule**: Protecting kid characters mechanically

**Art-to-Game Adaptation**: Successful visual-to-gameplay conversion
