# Jesse Ross

**Active Years**: 2012-present
**Birth Year**: ~1985
**Nationality**: United States

Jesse Ross stands as contemporary indie horror's premier designer, creating Trophy Dark (2019—doomed treasure-hunters descending into nightmare forest), Trophy Gold (2020—non-doomed variant), and The Skeletons (2015—immortal guardians witnessing time), demonstrating how minimal rules can create maximum tension through death-spirals, brilliant Ruin mechanics (corruption track), and Hunt Rolls (risk-reward dice pools), influencing doomed-protagonist design and establishing Trophy as major horror RPG.

## Major Works

### Trophy Dark (2019)

Doomed treasure-hunters in nightmare forest—you WILL die or become Lost (corrupted into monster).

**Ruin Mechanic**: Track 1-6; at 6 you're Lost. Everything advances Ruin.

**Hunt Roll**: Roll d6 equal to Ruin—more dice = better success but risking 6s that advance Ruin

**Minimal OSR Horror**: Extremely simple rules, maximum dread

**Inevitable Doom**: Death or corruption guaranteed; question is how much treasure first

**Critical Acclaim**: Established Trophy as major horror RPG

### Trophy Gold (2020)

Non-doomed variant where death is possible but not inevitable, creating sustained campaign play.

**Ruin Still Central**: But reaching 6 Ruin doesn't automatically end character

**Dungeon Delving**: Gold-seeking adventures with Trophy's horror tone

**Campaign Viable**: Can play ongoing rather than one-shot doomed narratives

**Refinement**: Addresses players wanting Trophy without guaranteed doom

### The Skeletons (2015)

Immortal skeletons guarding tombs/treasures across centuries, witnessing visitors, aging, and inevitable decay.

**Immortal Perspective**: Playing across vast time

**Guard Duty**: Protecting what you're bound to guard

**Tragedy**: Watching world change while you persist unchanging

**Poignant**: Melancholy meditation on time, duty, immortality

## Design Philosophy

Ross emphasizes:

**Doomed Protagonists**: Death/corruption certainty creating specific dramatic focus

**Minimal Rules**: Simple systems generating complex tension

**Horror Through Mechanics**: Death-spirals and corruption tracks creating dread

**Risk-Reward**: Hunt Roll's push-luck brilliance

**Dark Fantasy**: Weird horror aesthetics

**Accessible Complexity**: Simple to learn, deep in play

## Innovations

**Ruin Mechanic**: Corruption track with death-spiral creating escalating tension

**Hunt Roll**: Brilliant risk-reward where more dice risk more corruption

**Trophy System**: Influential minimal horror framework

**Doomed Design**: Guaranteed negative outcomes enhancing focus

**Gold/Dark Split**: Two modes (doomed vs. sustainable) from one engine

Praised for elegant dread creation, brilliant mechanics, Dark Souls influence, accessible horror. Players report intense experiences. Trophy Gold addressed "want to continue" desire.

## Legacy

Trophy established as major indie horror brand. Ruin and Hunt Roll mechanics influenced other designers. Doomed-protagonist design validated. OSR-adjacent horror gaming influenced.
