# Chris Taylor

**Active Years**: 2014-present
**Birth Year**: ~1986
**Nationality**: United Kingdom

Chris Taylor co-designs with Grant Howitt (Spire, Heart) and co-founded Rowan, Rook and Decard, bringing development rigor, business acumen, and refinement discipline to Howitt's creative exuberance—creating partnership where Taylor's systematic approach balances Howitt's prolific creativity, resulting in polished products combining ambitious design with sustainable business model, demonstrating how collaborative partnerships with complementary skills produce stronger results than solo efforts.

## Major Works

### Spire and Heart (with Grant Howitt)

Co-designed both games, contributing particularly to:
- Mechanical refinement and playtesting
- Business planning and production
- Development and editing
- Rowan, Rook and Decard operations

## Design Partnership

Taylor and Howitt demonstrate effective collaboration:
- **Howitt**: Creative vision, initial design, aesthetic direction
- **Taylor**: Development, refinement, business operations, production management

This division of labor enables ambitious projects to reach polished completion.

## Business Innovation

**Rowan, Rook and Decard**: Co-founded successful UK indie publisher demonstrating:
- Professional production values
- Sustainable business model
- Author support and development
- Beautiful physical products

## Design Philosophy

Taylor emphasizes:

**Development Rigor**: Playtesting and refining ambitious designs

**Production Excellence**: Creating beautiful books and professional quality

**Business Sustainability**: Enabling creative work through solid operations

**Collaborative Partnership**: Complementary skills strengthening output
