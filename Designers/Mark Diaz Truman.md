# Mark Diaz Truman

**Active Years**: 2012-present
**Birth Year**: ~1980
**Nationality**: United States

Mark Diaz Truman co-founded Magpie Games and designed/co-designed Urban Shadows (political urban fantasy PbtA), Cartel (narco-thriller examining drug war), and Root RPG (adapting acclaimed board game), demonstrating how PbtA could serve political intrigue and difficult subjects while building Magpie into major indie publisher combining design excellence with professional operations, influencing both game design (political PbtA, factional play) and indie publishing (professional standards, sustainable business).

## Major Works

### Urban Shadows (2015, with Andrew Medeiros)

Urban fantasy political PbtA with Debt mechanics and faction play.

**Co-Design Role**: Particularly contributed to political mechanics, faction dynamics, setting development

**Impact**: Established Magpie as publisher, demonstrated political PbtA viability

### Cartel (2018)

Narco-thriller PbtA addressing drug war's complexity with safety tools and thoughtful handling.

**Solo Design**: Tackled extremely difficult subject (Mexican drug war) with research and sensitivity

**Multi-Sided Play**: PCs from different factions (cartel, law, civilian)

**Controversial**: Subject matter sparked necessary discussions about appropriate gaming content

**Thoughtful**: Extensive safety tools, acknowledgment of real human cost

### Root RPG (2020, lead designer)

Led team adapting Cole Wehrle's Root board game to PbtA:

**Licensed Success**: Faithful adaptation bringing board game universe to RPG

**Vagabond Focus**: Playing wandering adventurers in woodland warfare

**Integration**: RPG and board game interaction

## Co-Founding Magpie Games

Truman co-founded and leads Magpie Games, building it into major indie publisher:

**Professional Standards**: High production values, editing, development

**Designer Support**: Fair contracts, creator support, professional development

**Commercial Success**: Multiple bestselling titles

**Diverse Catalog**: Urban Shadows, Masks, Root, others—varied successful games

**Business Innovation**: Sustainable indie publishing model

## Design Philosophy

Truman emphasizes:

**Political Gaming**: Factional play, social conflict, power dynamics

**Difficult Subjects**: Tackling complex topics with research and sensitivity

**PbtA Political Focus**: Demonstrating Apocalypse World works for intrigue

**Professional Publishing**: High standards for production and creator support

**Collaborative Design**: Working with teams and partners

**Safety Awareness**: Incorporating safety tools for sensitive content

## Innovations

**Debt Mechanics** (with Medeiros): Political favor system

**Multi-Factional Play**: PCs from opposing sides cooperating/conflicting

**Political PbtA**: Intrigue-focused Apocalypse World

**Publishing Model**: Professional indie operations

**Licensed Adaptation**: Board game to RPG conversion excellence
