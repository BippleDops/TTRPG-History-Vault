# Adam Koebel

**Active Years**: 2010-2020
**Birth Year**: ~1982
**Nationality**: Canada

Adam Koebel co-designed Dungeon World (2012) with Sage LaTorra and became prominent actual-play GM/streamer (RollPlay series), demonstrating fiction-first gaming through live play and teaching GM techniques to thousands through streaming, influencing both PbtA design (Dungeon World's massive success) and actual-play culture (educational GMing demonstrations) before retiring from public gaming in 2020 following controversy.

## Major Work: Dungeon World (2012)

Co-designed PbtA fantasy bringing narrative mechanics to traditional dungeon-crawling:

**Co-Design Role**: Collaborated with LaTorra on adapting AW to fantasy

**Commercial Success**: Best-selling PbtA game introducing thousands to narrative gaming

**Teaching Tool**: Used extensively for teaching PbtA principles

**Impact**: Gateway between traditional and narrative gaming

## Actual-Play Career

**RollPlay Series**: Created/GMed multiple campaign streams

**Educational GMing**: Demonstrations teaching fiction-first techniques

**Streaming Pioneer**: Early prominent RPG streamer

**Community Building**: Built following through educational content

## 2020 Retirement

Koebel retired from public gaming following controversy, stepping back from design and streaming.

## Design Philosophy

Emphasized fiction-first play, collaborative world-building, teaching through demonstration, making narrative gaming accessible.

## Legacy

Dungeon World remains his lasting contribution. RollPlay series influenced early actual-play culture. Educational GMing helped popularize fiction-first techniques.
