## Overview

Jeremy Crawford is an American game designer who co-designed *Blue Rose* (2005) for [[Green Ronin Publishing]] before joining [[Wizards of the Coast]] in 2007. He was rules manager for [[D&D Fourth Edition (2008)]] and, with [[Mike Mearls]], co-lead designer of [[Dungeons & Dragons Fifth Edition (2014)]], for which he served as lead rules developer, managing editor, and author of the official "Sage Advice" rulings. He became game director for Dungeons & Dragons in 2024 and led the revised 2024 *Player's Handbook*; he left Wizards of the Coast in April 2025 and became game director at Darrington Press.
