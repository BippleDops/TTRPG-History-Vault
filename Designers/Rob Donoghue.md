## Overview

Rob Donoghue is an American game designer and, with [[Fred Hicks]], a co-founder of [[Evil Hat Productions]]. The two developed [[FATE (2003)|Fate]] out of their dissatisfaction with *Fudge* and released its first edition through a Yahoo! Group in January 2003, followed by a second edition that August. Donoghue was a lead designer of *Spirit of the Century* (2006), which carried the third edition of the Fate rules, and one of the designers of *The Dresden Files Roleplaying Game* (2010). Outside Evil Hat he co-designed *Leverage: The Roleplaying Game* (2011) and contributed to Cortex Plus titles and to [[D&D Fourth Edition (2008)]] supplements.
