## Overview

Mike Mearls is an American game designer who worked at [[Wizards of the Coast]] from 2005 to 2023. He contributed to late [[Dungeons & Dragons Third Edition (2000)|3rd Edition]] books, was closely associated with the [[D&D Fourth Edition (2008)|4th Edition]] *Essentials* line, and co-designed the *Castle Ravenloft* board game (2010). With [[Jeremy Crawford]] he was co-lead designer of [[Dungeons & Dragons Fifth Edition (2014)]], later serving as senior manager of D&D research and design and then franchise creative director before moving to the *Magic: The Gathering* studio in 2020. He was laid off in December 2023, joined [[Chaosium]] as executive producer of roleplaying games in 2024, and moved to Asmodee in 2025.
