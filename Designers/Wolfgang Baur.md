# Wolfgang Baur

**Active Years**: 1992-present
**Birth Year**: 1967
**Nationality**: American

## Biography

Wolfgang Baur (born 1967) is a game designer, publisher, and entrepreneur who has significantly shaped modern tabletop roleplaying through his design work, innovative publishing models, and influential monster design. As founder of Kobold Press, he created one of the most successful third-party RPG publishers, demonstrating that independent companies could produce professional, high-quality content for major game systems.

Baur's gaming career began with freelance design work for TSR in the early 1990s. His early contributions to Dragon Magazine and Dungeon Magazine caught attention for their creativity and polish. He quickly moved into adventure and setting design, contributing memorable work to Planescape, Al-Qadim, and other TSR settings.

During his time at TSR and later Wizards of the Coast, Baur authored numerous adventures and supplements, including significant Planescape contributions that helped define that setting's surreal, philosophical tone. His work showed distinctive voice combining dark fairy tale aesthetics, literary influences, and encounter design focused on memorable, flavorful monsters.

In 2006, Baur pioneered the "Open Design" model, a patron-based publishing approach where supporters backed projects during development and participated in the design process. This innovative model predated Kickstarter and demonstrated the viability of community-supported game publishing. Open Design produced acclaimed products including adventure paths and the Midgard Campaign Setting.

When Kickstarter emerged, Baur quickly recognized its potential for game publishing. His Midgard Campaign Setting Kickstarter (2011) was among the earliest successful RPG crowdfunding campaigns, validating the platform for the industry. Subsequent campaigns for Tome of Beasts and other products demonstrated that independent publishers could achieve substantial success through crowdfunding.

Open Design evolved into Kobold Press, which became a major third-party publisher for D&D 5th Edition. Kobold Press products—including the Tome of Beasts series, Creature Codex, and various adventures—achieved commercial and critical success, establishing the company as leading third-party publisher for 5E.

Beyond publishing, Baur contributed extensively to game design education through the Kobold Guide series, collecting essays on game design, world-building, monster design, and other topics from industry professionals. These guides became valuable resources for aspiring designers.

Baur continues leading Kobold Press while remaining active as designer, with recent projects including the Project Black Flag open license initiative responding to changes in D&D licensing.

## Design Philosophy

Baur's design philosophy emphasizes evocative content, DM support, and dark fairy tale aesthetics:

**Evocative Over Mechanical**: Game content should inspire imagination first, provide mechanics second. Flavor and atmosphere create memorable experiences.

**Monster-Centric Design**: Compelling monsters drive great encounters. Invest design energy in making monsters interesting, flavorful, and tactically engaging.

**Dark Fairy Tale Aesthetic**: Draw inspiration from European folklore, dark fantasy, and fairy tales. Magic should feel dangerous and wondrous, not merely functional.

**Support the DM**: Design should provide tools, inspiration, and framework for DMs rather than prescribing specific narratives. Enable creativity rather than constraining it.

**Literary Influences**: Bring literary sophistication to game design, drawing from mythology, folklore, and fantasy literature beyond standard D&D sources.

**Community Collaboration**: Involve the community in development through patron models and crowdfunding. Player feedback during design creates better products.

**Professional Third-Party Publishing**: Third-party publishers should maintain professional standards matching or exceeding official products in quality, production values, and design.

**Open Gaming Support**: Support open gaming licenses and initiatives that enable creative freedom and community development.

## Major Works

### Midgard Campaign Setting

Midgard represents Baur's most comprehensive world-building achievement, a dark fantasy setting drawing heavily on European and Middle Eastern folklore, mythology, and fairy tales. Unlike standard fantasy settings, Midgard embraces darker, more morally complex tone while maintaining playability.

The setting features distinctive nations and cultures inspired by real-world mythology: the Crossroads region with Germanic and Slavic influences, the Dragon Empire combining Byzantine and Eastern European elements, the Southlands with North African and Middle Eastern inspiration, and various other regions each with unique flavor.

Midgard emphasizes magic as dangerous and wondrous force, with powerful fey courts, dragon-ruled empires, and gods actively involved in mortal affairs. The setting provides detailed lore while leaving space for DM creativity, supporting various campaign styles from political intrigue to dungeon exploration.

Originally funded through Open Design's patron model and later through Kickstarter, Midgard demonstrated that independent publishers could create comprehensive campaign settings rivaling official products. The setting has been supported through numerous supplements, adventures, and sourcebooks across multiple editions.

Midgard's success influenced how third-party publishers approach campaign settings, demonstrating market appetite for alternatives to official settings and validating investment in detailed world-building.

### Tome of Beasts Series

The Tome of Beasts series represents Baur's monster design philosophy in practical form. These massive monster compendiums provide hundreds of original creatures for D&D 5th Edition, filling niches official products left unexplored while maintaining high quality standards.

Tome of Beasts (2016) emerged from a successful Kickstarter campaign and became Kobold Press's biggest hit. The book provided over 400 monsters with evocative lore, tactical depth, and distinctive abilities. Unlike generic monster collections, each creature received careful design attention, with interesting mechanics supporting flavorful concepts.

The series emphasized monsters from European folklore, dark fantasy, and mythology rather than repeating standard D&D creatures. This fresh content appealed to DMs seeking novelty for experienced players who knew standard monster capabilities.

Subsequent volumes—Creature Codex (2018) and Tome of Beasts 2 and 3—maintained quality while exploring different thematic territories. The series became essential resources for many 5E DMs, demonstrating that third-party monster design could match or exceed official quality.

The Tome of Beasts series influenced D&D 5E third-party publishing, proving substantial market existed for high-quality supplemental content. Its success helped establish Kobold Press as premier third-party publisher.

### Tyranny of Dragons

Tyranny of Dragons—consisting of Hoard of the Dragon Queen and The Rise of Tiamat—was among the first major adventures for D&D 5th Edition. Baur led the design team (with Steve Winter) creating this epic dragon-focused campaign spanning levels 1-15.

The adventure path featured classic D&D themes—dragons, the Cult of the Dragon, and apocalyptic threats—while introducing new players to 5E. Though reception was mixed regarding balance and pacing (partly due to being designed alongside still-developing 5E rules), the adventure demonstrated ambitious scope and provided memorable encounters.

Tyranny of Dragons showed major third-party designer involvement in official D&D products, with Kobold Press developing content for Wizards of the Coast. This collaboration highlighted Baur's industry stature and Kobold Press's capabilities.

### Kobold Guide Series

The Kobold Guide to Game Design series collects essays from professional game designers on various design topics. These guides serve educational purpose, helping aspiring designers learn craft from experienced professionals.

Volumes cover game design generally, board game design, world-building, plots and campaigns, and other topics. Contributors include industry luminaries sharing practical advice, techniques, and philosophy.

The guides influenced game design education by making professional knowledge accessible. They demonstrate Baur's commitment to supporting the broader design community and raising industry standards.

## Innovations and Contributions

### Patron Publishing Model

Baur pioneered the patron model through Open Design (founded 2006), where supporters funded projects during development and participated in design process through private forums. This innovative approach:

- Provided funding before product completion
- Engaged community in development
- Built audience investment in products
- Demonstrated viability of community-supported publishing
- Predated and influenced RPG crowdfunding culture

### Crowdfunding Leadership

Baur was among first RPG publishers recognizing Kickstarter's potential, launching successful campaigns demonstrating the platform's viability for game publishing. His early successes helped establish crowdfunding as standard RPG publishing model.

### Third-Party Publishing Excellence

Through Kobold Press, Baur demonstrated third-party publishers could achieve professional quality and commercial success, creating sustainable business producing 5E content, establishing production quality standards, building brand recognition rivaling official products, and supporting open gaming licenses.

### Monster Design Theory

Baur advanced monster design through practical work and theoretical writing, emphasizing flavor-driven mechanics, tactical interesting abilities, evocative lore integration, and design discipline in the Tome of Beasts series. His monster design influence appears across third-party and official products.

### Dark Fantasy Aesthetic

Baur helped establish darker, more folklore-based fantasy aesthetic in contemporary D&D, drawing from European fairy tales and mythology, embracing moral complexity, treating magic as dangerous and wondrous, and creating atmospheric, evocative content.

## Influence on Later Designers

Baur's influence extends through publishing innovation, design aesthetic, and community building:

**Third-Party Publishing**: Demonstrated viability and potential of third-party publishing for D&D 5E, establishing quality standards and business models, inspiring other third-party publishers, and proving market appetite for non-official content.

**Crowdfunding Adoption**: Early successful Kickstarter campaigns encouraged other designers and publishers to embrace crowdfunding, helping establish current funding landscape.

**Monster Design**: The Tome of Beasts' approach influenced how designers create monsters, emphasizing flavor, tactics, and originality. Many third-party monster products follow its model.

**Dark Fantasy Aesthetic**: Helped popularize darker, folklore-influenced fantasy in 5E era, influencing setting design and adventure tone across the industry.

**Community Engagement**: The patron model and community-focused approach influenced how publishers engage with audiences during development.

**Design Education**: The Kobold Guide series helped train new generation of designers, raising overall industry design literacy.

## Quotes and Interviews

"A great monster tells a story in its stat block. The mechanics should make the DM excited to run it, and the flavor should make the players remember it."

"The patron model taught me that involving your audience in development creates better products and stronger community. When people invest in creation, they become advocates."

"Midgard draws from the darker corners of European folklore because those stories have power. Fairy tales weren't originally safe, sanitized stories—they were about danger, transformation, and consequence."

"Third-party publishers can succeed by maintaining professional standards and creating content people want. It's not about competing with Wizards; it's about serving the community's needs."

"Kickstarter changed game publishing by letting creators connect directly with audiences. You can prove there's market for your idea before investing in production."

"The best monster designs come from 'what if' questions. What if angels were actually terrifying? What if scarecrows hunted people? Start with concept, then build mechanics supporting that concept."

## Legacy

Wolfgang Baur's legacy encompasses design excellence, publishing innovation, and community building. Through Kobold Press, he demonstrated that independent publishers could achieve significant commercial and creative success in the modern RPG landscape, creating sustainable third-party publishing business and establishing quality standards for 5E content.

His patron model and early crowdfunding leadership influenced how RPG projects are funded and developed, helping establish crowdfunding as standard industry practice. The community engagement approaches he pioneered affect how publishers interact with audiences.

As designer, Baur's monster design work influences how creatures are created across the industry. The Tome of Beasts series established new standards for third-party monster supplements, while his adventures and settings demonstrate sophisticated dark fantasy aesthetics.

The Kobold Guide series contributes to design education, helping new designers learn craft and raising overall industry standards. His willingness to share knowledge and support the community creates positive industry culture.

Through Project Black Flag and open gaming advocacy, Baur continues fighting for creator rights and open gaming, responding to industry changes affecting independent publishers.

Baur's career demonstrates that creative vision, business acumen, and community engagement can build successful, sustainable game publishing company. His influence extends beyond specific designs to shape industry structure, funding models, and community relationships.

Kobold Press's continued success and growth validates Baur's approach, proving there's substantial market for high-quality third-party content when executed with professionalism, creativity, and respect for both the community and the craft.
