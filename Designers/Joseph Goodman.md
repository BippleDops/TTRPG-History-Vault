## Overview

Joseph Goodman is an American game designer and the founder and owner of [[Goodman Games]], which he started in 2001 to publish *Broncosaurus Rex* under the new d20 System license. He began the Dungeon Crawl Classics adventure line in 2003 with the stated aim of publishing intelligent dungeon crawls for older gamers, created the *DragonMech* setting (2004), and wrote the [[Dungeon Crawl Classics (2012)|Dungeon Crawl Classics Role Playing Game]] (2012). Goodman Games has remained a freelancer-driven company without in-house designers, and it joined the ORC License alliance in January 2023 during the [[OGL 1.1 Controversy (2023)]].
