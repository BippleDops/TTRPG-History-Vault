# Brendan Conway

**Active Years**: 2014-present
**Birth Year**: ~1982
**Nationality**: United States

Brendan Conway revolutionized superhero gaming with Masks: A New Generation (2016), replacing traditional stats with Labels (emotional self-perceptions: Danger, Freak, Savior, Superior, Mundane) and creating Influence mechanics where adults and peers shift teen heroes' self-image mechanically, demonstrating how PbtA could serve coming-of-age superhero stories where identity formation matters more than power levels, winning ENnie Gold 2017 and establishing teen superhero as distinct gaming genre separate from adult hero play.

## Major Works

### Masks: A New Generation (2016)

Revolutionary teen superhero PbtA using Labels instead of stats:

**Labels Innovation**: Stats are self-perceptions (Am I dangerous? weird? heroic? better than others? just normal?)

**Influence**: Adults/peers shift Labels mechanically, representing how others' opinions affect teen identity

**Playbooks**: Emotional archetypes (The Bull, The Delinquent, The Doomed, The Janus, The Legacy, etc.) rather than power-sets

**Coming-of-Age**: Identity formation central, powers secondary

**ENnie Gold 2017**: Won Best Game

**Commercial Success**: Magpie's best-seller, multiple supplements, ongoing development

**Cultural Impact**: Established teen superhero as recognized genre. Labels influenced psychological stat designs. Influence mechanics widely adopted.

### Root: The Tabletop Roleplaying Game (2020)

Co-designed PbtA adaptation of Root board game with Magpie team.

**Licensed Adaptation**: Successfully converting board game to RPG

**Woodland Animal Politics**: Cute creatures in serious factional warfare

**Collaborative**: Team design for Magpie

## Design Philosophy

Conway emphasizes:

**Emotional Stats**: Labels as self-perception rather than objective capabilities

**Influence Mechanics**: Others affecting your identity mechanically

**Teen Experiences**: Centering adolescent perspectives

**Identity Formation**: Becoming who you are through play

**PbtA Mastery**: Understanding and innovating within Apocalypse World framework

**Superhero Genre**: Teen heroes as distinct from adult heroes

## Innovations

**Labels System**: Revolutionary emotional self-perception stats

**Influence Mechanics**: Authority figures shifting character identity

**Teen Superhero Focus**: Distinct genre from adult superheroing

**Conditions Over Hit Points**: Emotional states instead of health

**Grow Up/Give In**: Advancement choosing identity path
