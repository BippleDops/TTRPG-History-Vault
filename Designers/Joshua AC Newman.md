# Joshua AC Newman

**Active Years**: 2006-present
**Birth Year**: ~1975
**Nationality**: United States

Joshua AC Newman stands as indie RPG's premier social science fiction designer, creating Shock: Social Science Fiction (2006)—game using Issue-Shock matrix to collaboratively build speculative worlds examining social problems through technological/cultural changes—and Mobile Frame Zero (2012)—combining tactical mecha wargaming with narrative RPG—demonstrating how games could explore literary sci-fi's social commentary focus while maintaining mechanical sophistication.

## Major Works

### Shock: Social Science Fiction (2006)

Matrix-based worldbuilding where players cross Issues (social problems) with Shocks (changes) generating story space, creating literary sci-fi stories examining social questions through extrapolation.

**Innovation**: Issue-Shock matrix collaborative worldbuilding

**Literary Focus**: Explicitly inspired by Le Guin, Delany, Robinson—social commentary sci-fi

**Critical Design**: Using games to examine social issues through speculative lens

### Mobile Frame Zero (2012)

Tactical mecha wargame blending miniatures combat with narrative RPG elements, creating complete game supporting both tactical battles and character-driven stories.

**Hybrid Design**: Wargame precision with RPG narrative

**Mecha Focus**: Giant robots with tactical depth

**Narrative Integration**: Story alongside tactical play

### The Bloody-Handed Name of Bronze (2014)

Science fantasy exploring Bronze Age-inspired setting with deep worldbuilding and philosophical depth.

**Ambitious Setting**: Extensively developed world

**Philosophical**: Examining meaning, culture, power through mechanics

## Design Philosophy

Newman's approach emphasizes:

**Social Science Fiction**: Using sci-fi to examine contemporary social issues

**Collaborative Worldbuilding**: Matrix and structured tools for group creation

**Issue-Focused Gaming**: Centering social questions mechanically

**Tactical Meets Narrative**: Combining precision mechanics with story

**Literary Inspiration**: Adapting written sci-fi traditions to gaming

## Innovations

**Issue-Shock Matrix**: Collaborative worldbuilding crossing problems with changes

**Wargame-RPG Hybrid**: Mobile Frame Zero's combined tactical/narrative play

**Social Commentary Mechanics**: Systems encouraging examining societal issues

## Cultural Impact

Influenced social sci-fi gaming, collaborative worldbuilding matrices, tactical-narrative hybrids. Demonstrated games could pursue literary sci-fi's thematic depth.
