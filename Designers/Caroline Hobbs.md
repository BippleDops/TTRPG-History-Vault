# Caroline Hobbs

**Active Years**: 2010-present
**Birth Year**: ~1983
**Nationality**: United States

Caroline Hobbs designed Downfall (2012), GMless game where players collaboratively create civilization, define its three Pillars (core values), then systematically destroy those Pillars through play chronicling inevitable collapse, demonstrating how mechanics could model societal breakdown while creating tragic narratives examining how civilizations destroy themselves through their own values corrupted or taken to extremes, influencing civilization-building/collapse games and showing how predetermined doom could enhance rather than limit GMless collaborative storytelling.

## Major Work: Downfall (2012)

**Pillar Mechanics**: Three Pillars tracking civilization integrity, damaged through play until collapse

**Collaborative Creation**: Group builds civilization from scratch

**Inevitable Doom**: Mechanics guarantee collapse—question is how

**GMless**: Pure collaborative without facilitator

**Impact**: Influenced civilization collapse gaming. Demonstrated societies-as-characters viable. Pillar mechanics appeared in various forms.

## Design Philosophy

**Civilization Focus**: Societies not just individuals as protagonists

**Tragic Inevitability**: Predetermined endings enhancing drama

**Collaborative Tragedy**: Group-created doomed civilizations

**Pillar Tracking**: Core values as mechanical elements

**GMless Structure**: Authority distribution through framework

## Innovation

**Pillar Mechanics**: Society values as degrading tracks

**Civilization Collapse**: Systematic societal breakdown mechanics

**Collaborative Doom**: Group building and destroying together

## Cultural Impact

Influenced civilization-collapse gaming. Demonstrated societies could be game protagonists. Pillar tracking appeared in other designs.

## Reception

Praised for modeling societal dynamics, collaborative creation, inevitable tragedy. Some wanted more mechanical depth. Used in some educational contexts discussing civilization decline.
