# Jared Sorensen

**Active Years**: 1999-present
**Birth Year**: ~1973
**Nationality**: United States

Jared Sorensen stands as indie RPG's most playfully experimental designer, creating InSpectres (Ghostbusters franchise comedy), Lacuna Part 1 (Matrix-meets-Company surreal mystery), octaNe (post-apocalyptic gonzo), and Parsely Games (parser-based text adventure RPGs)—demonstrating indie games could be fun, funny, and accessible while mechanically innovative, influencing how designers think about genre emulation, comedy gaming, and experimental mechanics serving entertainment over theoretical demonstration.

## Major Works

### InSpectres (2002)

Ghostbusters franchise simulator where players run supernatural investigation business told through mockumentary format with confessionals, using success-level narrative control and franchise mechanics.

**Innovation**: Confessional device, mockumentary framing, success-driven narration, franchise simulation

**Impact**: Demonstrated comedy was legitimate indie genre. Showed narrative mechanics could be lighthearted and fun.

### Lacuna Part 1 (2004)

Mystery agents entering mysterious urban mindscape hunting personality fragments, with reality-questioning surreal atmosphere, static penalty accumulating toward breakdown, and sparse oppressive presentation.

**Innovation**: Static accumulation, reality-questioning mechanics, oppressive minimalism

**Influence**: Showed how presentation style (sparse text, clinical tone) enhances theme

### octaNe (2004)

Over-the-top post-apocalyptic gonzo where everything is ridiculous and awesome, embracing absurdist action.

**Innovation**: Gonzo gaming, embracing ridiculousness mechanically

### Parsely Games (2009)

Text parser adventure games at tabletop—one player is Parser (computer), others give commands like "GET LAMP", Parser responds according to programmed responses.

**Innovation**: Bringing parser games to tabletop format, performance art gaming

**Reception**: Cult following for bizarre brilliance

## Design Philosophy

Sorensen emphasizes:

**Fun Over Seriousness**: Not all games need heavy themes

**Comedy Legitimacy**: Funny games can be mechanically sophisticated

**Genre Emulation**: Tight mechanics capturing specific genre feels

**Experimental Play**: Willing to try bizarre concepts

**Accessibility**: Making indie games welcoming and entertaining

**Playfulness**: Games can be playful, irreverent, weird

## Innovations

**Confessional Mechanics** (InSpectres): Documentary framing devices

**Success-Driven Narration**: Better rolls give more narrative control

**Static Accumulation** (Lacuna): Stress building toward inevitable breakdown

**Parser Gaming**: Text adventures at tabletop

**Franchise Simulation**: Business management in RPG context

**Gonzo Gaming**: Embracing absurdity mechanically

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated indie games could be funny and fun. Comedy horror validated as genre. Experimental designs encouraged playful approaches. Influenced accessible indie design.

## Reception

Praised for playful innovation, accessibility, fun factor. InSpectres especially successful commercially. Lacuna cult classic among surrealism fans.

## Legacy

InSpectres influenced comedy gaming and confessional mechanics. Parsely Games inspired performance-based gaming. Demonstrated experimental playful indie design viability.
