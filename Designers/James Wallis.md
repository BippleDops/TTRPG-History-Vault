# James Wallis

**Active Years**: 1990-present
**Birth Year**: ~1971
**Nationality**: United Kingdom

James Wallis designed The Extraordinary Adventures of Baron Munchausen (1998—competitive lying storytelling game), Once Upon a Time (1993—fairy tale storytelling card game), and founded Hogshead Publishing (UK indie publisher 1994-2003), demonstrating storytelling games and competitive narrative frameworks could work through elegant card-based systems, influencing UK indie gaming and storytelling card game design.

**Baron Munchausen**: Competitive lying—players tell outrageous stories, others challenge/interrupt

**Once Upon a Time**: Fairy tale storytelling using cards

**Hogshead Publishing**: Important UK indie publisher 1994-2003

**UK Indie**: Prominent British designer and publisher

**Storytelling Focus**: Narrative card games

## Impact

Baron Munchausen became storytelling game classic. Once Upon a Time widely played. Hogshead published important UK titles. Influenced competitive storytelling design.
