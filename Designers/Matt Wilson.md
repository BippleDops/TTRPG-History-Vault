# Matt Wilson

**Active Years**: 2004-2012
**Birth Year**: ~1971
**Nationality**: United States

Matt Wilson created Primetime Adventures (2004)—revolutionary game organizing play as television series with rotating screen presence, fan mail currency, and collaborative series creation—demonstrating how TV structure could organize RPG campaigns more effectively than traditional adventuring frameworks, influencing numerous subsequent designs adopting season structures, spotlight mechanics, and collaborative world-building before later semi-retiring from active design while PTA's influence continued shaping narrative gaming.

## Major Work: Primetime Adventures (2004)

**TV Season Structure**: Campaigns organized as TV seasons with episodes

**Screen Presence**: Rotating spotlight (1-3 per episode) ensuring everyone gets featured episodes

**Fan Mail**: Currency rewarding entertaining play, spendable for narrative control

**Collaborative Series Creation**: Group creates TV show concept before playing

**Issue-Based**: Characters have central dramatic problems driving development

**Winner-Narrates**: High card wins conflict and narrates outcome

**Stakes-Setting**: Explicit pre-conflict negotiation of what's at risk

**Diana Jones Award 2005**: Shared award with Dogs in the Vineyard

**Influence**: Season structures, spotlight rotation, collaborative creation all appeared in subsequent games

## Design Philosophy

Wilson's approach emphasized:

**Television as Framework**: TV narrative structures organizing play

**Ensemble Spotlight**: Rotating focus ensuring everyone shines

**Collaborative Creation**: Group-created settings over GM preparation

**Entertainment Rewards**: Mechanically rewarding fun play

**Stakes Negotiation**: Explicit discussion of conflict outcomes

**Producer Role**: Redefining GM as facilitator not plot-controller

## Innovations

**Screen Presence Rotation**: Mathematical spotlight distribution

**Fan Mail Currency**: Rewarding entertainment mechanically

**Television Season Structure**: Organizing campaigns as series

**Collaborative Series Creation**: Group worldbuilding framework

**Winner-Narrates**: Divorcing mechanical victory from fictional outcome

**Issue-Based Development**: Character problems driving advancement

## Awards

- **Diana Jones Award 2005**: Shared for excellence
- **Indie RPG Award Nominations**
- **Critical Acclaim**: Recognized as innovative design

## Cultural Impact

**Season Structure Adoption**: Numerous games organized around TV seasons

**Spotlight Mechanics**: Screen Presence concept widely adopted

**Collaborative Creation**: Validated group worldbuilding

**Fan Mail Derivatives**: Entertainment-reward currencies proliferated

**Television Framework**: Legitimized using TV conventions for RPG structure

## Legacy

Primetime Adventures influenced season structures, spotlight management, collaborative creation across indie design. While Wilson semi-retired, PTA's innovations continue influencing contemporary games.
