## Overview

Skip (Ralph) Williams is an American game designer from Lake Geneva, Wisconsin. He began working for TSR in 1976 as a part-time clerk in the Dungeon Hobby Shop, directed the Gen Con game fair from 1980 to 1983, and later joined TSR's RPGA staff and design department. From 1987 to 2004 he wrote the "Sage Advice" rules column in *Dragon* magazine. After Wizards of the Coast bought TSR he became a senior designer and, with [[Jonathan Tweet]] and [[Monte Cook]], one of the three core designers of [[Dungeons & Dragons Third Edition (2000)]], taking lead authorship of the 3rd Edition *Monster Manual*. He left Wizards of the Coast in 2002 and continued freelance design and column work afterwards.
