# Tomas Härenstam

**Active Years**: 2010-present
**Birth Year**: ~1972
**Nationality**: Sweden

Tomas Härenstam co-founded Free League Publishing (Fria Ligan) and designed/co-designed Mutant: Year Zero (creating Year Zero Engine), Forbidden Lands (sandbox hex-crawl), Alien RPG (licensed horror), Blade Runner RPG (licensed noir)—establishing Swedish design aesthetics (survival mechanics, hex-crawling, atmospheric horror, beautiful production), creating Year Zero Engine as major system, and demonstrating how thoughtful licensed adaptations could honor source material while providing excellent gameplay.

## Major Works

### Mutant: Year Zero (2014)

Post-apocalyptic survival creating Year Zero Engine:

**Year Zero Engine**: d6 dice pools with push-your-luck mechanics

**Survival Focus**: Resource management, zone exploration, community building

**Ark**: Player community/settlement development

**Zone Exploration**: Hex-crawl through radioactive wasteland

**Mutation Mechanics**: Random powers from radiation

**Impact**: Created engine powering multiple Free League games

### Alien RPG (2019)

Licensed Alien franchise horror using Year Zero:

**Cinematic Mode**: One-shot horror scenarios

**Campaign Mode**: Sustained survival horror

**Stress Mechanics**: Panic and psychological breakdown

**Faithful Adaptation**: Captures Alien films' blue-collar horror perfectly

**Commercial Success**: Strong sales, ENnie Gold 2020

**Impact**: Demonstrated licensed adaptations could excel

### Forbidden Lands (2018)

Sandbox hex-crawl survival fantasy:

**Open-World**: Player-driven exploration

**Survival**: Resource management, hunger, cold

**Stronghold Building**: Settlement construction

**Legends**: Collaborative myth creation

**Impact**: Revitalized hex-crawl gaming

### Blade Runner RPG (2022)

Licensed adaptation of Blade Runner using Year Zero:

**Noir Investigation**: Detective work in dystopian LA

**Humanity Tracking**: What makes us human?

**Year Zero Adaptation**: Engine serving noir investigation

**Faithful**: Captures Blade Runner's atmosphere

## Design Philosophy

Härenstam emphasizes:

**Survival Mechanics**: Resource management and environmental challenges

**Exploration**: Hex-crawling and discovery

**Atmospheric Horror**: Creating dread through mechanics

**Licensed Respect**: Honoring source material in adaptations

**Year Zero Versatility**: One engine for multiple genres

**Swedish Aesthetics**: Distinct design sensibilities

**Beautiful Production**: Matching mechanics with presentation
