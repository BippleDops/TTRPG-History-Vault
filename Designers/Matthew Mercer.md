## Overview

Matthew Mercer is an American voice actor best known in tabletop gaming as the Dungeon Master of [[Critical Role]], the actual-play series that began streaming in 2015 and became one of the most influential shows of the [[The Critical Role Effect (2015-present)|Critical Role era]]. He is a co-founder of Critical Role Productions, whose publishing arm Darrington Press was announced in 2020. His design credits include the Blood Hunter class for [[Dungeons & Dragons Fifth Edition (2014)]], the *Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting* (Green Ronin Publishing, 2017), and *Explorer's Guide to Wildemount* (Wizards of the Coast, 2020).
