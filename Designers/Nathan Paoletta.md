# Nathan Paoletta

**Active Years**: 2008-present
**Birth Year**: ~1982
**Nationality**: United States

Nathan Paoletta designed World Wide Wrestling (2014—PbtA pro wrestling where predetermined outcomes meet improvised drama), Annalise (2010—collaborative vampire horror with Claims/Secrets), and Action Movie World (2016—80s/90s action film gaming), demonstrating genre emulation mastery through mechanics capturing specific media formats (wrestling's worked shoots, horror's revelations, action's escalation), influencing how designers adapt non-traditional genres to tabletop while maintaining authentic genre feel.

## Major Works

### World Wide Wrestling (2014)

PbtA professional wrestling game capturing industry's unique nature:

**Kayfabe**: Wrestling's worked-shoot reality where outcomes are predetermined but execution improvised

**Booking**: Planned match results with spontaneous dramatic moments

**Heat**: Audience engagement and wrestler popularity tracking

**Creative**: Matches as collaborative performance not competitive combat

**Playbooks**: Wrestler archetypes (The Hardcore, The High-Flyer, The Golden Boy, The Veteran, etc.)

**Seasons**: Structured campaign as wrestling promotion's season

**ENnie Success**: Multiple awards establishing WWW as definitive wrestling RPG

**Impact**: Solved wrestling gaming's challenge (how to handle predetermined outcomes in RPG). Demonstrated niche genres viable with right mechanics.

### Annalise (2010)

Collaborative vampire horror where group defines what "vampire" represents:

**Claims and Secrets**: Establishing facts and hiding truths, revealing at dramatic moments

**Flexible Vampire**: Literal monster or metaphor for addiction/abuse/corruption

**Three Act Structure**: Organized progression toward confrontation

**Collaborative Horror**: No single GM; group shapes terror together

**Innovation**: Claims/Secrets influenced revelation mechanics in other games

### Action Movie World (2016)

80s/90s action film PbtA:

**Action Movie Tropes**: Embracing genre conventions (one-liners, explosions, impossible feats)

**Over-the-Top**: Mechanically supporting ridiculous action

**Genre Emulation**: Capturing Schwarzenegger/Stallone/Willis era action

## Design Philosophy

Paoletta emphasizes:

**Genre Emulation**: Mechanics capturing specific media formats authentically

**Niche Gaming**: Pursuing narrow genres with dedicated mechanics

**PbtA Flexibility**: Demonstrating Apocalypse World's range

**Collaborative Creation**: Group-created content and shared authority

**Claims/Secrets**: Revelation timing as mechanical consideration

## Innovation

**Worked-Shoot Mechanics** (WWW): Handling predetermined outcomes in RPG context

**Claims and Secrets** (Annalise): Timed revelation systems

**Heat and Audience** (WWW): Crowd reaction as mechanical element

**Kayfabe Gaming**: Wrestling's unique reality in RPG
