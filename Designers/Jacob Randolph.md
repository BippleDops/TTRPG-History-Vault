# Jacob Randolph

**Active Years**: 2013-present
**Birth Year**: ~1982
**Nationality**: United States

Jacob Randolph designed Fellowship (2015), creating Lord of the Rings-style epic fantasy using PbtA with revolutionary cultural playbooks where each player represents entire people (THE Dwarf, THE Elf, THE Orc) creating that culture collaboratively while Fellowship journey toward confronting Overlord demonstrates how distributed worldbuilding and cultural representation could organize epic fantasy differently from traditional approaches.

## Major Work: Fellowship (2015)

Epic fantasy PbtA with cultural playbooks:

**Cultural Playbooks**: Represent entire cultures, not just individuals

**Collaborative Creation**: Each player builds their culture's homeland, values, abilities

**Overlord**: Shared antagonist threatening all peoples

**Epic Journey**: Structured quest across player-created world

**Second Edition**: Refined mechanics and expanded options

**Impact**: Demonstrated collaborative epic fantasy viability. Cultural playbooks influenced diversity representation in gaming.

## Design Philosophy

Randolph emphasizes:

**Cultural Representation**: Playing culture-bearers rather than isolated individuals

**Collaborative Worldbuilding**: Group creating setting through character creation

**Epic Fantasy**: Lord of the Rings style quests with narrative mechanics

**Diversity**: Different cultures cooperating while maintaining distinct identities

## Innovation

**Cultural Playbooks**: Revolutionary approach to representing diversity

**Overlord Mechanics**: Shared antagonist with mechanical progression

**Journey Structure**: Epic quest framework for PbtA

**Homeland Creation**: Collaborative cultural development

## Cultural Impact

Cultural playbooks influenced diversity representation mechanics. Demonstrated epic fantasy worked with PbtA. Collaborative worldbuilding became standard approach. Second edition showed iterative design improvement.

## Reception

Praised for cultural playbooks, collaborative creation, epic scope. Some found cultural representation potentially reductive. Popular for epic fantasy using narrative mechanics.

## Legacy

Cultural playbooks influenced how games represent diversity. Epic fantasy PbtA validated. Collaborative worldbuilding standards raised. Fellowship second edition continued refinement.
