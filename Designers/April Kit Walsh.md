# April Kit Walsh

**Active Years**: 2019-present
**Birth Year**: ~1987
**Nationality**: United States

April Kit Walsh created Thirsty Sword Lesbians (2021)—unapologetically queer PbtA swashbuckling where lesbian/sapphic protagonists fight patriarchy through swordplay and flirting—achieving mainstream success through Evil Hat backing, demonstrating explicitly queer-centered games could succeed commercially beyond niche audiences, winning ENnie Awards and establishing "sword-sapphic" as recognized subgenre while contributing to queer gaming's renaissance.

## Major Work: Thirsty Sword Lesbians (2021)

**Queer Centering**: Lesbian/sapphic protagonists not as representation but as actual focus

**Swashbuckling**: Sword-fighting adventure with romantic elements

**Fight the Patriarchy**: Literally—many antagonists represent oppressive systems

**Playbooks**: Ten archetypes—The Beast, The Chosen, The Devoted, The Infamous, The Kinetic, The Nature Witch, The Scoundrel, The Seeker, The Spooky Witch, The Trickster

**Flirting Mechanics**: Romance mechanically supported alongside combat

**Joyful Tone**: Queer joy and community emphasized despite serious themes

**Evil Hat Success**: Major publisher backing brought mainstream visibility

**Commercial Breakthrough**: Strong sales demonstrated queer-centered viability

**ENnie Awards**: Won Best Game and other categories

**Cultural Impact**: Viral title, mainstream coverage, queer gaming milestone

## Design Philosophy

Walsh emphasizes:

**Queer Centering**: LGBTQ+ as default not exceptional

**Joyful Representation**: Celebration not just struggle

**Mainstream Accessibility**: Bringing queer games to wider audiences

**Sword and Romance**: Balancing action and emotional content

**Fighting Oppression**: Literal and thematic resistance to patriarchy

## Innovation

**Sword-Sapphic Genre**: Essentially created/defined subgenre

**Mainstream Queer Success**: Demonstrated commercial viability

**Dual Focus**: Combat and romance equally mechanically supported

## Awards

## Cultural Impact

**Mainstream Queer Gaming**: Evil Hat's support brought explicit queer content to mainstream

**Viral Success**: Title became meme driving awareness

**Market Validation**: Showed queer-centered games commercially viable

**Sword-Sapphic Genre**: Established recognized subgenre

**She-Ra Synergy**: Released during animated series popularity

## Reception

Praised for joyful queer celebration, fun swashbuckling, romantic mechanics, beautiful production. Title sparked brief controversy (some found "thirsty" inappropriate) but gameplay won over critics. Strong commercial and critical success.

## Legacy

Demonstrated major publishers would back explicitly queer games. Proved queer centering commercially viable. Influenced queer game design. Established sword-sapphic subgenre. Contributed to LGBTQ+ gaming normalization.
