# Greg Costikyan

**Active Years**: 1982-present
**Birth Year**: 1959
**Nationality**: United States

Greg Costikyan co-designed Paranoia (1984—dystopian computer-controlled satire), Toon (1984—cartoon comedy), contributed to Star Wars d6, and designed Violence (1999—metagame about game design), demonstrating comedy RPGs could be mechanically sophisticated while pursuing experimental designs and game theory analysis, influencing comedy gaming and design criticism through both games and extensive writing about game design philosophy.

## Major Works

**Paranoia** (1984): Dystopian satire with Computer controlling clone citizens

**Toon** (1984): Cartoon physics comedy

**Star Wars d6**: Contributions to early success

**Violence** (1999): Experimental metagame

**Game Theory**: Extensive writing on design

## Design Philosophy

Comedy gaming viability, satire through mechanics, experimental approaches, game design analysis and criticism.

## Cultural Impact

Paranoia became comedy RPG classic. Toon demonstrated cartoon gaming. Design writing influenced theory discussions. Experimental approaches pushed boundaries.
