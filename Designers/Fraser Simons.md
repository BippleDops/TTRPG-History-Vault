# Fraser Simons

**Active Years**: 2014-present
**Birth Year**: ~1980
**Nationality**: Canada

Fraser Simons revolutionized cyberpunk gaming with The Veil (2016), replacing traditional stats with emotional states (Mad, Peaceful, Joyful, Scared, Sad, Powerful) and centering transhumanist questions (consciousness, identity, reality) over typical cyberpunk action, demonstrating how PbtA could serve philosophical sci-fi while emotional stats created different character experience—you roll using current emotional state rather than fixed attributes, making psychology central to mechanics.

## Major Works

### The Veil (2016)

Cyberpunk PbtA with revolutionary emotional state stats:

**Emotional Stats**: Stats are emotions—Mad, Peaceful, Joyful, Scared, Sad, Powerful

**State Shifting**: Emotions change based on experiences

**The Veil**: Neural interface overlay blurring physical/digital

**Mindscape**: Mental/digital spaces characters navigate

**Transhumanist Focus**: Identity, consciousness, humanity questions

**Innovation**: Using emotions as stats influenced psychological mechanics widely

**Impact**: Demonstrated cyberpunk PbtA viability, transhumanist themes in gaming

### The Veil: Cascade (2019)

Expanded setting and mechanics exploring more transhumanist territory.

### The Veil: Inheritance (2020)

Further development of Veil universe and mechanics.

## Design Philosophy

Simons emphasizes:

**Emotional Stats**: Using feelings as character statistics

**Transhumanism**: Identity, consciousness, reality questions

**Mindscape Gaming**: Digital/mental space exploration

**Cyberpunk Reinvention**: Beyond typical combat/hacking focus

**Philosophical Depth**: Using mechanics for philosophical inquiry

**PbtA Innovation**: Pushing Apocalypse World framework boundaries

## Innovations

**Emotional State Stats**: Revolutionary approach to character statistics

**State Dynamics**: Emotions changing through play

**Mindscape Mechanics**: Digital/mental space navigation systems

**Transhuman PbtA**: First major transhumanist PbtA game

**Giri System**: Japanese-inspired obligation mechanics

## Cultural Impact

**Emotional Stats Influence**: Other games adopted emotional/psychological stats

**Cyberpunk PbtA**: Demonstrated genre worked with Apocalypse World engine

**Transhumanism Gaming**: Brought philosophical sci-fi to narrative gaming

**Canadian Indie**: Prominent Canadian indie designer

## Reception

Praised for emotional stat innovation, transhumanist depth, mindscape mechanics. Some found emotional stats confusing initially. Strong following among cyberpunk and philosophical gaming fans.

## Legacy

Emotional stats influenced psychological character design. Demonstrated cyberpunk PbtA distinct from combat-heavy alternatives. Transhumanist gaming gained sophisticated narrative option. The Veil trilogy established setting continuity.
