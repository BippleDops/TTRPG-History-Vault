# Erick Wujcik

**Active Years**: 1985-2008
**Birth Year**: 1951
**Death Year**: 2008
**Nationality**: United States

Erick Wujcik designed Amber Diceless RPG (1991—based on Roger Zelazny novels, eliminating dice for GM-adjudicated resolution emphasizing player skill and cosmic-scope intrigue) and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles RPG (1985—first TMNT game before franchise exploded), demonstrating diceless play viability and creating games emphasizing player creativity over randomness, influencing diceless design and showing how licensed properties could launch franchises before mainstream media success, leaving lasting legacy through Amber's continued influence despite his 2008 death.

## Major Works

### Amber Diceless RPG (1991)

Revolutionary diceless game based on Zelazny's Chronicles of Amber:

**No Dice**: Pure GM adjudication based on stats and fictional positioning

**Auction System**: Character creation through bidding points for rankings

**Cosmic Scope**: Playing Amberites (reality-walking demigods)

**Player Skill**: Success determined by clever play not random rolls

**Pattern/Logrus/Trump**: Magic systems from novels

**Intrigue Focus**: Political maneuvering among immortal relatives

**Impact**: Proved diceless could work. Influenced narrative gaming. Became cult classic.

**Reception**: Devoted following. Some loved freedom from randomness; others missed dice uncertainty.

### Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles & Other Strangeness (1985)

First TMNT RPG before franchise mainstream explosion:

**Early License**: Secured rights before cartoon/toys made TMNT phenomenon

**Palladium System**: Using Palladium's house engine

**Mutation Creation**: Random animal mutation tables

**Pre-Franchise Success**: Game helped build TMNT awareness

**Impact**: Demonstrated getting licenses early could pay off

## Design Philosophy

Wujcik emphasized:

**Diceless Viability**: Removing randomness focusing on player skill

**Cosmic Scope**: Amber's reality-walking ambition

**Player Creativity**: Rewarding clever solutions over lucky rolls

**Licensed Adaptation**: Respectful source material treatment

**Intrigue Over Combat**: Political maneuvering emphasis

## Innovation

**Diceless Play**: Amber proved dice weren't necessary

**Auction Character Creation**: Bidding for stat rankings

**Cosmic-Scale Gaming**: Reality manipulation and multiversal scope

**Early Licensing**: TMNT before mainstream success

## Awards

## Cultural Impact

**Diceless Gaming**: Amber demonstrated viability, influenced Lords of Gossamer & Shadow and other diceless games

**TMNT Launch**: Early RPG helped franchise awareness

**Player Skill Focus**: Influenced narrative gaming's de-emphasis of randomness

**Cosmic Gaming**: Amber's scope influenced reality-manipulation gaming

## Death and Legacy

Wujcik died 2008 from cancer. Amber continues through community and spiritual successors (Lords of Gossamer & Shadow). TMNT RPG remembered as franchise cornerstone despite later editions.
