# Andrew Gillis

**Active Years**: 2018-present
**Birth Year**: ~1983
**Nationality**: United States

Andrew Gillis designed Girl by Moonlight (2022), bringing magical girl anime to PbtA with four complete settings (String of Pearls street-level, Alien Neon Babylon cyberpunk, Grand Season epic fantasy, Thousand Arrows wuxia), demonstrating how single game could provide multiple magical girl variations while Shadow corruption mechanics created Madoka Magica-style tragic trajectories, successfully adapting anime genre to Western tabletop through Evil Hat backing.

## Major Work: Girl by Moonlight (2022)

Magical girl PbtA with four complete settings:

**Four Settings**: 
- **String of Pearls**: Contemporary magical girls
- **Alien Neon Babylon**: Cyberpunk noir magical girls  
- **Grand Season**: Epic fantasy chosen heroes
- **Thousand Arrows**: Wuxia martial heroes

**Transformation Mechanics**: Magical girl transformation sequences

**Shadow Corruption**: Gradual corruption from magical duties toward becoming villain

**Mundane/Magical Balance**: School/life alongside heroics

**Tragic Potential**: Madoka-inspired possibility of corruption and doom

**Evil Hat Production**: Major publisher backing beautiful production

**Impact**: Brought magical girl anime respectfully to Western gaming

## Design Philosophy

Gillis emphasizes:

**Anime Adaptation**: Faithful genre translation

**Multiple Settings**: Providing variety within magical girl framework

**Transformation Sequences**: Mechanically representing anime conventions

**Tragic Potential**: Madoka-style darkness alongside traditional optimism

**Beautiful Production**: Matching anime aesthetics

## Innovation

**Four Settings in One**: Complete distinct magical girl variations

**Shadow Corruption**: Magical duties corrupting toward villainy

**Anime Conventions**: Transformation, friendship power, gradual darkness

## Cultural Impact

Brought magical girl anime to Western PbtA. Demonstrated anime genres viable in tabletop. Four settings showed value diversity. Evil Hat backing validated anime gaming mainstream potential.

## Reception

Praised for faithful magical girl adaptation, four distinct settings, Shadow mechanics. Magical girl fans appreciated respectful treatment. Some found niche but enthusiastic following embraced it.

## Legacy

Validated magical girl as tabletop genre. Demonstrated anime adaptations could work. Four-settings model showed value in comprehensive packages.
