# Andrew Medeiros

**Active Years**: 2013-present
**Birth Year**: ~1982
**Nationality**: Canada

Andrew Medeiros co-designed Urban Shadows (2015) with Mark Diaz Truman, creating urban fantasy PbtA where Debt mechanics (favors owed/held creating political leverage) revolutionized how games handled influence and obligations, demonstrating PbtA worked for political supernatural drama with factional play, helping establish Magpie Games as major publisher while proving Canadian indie designers could compete internationally.

## Major Work: Urban Shadows (2015)

Co-designed urban fantasy political PbtA featuring:

**Debt Innovation**: Revolutionary mechanic where characters owe/hold Debts (favors), creating political web of obligations more interesting than simple money

**Four Factions**: Night, Power, Wild, Mortality—tracking relationships and power struggles

**Political Focus**: Supernatural politics over monster-hunting action

**Corruption**: Gradual transformation through moral compromises

**ENnie Success**: Multiple awards establishing Urban Shadows as major PbtA game

**Debt Legacy**: Debt mechanics appeared in numerous subsequent designs as template for handling social/political influence

## Design Philosophy

Medeiros emphasizes:

**Political Intrigue**: Faction dynamics and power struggles

**Urban Fantasy**: Contemporary supernatural settings

**Debt as Leverage**: Social obligations mechanically tracked

**Collaborative Montreal Design Scene**: Canadian indie presence

## Innovation

**Debt Mechanics**: Favors as currency and political leverage

**Faction Integration**: Four-faction structure with mechanical significance

**Urban Shadow Generation**: Collaborative city creation

## Cultural Impact

Debt mechanics widely adopted. Urban fantasy PbtA established. Canadian indie gaming represented. Magpie Games partnership successful.
