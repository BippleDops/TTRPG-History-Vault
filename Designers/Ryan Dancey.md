## Overview

Ryan S. Dancey is an American games-industry businessman. With Five Rings Publishing partner Bob Abramowitz he helped negotiate the 1997 sale of the near-bankrupt [[TSR]] to [[Wizards of the Coast]], after which he became business head of Wizards' roleplaying department and was involved in the development of [[Dungeons & Dragons Third Edition (2000)]]. Dancey largely conceived the [[Release of Open Gaming License (2000)|Open Game License]] and the d20 System Trademark License, arguing that D&D's strength lay in its community of players and that TSR's aggressive policing of fan material had alienated them. He left Wizards of the Coast at the end of 2002 and later worked in online games and consulting.
