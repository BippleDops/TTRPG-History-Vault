# Joe Mcdaldno

**Active Years**: 2014-present
**Birth Year**: ~1988
**Nationality**: United States

Joe Mcdaldno designed Pigsmoke (2014) and Before the Storm (2016), creating intimate minimalist games focusing on small-scale relationship dynamics and personal stories, demonstrating how ultra-minimal rules could enable deep character interactions, influencing indie gaming's relationship-focused design while maintaining low profile despite quality work appreciated by story games community.

# Joe Mcdaldno

**Active Years**: 2012-present
**Birth Year**: ~1985
**Nationality**: United States

Joe Mcdaldno designed Before the Storm (2018—community facing apocalypse preparing for end), Pigsmoke (2015—small-town corruption), and contributed to The Quiet Year, creating melancholic games examining communities under pressure, isolation, and how people face doom together, demonstrating minimalist mechanics could create profound emotional experiences exploring community dynamics and inevitable tragedy.

## Works

**Before the Storm** (2018): Community preparing for apocalypse

**Pigsmoke** (2015): Small-town corruption and decay  

**Contributions**: Various community and collaborative games

## Design Philosophy

Emphasizes melancholic tone, community under pressure, isolation themes, minimalist emotional mechanics.
