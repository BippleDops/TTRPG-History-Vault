# Sage LaTorra

**Active Years**: 2010-present
**Birth Year**: ~1985
**Nationality**: United States

Sage LaTorra co-designed Dungeon World (2012) with Adam Koebel, adapting Powered by the Apocalypse for traditional dungeon-crawling fantasy, demonstrating narrative mechanics could serve classic adventuring through fiction-first resolution, collaborative world-building, and Bonds (party relationship mechanics), becoming one of PbtA's biggest commercial successes and introducing thousands to narrative gaming through familiar D&D-style play.

## Major Work: Dungeon World (2012)

Co-designed breakthrough PbtA fantasy with Adam Koebel:

**PbtA Dungeon-Crawling**: Apocalypse World mechanics serving traditional fantasy adventuring

**Fiction-First**: Describe actions based on fiction; mechanics follow from fictional positioning

**Playbooks**: Classic classes (Fighter, Wizard, Thief, Cleric, etc.) as PbtA playbooks

**Bonds**: Relationship mechanics between party members

**Collaborative Dungeon Creation**: GM improvises using player input

**Spout Lore**: Players can establish facts through successful rolls

**Fronts**: GM threat-tracking borrowed from Apocalypse World

**Massive Success**: Sold exceptionally well, introduced many to PbtA

**Impact**: Proved PbtA could serve traditional fantasy. Became gateway to narrative gaming for D&D players.

## Design Philosophy

LaTorra emphasizes:

**Fiction-First Resolution**: Fictional positioning determines mechanical approach

**Collaborative Play**: Players contributing to world-building

**Accessibility**: Making PbtA approachable for traditional gamers

**D&D Alternative**: Familiar fantasy with modern mechanics

## Innovation

**PbtA Fantasy**: First major dungeon-crawling PbtA

**Bonds System**: Party relationship mechanics in PbtA context

**Spout Lore**: Player fact-establishment through knowledge rolls

**Fiction-First Dungeoneering**: Narrative approach to classic adventuring

## Awards

- **ENnie Awards**: Multiple categories
- **Golden Geek Awards**: Best RPG
- **Commercial Success**: Top-selling PbtA game

## Cultural Impact

**Gateway Game**: Introduced thousands to PbtA through familiar fantasy

**Commercial Validation**: Proved PbtA commercially viable beyond indie niche

**D&D Alternative**: Provided narrative option for fantasy gaming

**Bonds Proliferation**: Relationship mechanics appeared in many PbtA games

## Reception

Praised for accessible PbtA introduction, traditional fantasy with narrative mechanics, commercial success. Some purists wanted more D&D complexity; others wanted purer narrativism. Widely successful despite/because of middle-ground approach.

## Legacy

Dungeon World became PbtA's commercial flagship. Gateway for traditional gamers to narrative mechanics. Demonstrated PbtA worked for classic fantasy. Bonds influenced party relationship designs.
