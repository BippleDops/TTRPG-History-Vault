# Willem Larsen

**Active Years**: 2005-2015
**Birth Year**: ~1975
**Nationality**: United States

Willem Larsen designed Capes (2005), competitive superhero RPG where players create conflicts for each other earning resources through generating interesting complications, demonstrating player-vs-player narrative competition could work collaboratively through mechanical framework rewarding creating drama for others, influencing competitive-cooperative design thinking though remaining niche due to unusual approach requiring full group buy-in.

## Major Work: Capes (2005)

**Competitive Superhero Gaming**: Players compete to narrate outcomes while creating conflicts for each other

**Conflict Creation**: Earn resources by generating interesting complications

**No GM**: Purely player-driven

**Revolutionary**: Competitive narrative framework

**Niche**: Required specific group dynamics

**Impact**: Demonstrated competitive-narrative viability, influenced mixed-motive design thinking

## Design Philosophy

**Competition Serves Story**: Players opposing creates better narratives

**Resource Economy**: Earning through creating complications

**No GM**: Pure player competition

**Superhero Genre**: Comic book dramatic conflicts

## Innovation

**Competitive Narrative**: Player-vs-player within collaborative framework

**Conflict Generation Rewards**: Mechanically incentivizing creating problems

**GMless Competition**: Authority through competitive play

## Reception

Praised for innovative approach. Niche appeal—requires group fully embracing competition. Influential on designers more than widely played.

## Legacy

Influenced competitive-cooperative thinking. Demonstrated player competition could serve narrative. Niche but respected innovative design.
