# Meguey Baker

**Active Years**: 2004-present
**Birth Year**: 1971
**Nationality**: United States

Meguey Baker stands as one of indie RPG's most thoughtful and influential designers, co-creating Apocalypse World (with Vincent Baker), designing 1001 Nights (storytelling Arabian Nights game), Psi*Run (amnesiac psychic fugitive game), and Mobile Frame Zero: Firebrands (mecha romance), while pioneering safety tools and consent practices in gaming, advocating for diverse narratives and marginalized perspectives, influencing how designers think about collaboration, safety, and creating welcoming inclusive gaming spaces.

## Early Career and Partnership

Baker emerged from The Forge community alongside her partner Vincent Baker, both pursuing narrativist design but with complementary approaches—Vincent focused on mechanical elegance, Meguey on social dynamics, collaboration, and inclusive design.

Her design philosophy emphasizes:
- Safety and consent as foundational
- Diverse perspectives and stories
- Collaborative rather than competitive play
- Mechanics supporting emotional and social territory
- Making gaming welcoming to marginalized communities

## Major Works

### Apocalypse World (2010, co-designer with Vincent Baker)

While often credited primarily to Vincent, Meguey co-designed Apocalypse World, particularly contributing to:
- Sex moves and relationship mechanics
- Safety and consent considerations
- Social dynamics and community aspects
- Playbook diversity and inclusive options

Her influence shaped AW's groundbreaking approach to relationships, sex, and social play.

### 1001 Nights (2006)

Baker's solo masterpiece, 1001 Nights creates storytelling game inspired by Arabian Nights where players portray courtiers telling stories to Sultan, using nested narratives (stories within stories) to pursue political agendas, with brilliant mechanic where story content indirectly advances political goals rather than direct advocacy, creating multi-layered narrative examining power, storytelling, and indirect influence.

**Key Innovation**: Nested narratives where fictional stories comment on/influence courtly politics

**Arabian Nights**: Drawing on Scheherazade tradition of stories-within-stories

**Political Intrigue**: Indirect pursuit of power through storytelling

**Critical Acclaim**: Won ENnie for Best Game

### Psi*Run (2012)

Amnesiac psychic fugitives fleeing mysterious Chasers while slowly recovering memories about who they are and why they're being hunted, using innovative resolution where failing rolls lets you answer questions about your character (recovering memories) while succeeding moves you toward safety but maintains amnesia—creating tension between learning identity and achieving safety.

**Memory Recovery**: Brilliant mechanic where failure reveals character background

**Fugitive Narrative**: Constant flight creating tension

**Question-Based Character Creation**: You don't know who you are; discover through play

**Success/Failure Trade-off**: Succeed and escape but remain ignorant, or fail and learn but face danger

### Mobile Frame Zero: Firebrands (2017)

Mecha romance game using minigames to resolve different interaction types—piloting mecha, intimate conversations, vulnerable moments—creating diverse gameplay and emotional range while maintaining fast pacing.

**Minigame Structure**: Different conflict types use different specialized resolution systems

**Mecha Romance**: Balancing giant robot battles with interpersonal relationships

**Emotional Range**: Mechanics for tenderness alongside violence

## Design Philosophy

Baker's approach emphasizes:

**Safety and Consent**: Pioneering safety tools (Same Page Tool, Stars & Wishes) and consent practices

**Inclusive Design**: Creating space for marginalized identities and diverse narratives

**Collaborative Over Competitive**: Encouraging cooperation rather than opposition

**Emotional and Social Mechanics**: Systems for relationships, feelings, social dynamics

**Diverse Representation**: Including options beyond dominant narratives

**Community Care**: Building welcoming gaming communities

**Education**: Teaching about safety and consent in gaming

## Innovations and Contributions

**Safety Tools**:
- **Same Page Tool**: Pre-game discussion framework establishing expectations
- **Stars and Wishes**: Post-game reflection on what worked (stars) and what to change (wishes)
- **Consent Practices**: Advocating for explicit consent in gaming scenarios

**Mechanical Innovations**:
- **Nested Narratives** (1001 Nights): Stories-within-stories affecting politics
- **Failure Reveals Character** (Psi*Run): Learning through failing
- **Minigame Structures** (Firebrands): Specialized systems for different interaction types

**Community Contributions**:
- **Education**: Teaching safety and consent widely
- **Advocacy**: Supporting marginalized designers and players
- **Mentorship**: Helping emerging designers, especially women and queer creators
