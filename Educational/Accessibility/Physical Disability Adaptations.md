# Physical Disability Adaptations

**Category**: Accessibility
**Era**: 2000s-present

## Overview

Physical Disability Adaptations enable players with mobility limitations, motor control challenges, or other physical disabilities to participate through ergonomic seating, adaptive dice-rolling aids, digital tools reducing physical manipulation, VTT accessibility, and flexible play structures accommodating energy/pain limitations, demonstrating tabletop gaming accessible when groups accommodate physical needs and designers consider ergonomics, with growing awareness that universal design principles benefit all players not just those with disabilities.

**Adaptations Include**:
- Dice towers/rollers for limited dexterity
- Digital dice for motor control challenges
- Ergonomic seating and table height
- Reduced physical manipulation through VTTs
- Flexible session length for energy/pain
- Adaptive equipment and aids

**Universal Design**: Benefiting all players through accessibility thinking

**Community**: Physically disabled gamers sharing solutions

**Technology**: Digital tools enabling participation

**Awareness**: Growing designer consideration

## Common Adaptations

### Dice Rolling

**Dice Towers**: Easier than hand-rolling

**Digital Dice**: Apps/VTTs for motor control challenges

**Dice Trays**: Contained rolling

**Automated Rollers**: Mechanical or electronic

**Large Dice**: Easier to grasp/read

### Physical Setup

**Ergonomic Seating**: Accommodating mobility needs

**Table Height**: Wheelchair accessibility

**Reach Considerations**: Materials within comfortable range

**Breaks**: Frequent for pain/energy management

**Remote Options**: Online reducing travel/seating issues

### Digital Solutions

**VTTs**: Reducing physical manipulation

**Character Sheet Apps**: Digital vs. paper

**Voice Controls**: For limited motor control

**Accessibility Features**: VTT keyboard navigation, shortcuts

### Session Structure

**Flexible Length**: Accommodating energy/pain limitations

**Frequent Breaks**: Managing physical needs

**Remote Options**: When in-person challenging

**Asynchronous**: Play-by-post for energy management
