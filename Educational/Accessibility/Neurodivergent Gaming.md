# Neurodivergent Gaming

**Category**: Accessibility
**Era**: 2010s-present

## Overview

Neurodivergent Gaming addresses accommodations for ADHD, autistic, and other neurodivergent players through sensory considerations (lighting, noise), social structure (clear expectations, explicit communication), executive function supports (written procedures, timers, organized materials), and recognition that TTRPGs can be simultaneously challenging and therapeutic for neurodivergent individuals, with growing awareness that designing for neurodivergent access creates better games for everyone through clarity, structure, and explicit communication.

**Neurodivergent Includes**:
- ADHD (attention, executive function)
- Autism (social communication, sensory)
- Dyslexia (reading/writing)
- Other cognitive differences

**Double-Edged**: RPGs can be challenging (social demands, sensory input) AND beneficial (structured social practice, special interest engagement, creative outlet)

**Accommodations**:
- Sensory: Lighting, noise control, fidgets
- Social: Clear expectations, explicit communication
- Executive Function: Organization, timers, procedures
- Processing: Time for responses, written backup

**Growing Awareness**: Neurodivergent perspectives increasingly considered

## Common Accommodations

### Sensory

**Lighting**: Avoiding harsh/flickering lights

**Noise**: Managing volume, background sounds

**Fidgets**: Allowing stim toys, doodling

**Breaks**: Sensory regulation time

**Environment**: Comfortable physical space

### Social Communication

**Explicit Expectations**: Clear social rules

**Written**: Backup written communication

**Processing Time**: Not rushing responses

**Literal Language**: Reducing ambiguity/sarcasm without clarification

**Social Scripts**: Providing structure for social interaction

### Executive Function

**Organization**: Helping materials management

**Timers**: Managing session length and turns

**Written Procedures**: Clear step-by-step processes

**Reminders**: For tracking details

**Structure**: Predictable formats

### Gameplay

**Session Length**: Shorter sessions for attention/energy

**Clear Structure**: Predictable session format

**Processing Time**: Allowing thinking time

**Special Interests**: Engaging with focused topics

**Rules Clarity**: Explicit unambiguous rules

## Therapeutic Benefits

Many neurodivergent individuals find RPGs therapeutic:

**Social Skills Practice**: Structured social interaction

**Executive Function**: Planning and decision-making practice

**Creative Expression**: Safe imaginative outlet

**Special Interests**: Deep engagement with topics

**Community**: Finding accepting social groups

**Game to Grow**: Organization using RPGs therapeutically for neurodivergent youth

## Challenges

**Social Demands**: Reading social cues, group dynamics

**Sensory Overload**: Noise, lighting, crowding

**Executive Function**: Tracking details, time management

**Processing Speed**: Keeping up with fast-paced play

**Ambiguity**: Unclear rules/expectations

**Masking**: Effort of appearing neurotypical

## Solutions

**Communication**: Clear explicit expectations

**Environment**: Sensory-friendly spaces

**Structure**: Predictable formats

**Patience**: Accommodating processing needs

**Written**: Backup documentation

**Flexibility**: Adapting to individual needs

## Cultural Impact

**Awareness**: Neurodivergent gaming increasingly recognized

**Therapeutic Use**: RPGs in therapy and social skills groups

**Universal Design**: Accommodations helping everyone

## Best Practices

**For Designers**:
- Clear unambiguous rules
- Structured procedures
- Sensory considerations
- Processing time built-in
- Test with neurodivergent players

**For GMs/Groups**:
- Establish clear expectations
- Sensory-friendly environment
- Allow fidgets/stims
- Processing time
- Written backup
- Explicit communication
- Breaks as needed
- Patience and understanding

**For Players**:
- Communicate needs
- Request accommodations
- Share strategies
- Advocate for inclusion
