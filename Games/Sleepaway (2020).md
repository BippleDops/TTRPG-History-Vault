# Sleepaway

**Publisher**: Possum Creek Games
**Designer**: Jay Dragon
**Year**: 2020
**System**: Belonging Outside Belonging
**Genre**: horror, queer, summer-camp, coming-of-age
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Sleepaway, designed by Jay Dragon in 2020, creates queer trans horror at summer camp using Belonging Outside Belonging system (No Dice, No Masters—diceless GMless framework), where players portray camp counselors with playbooks ("The Quiet One," "The Visionary," "The Neighbor") navigating queer/trans identities, summer camp dynamics, and encroaching supernatural horror through structured moves and tokens, creating coming-of-age stories blending queer joy, community, and scary monsters.

**Queer Trans Centering**: Explicitly designed for/about queer and trans experiences, making space for narratives typically marginalized.

**Belonging Outside Belonging**: Uses Avery Alder's diceless GMless framework where players use Weak Moves freely but Strong Moves cost tokens, creating self-regulating economy.

**Summer Camp Horror**: Blends nostalgia, coming-of-age, community, and monsters in teen slasher/supernatural tradition.

**Jay Dragon's Breakout**: Established Dragon as major queer indie designer, leading to Wanderhome and other successes.

## Core Mechanics

**No Dice, No Masters**: Diceless GMless collaborative play

**Playbooks**: Character archetypes with specific Weak and Strong Moves

**Token Economy**: Strong Moves cost tokens; Weak Moves generate situations requiring Strong Moves

**Setting Elements**: Collaborative creation of camp, counselors, and horror

**Move Structure**: Specific moves triggering in appropriate fictional circumstances

**Community Tokens**: Shared resources representing collective safety

## Setting and Themes

**Summer Camp**: Nostalgic setting with communal living, activities, wilderness

**Queer Trans Protagonists**: Counselors navigating identity alongside supernatural

**Horror Elements**: Monsters, ghosts, curses threatening safety

**Community**: Finding family and belonging among queer peers

**Coming of Age**: Growing up, self-discovery, facing fears

**Trans Joy and Horror**: Space for trans narratives in horror context

## Cultural Impact

Breakthrough for queer trans gaming. Demonstrated demand for explicitly queer-centered games. Influenced No Dice No Masters proliferation. Established Jay Dragon as major designer. Contributed to queer indie gaming renaissance.

## Reception

Praised for centering queer trans experiences, effective horror blend with community/joy, beautiful production. Became touchstone for queer gaming discussions. Commercial success unusual for explicitly queer niche game.
