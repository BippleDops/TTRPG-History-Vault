# Soth

**Publisher**: C.A. Jarvis
**Designer**: C.A. Jarvis
**Year**: 2020
**System**: Minimalist Horror
**Genre**: horror, minimalist, solo-optional
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Soth, designed by C.A. Jarvis in 2020, delivers ultra-minimalist horror fitting on single page with simple d6 dice pool resolution, doom track advancement toward inevitable bad ending, and solo-or-group flexibility, demonstrating extreme minimalism could create effective horror through elegant death-spiral mechanics and atmospheric prompts, influenced by Trophy Dark but distilled to absolute essentials, showing how much could be achieved with how little while maintaining genre authenticity and emotional impact.

**One-Page**: Complete horror game on single sheet

**d6 Pool Resolution**: Simple dice pools counting successes

**Doom Track**: Inevitable advancement toward bad ending

**Solo-Capable**: Works for single player or group

**Minimalism**: Everything stripped to essentials

**Horror Effective**: Despite simplicity, creates genuine tension

**Impact**: Demonstrated ultra-minimal horror viability. Influenced one-page horror designs.

## Core Mechanics

**Dice Pools**: Roll d6 pools (1-6 dice), count 5-6 as successes

**Doom Track**: Advances toward inevitable doom

**Character**: Minimal definition, focuses on situation

**Resolution**: Success/partial/failure based on success count

**Atmospheric**: Relies on description and situation over complex mechanics

## Setting and Themes

**Variable**: Any horror scenario

**Doom**: Inevitable bad ending creates tension

**Simplicity**: Focus on horror atmosphere over mechanical complexity

**Themes**: Whatever horror scenario demands

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated one-page could deliver effective horror. Influenced minimalist design. Showed Trophy-style doom could be distilled further.

## Reception

Praised for elegant minimalism, effective horror despite simplicity. Some want more mechanical depth. Cult following among minimalist horror fans.
