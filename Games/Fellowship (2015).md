# Fellowship

**Publisher**: Liberi Gothica Games
**Designer**: Jacob Randolph
**Year**: 2015
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: epic-fantasy, fellowship-journey
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Fellowship, designed by Jacob Randolph in 2015, creates Lord of the Rings-style epic quests using PbtA with brilliant twist: each player chooses playbook representing entire culture (Dwarf, Elf, Orc, Heir, Halfling) and creates that culture's details collaboratively, ensuring diverse Fellowship where each member brings unique cultural perspective while the Overlord (antagonist created collaboratively) threatens all peoples, requiring cooperation to overcome shared threat.

**Cultural Playbooks**: Don't play "a dwarf" but "THE Dwarf"—representing entire culture

**Collaborative Worldbuilding**: Each player creates their culture's details (values, homeland, traditions)

**Overlord Creation**: Group designs shared antagonist threatening all

**Epic Journey**: Structured quest toward confronting Overlord

**Cultural Powers**: Each playbook has abilities drawing on cultural archetype

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA 2d6+stat**: Standard resolution

**Cultural Playbooks**: Dwarf, Elf, Orc, Halfling, Heir, etc.—each representing entire people

**Homeland Creation**: Players define their culture's territory, values, abilities

**Fellowship Bonds**: Relationships between diverse cultures

**Overlord**: Shared antagonist with Cuts (wounds to power base) tracked

**Journey Structure**: Epic quest across created world to Overlord's stronghold

**Cultural Moves**: Each playbook has unique moves reflecting their people's nature

## Setting

**Collaboratively Created World**: Group builds fantasy world through culture creation

**Diverse Peoples**: Each Fellowship member's culture adds to world

**Threatened World**: Overlord endangers all, requiring cooperation

**Epic Fantasy**: High stakes, world-threatening dangers, heroic journeys

**Themes**: Unity in diversity, cultural perspectives, cooperation, heroic sacrifice

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated collaborative epic fantasy worked with PbtA. Cultural playbooks influenced how designers thought about representing diversity. Showed Lord of the Rings-style quests viable in narrative framework.

## Reception

Praised for collaborative worldbuilding, cultural playbooks, epic scope. Some found cultural representation reductive. Popular for epic fantasy campaigns using narrative mechanics.
