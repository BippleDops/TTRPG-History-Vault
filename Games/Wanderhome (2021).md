# Wanderhome

**Publisher**: Possum Creek Games
**Designer**: Jay Dragon
**Year**: 2021
**System**: Belonging Outside Belonging
**Genre**: pastoral, cozy, animal-folk
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Wanderhome, designed by Jay Dragon in 2021, creates gentle pastoral fantasy where animal-folk travel through Haeth (recovering from unspecified War) experiencing seasonal journeys, helping communities, forming bonds, and healing—using No Dice No Masters (Belonging Outside Belonging) framework where there's no failure, no combat (fighting is literally impossible), just cozy exploration, emotional connections, and witnessing beauty, becoming surprise hit demonstrating demand for wholesome non-violent gaming and establishing "cozy games" as recognized genre alongside traditional adventure gaming.

**No Failure, No Combat**: Revolutionary for RPGs—you cannot fail rolls (no dice), cannot fight (mechanically impossible)

**Cozy Gaming**: Emphasis on comfort, beauty, gentleness, community

**Seasonal Journey**: Travel through Haeth experiencing seasons changing

**Animal-Folk**: Playing various animals (frog, mouse, bird, possum, etc.)

**Post-War Healing**: The world is recovering from War; you witness and aid healing

**Belonging Outside Belonging**: Avery Alder's diceless GMless framework

## Core Mechanics

**No Dice No Masters**: Diceless GMless collaborative play

**Playbooks**: Nine natures—The Dancer, The Firelight, The Guardian, The Moth-Tender, The Peddler, The Pilgrim, The Poet, The Ragamuffin, The Shepherd

**Weak/Strong Moves**: Weak Moves free and generate situations; Strong Moves cost tokens but resolve situations

**Token Economy**: Self-regulating through move costs

**Place Creation**: Collaborative generation of locations visited

**Seasonal Moves**: Different moves available in different seasons

**Witnessing**: Specific moves for experiencing and recording beauty/kindness

**Kith**: Your animal-folk culture providing specific traits

## Setting

**Haeth**: Pastoral world of animal-folk recovering from War

**The War**: Unspecified recent conflict that scarred world

**Seasons**: Travel through changing seasons, each with distinct feel

**Communities**: Small villages and gatherings needing help/connection

**No Violence**: Fighting doesn't exist in Wanderhome mechanically or narratively

**Themes**: Healing, community, kindness, seasonal cycles, witnessing beauty, found family, recovery

## Cultural Impact

**Cozy Gaming Genre**: Practically created/defined cozy RPG genre

**Commercial Breakthrough**: Massive Kickstarter ($272K on $25K goal), strong retail

**ENnie Gold 2022**: Won Best Game and multiple other awards

**Mainstream Coverage**: Gaming and mainstream press covered as phenomenon

**No Dice No Masters Proliferation**: Triggered wave of Belonging Outside Belonging games

**Wholesome Gaming Movement**: Validated non-violent gentle gaming as legitimate market

**Jay Dragon Stardom**: Established Dragon as major indie designer

**Cultural Moment**: Released during pandemic when cozy escapism deeply resonated

## Reception

Praised for gentle beauty, emotional depth, gorgeous production, successful no-combat framework. Some traditional gamers confused by no-failure/no-combat but cozy gaming fans embraced enthusiastically. Became best-selling indie RPG 2021-2022.

## Legacy

Established cozy gaming as major genre. Demonstrated wholesome non-violent gaming had large audience. No Dice No Masters proliferated. Influenced pastoral and gentle game design. Confirmed Jay Dragon as major talent.
