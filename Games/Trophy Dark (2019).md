# Trophy Dark

**Publisher**: The Gauntlet / Hedgemaze Press
**Designer**: Jesse Ross
**Year**: 2019
**System**: Trophy System
**Genre**: horror, dark-fantasy, doomed-treasure-hunters
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Trophy Dark, designed by Jesse Ross in 2019, creates dark fantasy horror where treasure-hunters venture into haunted forest seeking gold knowing they're doomed—will die or become Lost (corrupted into monsters)—using minimal rules with brilliant Ruin mechanic (corruption track advancing toward inevitable doom) and Hunt Roll (risk-reward system where pushing for success risks Ruin), creating tense death-spirals where every success brings you closer to destruction, influenced by Dark Souls video games and weird horror literature, spawning Trophy Gold (non-doomed variant) and influencing doomed-protagonist design.

**Doomed Certainty**: You WILL die or become Lost. Only question is how much treasure you grab first and how grotesquely you transform.

**Ruin Mechanic**: Track from 1-6. At 6 Ruin, you're Lost (become monster, NPC). Everything advances Ruin—success and failure both corrupt you.

**Hunt Roll**: Push your luck resolution—roll d6 equal to Ruin. If any die shows 6, advance Ruin. Roll more dice to succeed better but risk corruption more.

**Minimal OSR Horror**: Extremely simple rules creating maximum tension through mechanical death-spiral.

## Core Mechanics

**Ruin Track**: 1-6, advancing toward inevitable doom

**Hunt Roll**: Roll d6 equal to current Ruin:
- 1-5: Each die adds to success
- 6: Advance Ruin immediately

**Risk Tokens**: Earned when endangering yourself, spent to add Hunt Roll dice (risking more Ruin)

**Burdens**: Protective equipment/magic that helps but takes inventory slots

**Sets**: Incantations and rituals providing benefits at Ruin costs

**Devil's Bargains**: GM offers benefits for accepting complications/Ruin

## Setting

**The Forest**: Dark haunted woods filled with gold and death

**Treasure-Hunters**: Desperate people seeking fortune despite knowing forest's reputation

**Doomed**: Historical fact—no one returns from forest unchanged or alive

**Themes**: Greed, corruption, body horror, inevitable doom, choosing how you fall

## Cultural Impact

Established Trophy as major horror RPG. Ruin mechanics influenced corruption/doom-track designs. Hunt Roll innovation praised for elegant risk-reward. Led to Trophy Gold (non-doomed variant). Influenced Dark Souls-inspired gaming.

## Reception

Praised for elegant dread-creation, brilliant Ruin spiral, perfect doom pacing. Players report intense horror experiences. Some want non-doomed option (Trophy Gold addresses this).

## Legacy

Trophy Gold expanded non-doomed play. Ruin mechanics appeared in various games. Influenced doomed-protagonist design. Demonstrated minimal rules could create maximum horror tension.
