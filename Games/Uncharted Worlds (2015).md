# Uncharted Worlds

**Publisher**: Self-published (Sean Gomes)
**Designer**: Sean Gomes
**Year**: 2015
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: space-opera, exploration, science-fiction
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Uncharted Worlds, designed by Sean Gomes in 2015, adapts PbtA for space exploration gaming where players create characters through modular combination of Origin (background), Career (profession), and Template (archetype), explore procedurally generated sectors, take jobs, and navigate faction politics—creating Star Trek/Mass Effect-style space adventure using Apocalypse World's narrative engine with streamlined character creation and sector generation tools enabling easy prep and improvisation.

**Modular Character Creation**: Origin + Career + Template combinations create diverse character options without requiring dozens of playbooks

**Sector Generation**: Quick tools for creating explorable space regions

**Exploration Focus**: Unlike combat-heavy space games, emphasizes discovery and interaction

**Debt and Obligations**: Economic/political pressures driving adventure hooks

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA 2d6+stat**: Standard resolution framework

**Modular Creation**: Combine Origin, Career, Template for character

**Sector Tools**: Generate systems, planets, factions procedurally

**Exploration Moves**: Specific mechanics for discovering new locations

**Factions**: Tracking relationships with various space polities

**Debt System**: Managing obligations and financial pressure

**Career Skills**: Abilities from professional background

## Setting

**Open Space**: Generic space opera setting—works for any sci-fi universe

**Exploration Emphasis**: Discovering unknown rather than purely mission-based

**Faction Politics**: Navigating competing interests

**Procedural Generation**: Tools for creating content on-fly

**Themes**: Discovery, wonder, political complexity, economic pressure

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated modular character creation viability in PbtA. Provided exploration-focused alternative to mission-based space games. Showed procedural generation tools could support narrative play.

## Reception

Praised for modular creation elegance, exploration focus. Some wanted more defined setting. Solid following among space opera fans seeking PbtA option.
