# Star Crossed

**Publisher**: Evil Hat Productions
**Designer**: Alex Roberts
**Year**: 2019
**System**: Jenga-based Romance
**Genre**: romance, two-player, doomed-love
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Star Crossed, designed by Alex Roberts in 2019, creates two-player doomed romance using Jenga tower where every interaction requires pulling block with tower-fall triggering relationship consummation or separation, using physical tower tension to mirror romantic tension brilliantly, demonstrating how physical props could enhance emotional gaming while establishing two-player romance as viable format, showing Jenga (famously used in Dread for horror) could serve romance equally well through same tension-building mechanics applied to different emotional register.

**Jenga Romance**: Physical tower tension = romantic tension

**Two-Player**: Just two participants needed

**Doomed Love**: Relationship fated, question is ending

**Tower Fall**: When Jenga falls, consummate or separate

**Physical Tension**: Building anxiety mirrors emotional buildup

**Any Genre**: Science fiction, fantasy, contemporary—whatever

**Accessible**: Non-gamers can play immediately

**Impact**: Demonstrated physical props serving romance. Validated two-player romance games. Showed Jenga versatility beyond horror.

## Core Mechanics

**Jenga Tower**: Physical tower central mechanic

**Every Interaction**: Pull block when interacting

**Tower Stability**: Degrades like Dread

**Fall**: When tower collapses, relationship resolves

**Resolution**: Kiss/consummate OR separate

**Character Creation**: Quick attraction-based

**Attraction**: What draws characters together (forbidden, dangerous, impossible)

## Setting and Themes

**Themes**: Forbidden love, impossible relationships, tension, longing, resolution
