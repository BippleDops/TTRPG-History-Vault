# Thousand Year Old Vampire

**Publisher**: Tim Hutchings
**Designer**: Tim Hutchings
**Year**: 2019
**System**: Solo Journaling
**Genre**: horror, vampire, journaling, solo
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Thousand Year Old Vampire, designed by Tim Hutchings in 2019, revolutionized solo journaling RPGs by creating prompts-driven game where single player chronicles vampire's centuries-long existence, rolling dice to select prompts ("You fall in love," "Someone you care about discovers your nature," "War devastates your region"), journaling responses while managing limited Memories (forgetting past as centuries accumulate), tracking Resources and Characters that die while you persist immortally, creating profound meditations on loss, isolation, inhuman perspective, and forgetting your own humanity across vast time.

**Solo Journaling Revolution**: While journaling games existed before, TYOV's success triggered explosion of solo journaling RPGs becoming major genre.

**Memory Mechanics**: Brilliant constraint—you can only maintain 5 Memories initially. When creating new Memory, old ones must be erased or combined, mechanically representing how centuries cause forgetting. Your early history fades; you can't remember who you were.

**Commercial Success**: Kickstarter raised over $70,000. Sold thousands of copies. Achieved mainstream gaming press coverage unusual for solo game.

**Beautiful Production**: Hardcover with evocative layout, creating book you want to hold while journaling your vampire's tragedy.

## Core Mechanics

**Prompt Selection**: Roll d10 on prompt table selecting journal prompt

**Journaling**: Write vampire's response to prompt (paragraph or more)

**Memories**: Limited slots (5-7) for remembered events; must erase old to record new

**Characters**: NPCs entering vampire's existence who age and die while you persist

**Resources**: Possessions and locations vampire maintains across centuries

**Skills**: Vampire's accumulated abilities from experiences

**Marks**: Physical changes from traumatic experiences

**Time Progression**: Each prompt advances time (years or decades)

**Endgame**: When prompts exhausted or vampire meets final death/transformation

## Setting and Themes

**Variable History**: Vampire's existence spans centuries across real/fictional history

**Immortality's Curse**: Watching everyone die, forgetting your past, becoming inhuman

**Memory Loss**: Mechanically represented—your early existence becomes blank

**Isolation**: Outliving connections, unable to form lasting relationships

**Loss**: Central theme—losing people, places, memories, humanity itself

**Inhumanity**: Gradually becoming monster through accumulated trauma

## Cultural Impact

**Solo Journaling Explosion**: TYOV's success triggered wave of journaling games (Artefact, Apothecaria, Wretched & Alone, hundreds more)

**Commercial Validation**: Proved solo games could achieve significant sales

**Mainstream Coverage**: Gaming press and mainstream outlets covered release

**Memory Mechanics**: Forgetting-as-mechanic influenced other designs

**Beautiful Production**: Raised standards for indie game presentation

**Kickstarter Success**: $70K+ showed solo game crowdfunding viability

## Reception

Praised for poignant exploration of immortality's horror, brilliant memory constraints, beautiful production. Players report profound emotional experiences journaling vampire's centuries. Some find it melancholy but cathartic.

## Legacy

Helped establish solo journaling as major RPG genre. Demonstrated solo games could achieve commercial success. Influenced memory and forgetting mechanics. Created template for prompted journaling games.
