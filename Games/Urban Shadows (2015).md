# Urban Shadows

**Publisher**: Magpie Games
**Designer**: Andrew Medeiros, Mark Diaz Truman
**Year**: 2015
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: urban-fantasy, political, supernatural
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Urban Shadows, designed by Andrew Medeiros and Mark Diaz Truman in 2015, creates urban fantasy political drama where vampires, werewolves, fae, wizards, and other supernatural beings navigate factional conflicts in contemporary city, using Powered by the Apocalypse framework with innovations including Debt (favors owed between characters creating political leverage), Faction mechanics (tracking power struggles), and playbooks spanning four Archetypes (Mortality, Night, Power, Wild)—creating Vampire: The Masquerade-style political intrigue with modern narrative mechanics.

**Debt Mechanic**: Revolutionary system where characters owe/are-owed Debts (favors), creating political web of obligations, leverage, and influence more interesting than simple monetary transactions.

**Four Factions**: Night (vampires, etc.), Power (wizards, etc.), Mortality (humans), Wild (werewolves, etc.)—each with political agendas and conflicts.

**Urban Fantasy**: Contemporary supernatural hidden-world or open-magic setting, customizable to group preference.

**Corruption**: Like many PbtA, you gradually corrupt/transform through play.

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA Foundation**: 2d6+stat rolls with 10+ success, 7-9 partial, 6- failure triggering GM moves

**Debt System**: 
- Owe Debt: Someone has leverage over you
- Hold Debt: You have leverage over someone
- Spending Debt: Cash in favors for mechanical benefits
- Debt Web: Complex network of obligations creating political dynamics

**Factions**: Night, Power, Wild, Mortality—track relationships and faction standings

**Circle Advancement**: Move between Archetypes as you corrupt/transform

**Corruption Track**: Gradually losing humanity/original nature

**Intimacy and Trust**: Relationship moves creating bonds or betrayals

**Faction Status**: Mechanical benefits/penalties based on standing with supernatural communities

## Setting

**Modern City**: Contemporary urban environment with supernatural presence

**Hidden or Open Magic**: Group decides if supernatural is secret or acknowledged

**Factional Territory**: Different supernatural communities control neighborhoods

**Political Intrigue**: Power struggles, alliances, betrayals among factions

**Themes**: Political maneuvering, identity, transformation, belonging, power's cost

## Cultural Impact

**Debt Innovation**: Debt mechanics widely praised and adopted by other designs

**PbtA Urban Fantasy**: Demonstrated Apocalypse World engine worked for contemporary supernatural

**Political Focus**: Showed PbtA could emphasize intrigue over action

**Faction Mechanics**: Influenced how games track organizational dynamics

**Commercial Success**: Magpie Games hit establishing publisher's reputation

**Second Edition**: Continued development and refinement (2019)

## Reception

Praised for Debt brilliance, faction mechanics, urban fantasy execution, political depth. Some found it complex for PbtA. Commercial and critical success. ENnie nominations and wins.

## Legacy

Debt mechanics influenced numerous games. Established urban fantasy as PbtA genre. Showed political supernatural gaming market. Confirmed Magpie Games as major publisher.
