# Alice is Missing

**Publisher**: Hunters Entertainment
**Designer**: Spenser Starke
**Year**: 2020
**System**: Silent Card-Based Mystery
**Genre**: mystery, tragedy, silent-game
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Alice is Missing, designed by Spenser Starke in 2020, creates 90-minute silent mystery where players investigating missing teenager communicate ONLY through text chat (Discord/group messages) while timer counts down, with card prompts revealing clues and suspect cards determining tragic ending, using complete silence and real-time countdown to create intense emotional experience blending true crime podcast aesthetics with collaborative storytelling, winning multiple awards and becoming phenomenon demonstrating how communication constraints could enhance rather than limit dramatic impact.

**Completely Silent**: NO talking—only text chat/messages

**90-Minute Timer**: Real-time countdown creating urgency

**Missing Person**: Alice went missing, you investigate

**Card Prompts**: Timed reveals advancing mystery

**Tragic**: Always ends badly (Alice's fate predetermined tragic)

**Text-Only**: Discord, group text, or messaging app

**Brilliant Constraint**: Silence creates haunting atmosphere

**Impact**: Demonstrated silent gaming viability. Became phenomenon. Showed real-time and text-only could create profound experiences.

## Core Mechanics

**Setup** (10 minutes):
- Character creation
- Relationship to Alice
- Assign suspect cards (one player is unknowingly involved)

**90-Minute Timer**: Actual real-time countdown

**Card Prompts**: Draw at specific times (10-min, 20-min, 30-min marks)

**Text Communication**: ALL communication via text/Discord

**Clue Reveals**: Cards provide information

**Suspect Cards**: Determining involvement/fate

**Ending**: Alice's fate revealed (always tragic)

**Complete Silence**: No verbal communication whatsoever

## Structure

**Pre-Game** (10 min): Setup and character creation

**90-Minute Investigation**: Real-time mystery with timed card draws

**Conclusion**: Alice's fate revealed, suspects identified

**Optional Debrief**: After game, verbal discussion

## Setting and Themes

**Contemporary Small Town**: Familiar relatable setting

**Missing Teenager**: Alice is gone

**Friend Group**: You know Alice, investigating

**True Crime**: Podcast/documentary aesthetic

**Tragedy**: Alice's fate always tragic (death, terrible circumstances)

**Themes**: Loss, grief, secrets, guilt, friendship tested

## Cultural Impact

**Silent Gaming**: Demonstrated complete silence viable

**Real-Time**: 90-minute actual countdown

**Text-Chat RPG**: Legitimized text-only play

**Pandemic Perfect**: Released perfect timing for Discord gaming

**Awards**: Multiple wins (ENnie, Origins, Golden Geek)

**Phenomenon**: Viral success, widespread play

**True Crime**: Tapping into podcast popularity

## Reception

Praised for haunting atmosphere through silence, brilliant use of medium, emotional impact, tight 90-minute runtime. Players report profound experiences, some crying. Became must-play phenomenon.

## Legacy

Established silent gaming genre. Demonstrated text-only viability. Real-time countdown influential. Showed communication constraints could enhance drama. Continues as widely-played modern classic.
