# Montsegur 1244

**Publisher**: Chimera Creative
**Designer**: Frederik Jensen
**Year**: 2008
**System**: Montsegur 1244 System
**Genre**: historical, tragedy, religious
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Montsegur 1244, designed by Frederik Jensen in 2008, creates intimate historical tragedy about Cathar perfecti (religious leaders) and believers besieged in Montsègur fortress during Albigensian Crusade's final stage, using Nordic LARP techniques adapted for tabletop—transparent character sheets, inner/outer circle scene framing, predetermined historical outcome (fortress falls, Cathars burn)—to examine faith, persecution, love, and sacrifice as characters await inevitable execution for heresy, creating profound meditation on religious conviction facing death.

**Historical Basis**: Meticulously researched 1243-1244 siege of Montsègur fortress in southern France where Catholic forces besieged Cathar believers culminating in mass execution by burning of 200+ perfecti who refused recanting their faith.

**Predetermined Tragedy**: Everyone knows how this ends—fortress falls, Cathars face choice of recanting or death by fire, most choose burning. Game explores HOW characters face this doom.

**Nordic LARP Techniques**: Borrows transparency, workshop, and scene framing techniques from Scandinavian LARP traditions, adapting them for tabletop intimate play.

## Core Mechanics

**Transparent Characters**: Character sheets shared openly—no secrets about beliefs, relationships, fears

**Inner/Outer Circle**: Scene framing technique where active players form inner circle, others observe from outer circle

**Bonds**: Relationships between characters tracked and developed

**Faith Mechanics**: Commitment to Cathar beliefs vs. temptation to recant

**Historical Timeline**: Game progresses through siege's actual chronology

**Endgame Choice**: Recant and live, or burn for faith?

## Setting and Themes

**Montsègur 1244**: Final Cathar stronghold under siege

**Cathars**: Dualist Christian sect deemed heretical by Catholic Church

**Albigensian Crusade**: Genocide/religious persecution campaign against Cathars

**Historical Accuracy**: Based on documented events, real people, actual theological positions

**Themes**: Faith under persecution, love facing death, community in crisis, martyrdom, religious conviction

## Cultural Impact

Brought Nordic LARP techniques to tabletop gaming. Demonstrated historical religious persecution could be handled sensitively. Influenced historical tragedy gaming.
