# The Quiet Year

**Publisher**: Buried Without Ceremony
**Designer**: Avery Alder
**Year**: 2013
**System**: The Quiet Year System
**Genre**: map-drawing, community-building, post-apocalyptic
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

The Quiet Year, designed by Avery Alder in 2013, creates collaborative map-drawing game where players portray post-apocalyptic community during one year of fragile peace before inevitable return of Frost Shepherds (undefined threat), drawing physical map of settlement as it develops, introducing projects and discoveries through card-prompted turns, using brilliant Contempt mechanic where players can hold Contempt tokens silently representing disagreements they don't voice—until game end when all held Contempt explodes into revealed conflicts, creating poignant examination of community tensions, unspoken resentments, and how fragile peace depends on what people DON'T say to each other.

**Map-Drawing Central**: Physical map development is core gameplay—players literally draw settlement, resources, threats on shared paper, creating tangible artifact representing community's growth.

**The Silence Rule**: Players cannot directly speak for community—you narrate what character proposes/does but cannot force consensus without card prompts allowing it. This creates realistic community dynamics where individuals suggest but groups decide.

**Contempt Mechanic**: When disagreeing with another player's choice, can silently hold Contempt token rather than arguing. At game end, reveal all Contempt explaining what you resented. This models how communities function through polite silence until tensions explode.

**Predetermined End**: The Frost Shepherds return after exactly 52 weeks. Your community's fate then is uncertain—the year of peace was just respite before whatever comes next.

## Core Mechanics

**52-Week Structure**: Deck of playing cards (no Jokers) with each card representing week

**Turn Order**: Draw card, consult suit for turn type (Clubs=Discover, Spades=Project, Hearts=Community, Diamonds=Want)

**Card Prompts**: Each card number provides specific prompt for that turn type

**Map Drawing**: Players physically draw on shared map when prompted

**Projects**: Community improvements tracked on map with token until completion

**Discoveries**: New threats/resources appear through card prompts

**Discussion Tokens**: Limited ability to hold community discussions (scarcity creates tension)

**Contempt Holding**: Silently take token when disagreeing with others' choices

**Endgame**: Reveal all Contempt, explain resentments, then Frost Shepherds arrive

## Setting and Themes

**Post-Apocalyptic**: Collapse has occurred; survivors rebuilding

**The Quiet Year**: Temporary peace before next disaster

**Frost Shepherds**: Undefined returning threat creating urgency

**Community Focus**: Not individual heroes but collective survival

**Themes**:
- Unspoken tensions in communities
- What people don't say to each other
- Fragile peace
- Community-building under threat
- Inevitable doom approaching
- Growth despite futility

## Cultural Impact

**Map-Drawing Game Proliferation**: Triggered wave of map-drawing games (The Ground Itself, Ex Novo, Dawn of Worlds variants)

**Contempt Mechanic**: Brilliant innovation modeling unspoken community tensions

**Solo/Low-Player Gaming**: Validated 2-4 player collaborative games

**Commercial Success**: Multiple printings, widespread play, convention staple

**Ennie Nomination**: Recognized for innovation

**Designer Recognition**: Established Alder as major designer alongside Monsterhearts

**Educational Use**: Some facilitators use for community-building exercises

**Art Object**: Beautiful production making game treasured physical object

## Reception

Praised for brilliant Contempt mechanic, meditative collaborative play, map-drawing satisfaction, poignant community dynamics. Players report profound experiences examining how communities function and fail. Some find silence rule challenging initially but appreciate it later.

## Legacy

Established map-drawing as major game genre. Contempt mechanic widely admired. Influenced community-focused gaming. Demonstrated collaborative quiet games could be deeply engaging.
