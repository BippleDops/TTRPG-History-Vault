# Spire: The City Must Fall

**Publisher**: Rowan, Rook and Decard
**Designer**: Grant Howitt, Chris Taylor
**Year**: 2018
**System**: Resistance System (d10 dice pools)
**Genre**: dark-fantasy, urban, revolution
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Spire: The City Must Fall, designed by Grant Howitt and Chris Taylor in 2018, casts players as dark elf revolutionaries in vertical city ruled by oppressive high elves, using Resistance System (stress and fallout mechanics inspired by Blades in the Dark) where characters pursue revolution through terrorism, espionage, and direct action while managing stress that accumulates toward inevitable death—creating tense revolutionary narratives examining resistance, violence, desperation, and price of fighting tyranny in impossibly stacked circumstances.

**The Setting**: Spire is mile-high vertical city with high elves ruling from top tiers, dark elves oppressed in lower levels. Protagonists are Ministry (secret revolutionary organization) agents working to bring down the system.

**Class System**: Ten playbooks representing revolutionary roles—Bound (blood mage), Carrion-Priest (death cleric), Firebrand (agitator), Junk-Mage (scrap sorcerer), etc.—each with unique abilities and stress relationship.

**Stress and Fallout**: Inspired by Blades, characters accumulate stress (Blood, Mind, Silver, Reputation, Shadow) triggering fallout (consequences) when tracks fill, creating death-spirals where revolutionaries burn out, die, or become what they hate.

**Revolutionary Tone**: Dark, desperate, violent—revolution isn't romantic but brutal necessary struggle.

## Core Mechanics

**d10 Dice Pools**: Roll pools, highest die determines outcome (1-5 fail, 6-7 succeed with stress, 8-9 succeed, 10 critical)

**Five Stress Tracks**: Blood, Mind, Silver, Reputation, Shadow—different challenges threaten different aspects

**Fallout**: When stress track fills, suffer consequence (minor, moderate, severe) and clear track

**Domains and Skills**: Characters have Domains (broad areas) and Skills (specific competencies) determining dice pools

**Classes**: Ten revolutionary roles with unique stress relationships and abilities

**Death Spirals**: Accumulating fallout makes characters increasingly vulnerable until death/retirement

**Resistance Actions**: Specific revolutionary activities (assassinations, propaganda, sabotage, espionage)

## Setting

**The Spire**: Mile-high city with strict class/racial hierarchies

**High Elves**: Oppressive ruling class controlling upper tiers

**Dark Elves (Drow)**: Oppressed working class in lower city

**The Ministry**: Secret revolutionary organization PCs serve

**Districts**: Each tier has unique character (slums, markets, religious districts, noble quarters)

**Weird Magic**: Blood magic, junk magic, weird mysticism permeating city

**Themes**: Revolution, oppression, terrorism vs freedom fighting, desperate violence, systemic resistance

## Cultural Impact

Established Resistance System as notable engine. Demonstrated revolutionary narratives could drive campaigns. Influenced stress/fallout mechanics. Led to Heart: The City Beneath (same city, different premise). Showed dark elves as protagonists rather than villains. Beautiful production raised indie standards.

## Reception

Praised for setting depth, revolutionary tone, stress mechanics, class variety, dark themes. Some uncomfortable with terrorism focus. Widely acclaimed within indie scene. Commercial success enabled Heart sequel.

## Legacy

Led to Heart: The City Beneath. Influenced stress/fallout systems. Established Howitt/Taylor as major design team. Demonstrated revolutionary gaming market. Beautiful production inspired other indies.
