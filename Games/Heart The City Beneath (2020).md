# Heart: The City Beneath

**Publisher**: Rowan, Rook and Decard
**Designer**: Grant Howitt, Chris Taylor
**Year**: 2020
**System**: Resistance System (refined)
**Genre**: dark-fantasy, horror, dungeon-delving
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Heart: The City Beneath, designed by Grant Howitt and Chris Taylor in 2020, explores living nightmare dungeon beneath Spire where delvers descend seeking riches/answers/redemption in reality-warping abyss that eats you psychologically and physically, using refined Resistance System where stress and fallout create death-spirals but focusing on personal quests (Calling-driven advancement) rather than revolutionary politics, creating weird horror dungeon-delving where the dungeon itself is malevolent god-corpse warping reality around it.

**The Heart**: Beneath Spire lies The Heart—dead god whose corpse became living nightmare dungeon:
- Reality warps based on emotional states
- Distances are subjective
- Time flows inconsistently  
- Mass delusions manifest physically
- The deeper you descend, the weirder everything becomes

**Ten Classes**: Delvers drawn to Heart for different reasons—Deadwalker (death-touched), Deep Apiarist (bee-keeper), Heartsblood (living weapon), Junk Mage (scrap sorcerer), etc.

**Calling System**: Characters have Calling (reason for delving) with milestones. Completing Calling milestones advances character and can end their story.

**Stress and Fallout**: Same elegant system from Spire but tuned for dungeon horror.

**Weird Fantasy**: Embraces surrealism, body horror, psychological terror, bizarre beauty.

## Core Mechanics

**Resistance System**: d10 pools with stress/fallout (same foundation as Spire)

**Callings**: Personal quests driving delve (Escape the City, Seek Enlightenment, Reunite with Lost Love)

**Calling Milestones**: Structured advancement through pursuing Calling

**Delve Structure**: Descending into Heart's levels (The Drowned, The Vermissian, The Hive, etc.)

**Landmarks**: Specific Heart locations with unique properties and dangers

**Heart's Influence**: Rules for reality warping, emotional manifestation, subjective distances

**Resources**: Managing supplies in hostile environment where nothing works as expected

## Setting

**The Heart**: Dead god-corpse beneath Spire forming nightmare megadungeon

**Layers**: Each depth has distinct character and escalating weirdness

**Denizens**: Bizarre inhabitants—mushroom people, abandoned angels, failed delvers, conceptual entities

**Delve Reasons**: People enter Heart seeking something worth risking madness/death

**Themes**: Descent into madness, body horror, weird beauty, psychological terror, ambition's cost

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated weird horror dungeon gaming viability. Refined Resistance System. Showed sequel could explore same setting differently (Heart beneath Spire). Influenced weird fantasy aesthetics. Beautiful production continued RRD high standards.

## Reception

Praised for weird creativity, stress refinement, Calling system, gorgeous art. Some found weirdness overwhelming. Commercial success confirmed RRD as major indie publisher. ENnie nominations.

## Legacy

Established Heart alongside Spire in shared setting. Influenced weird dungeon design. Showed Resistance System versatility. Beautiful books inspired indie production quality improvements.
