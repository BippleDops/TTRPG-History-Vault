# Murderous Ghosts

**Publisher**: Lumpley Games
**Designer**: D. Vincent Baker
**Year**: 2011
**System**: Murderous Ghosts System
**Genre**: horror, two-player, procedural
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Murderous Ghosts, designed by Vincent Baker in 2011, delivers pure two-player horror through brilliant procedural book design: one player is Ghost (hunting), one is Explorer (surviving), each with separate book containing numbered entries creating branching narrative as players follow instructions, roll dice, and reference new entries based on outcomes—no prep, no GM, just two people, two books, and escalating terror as Explorer tries escaping haunted location before Ghost kills them.

**Key Innovation**: Procedural Choose-Your-Own-Adventure structure for two players creates genuine horror through uncertainty—Ghost player doesn't know what Explorer experiences, Explorer doesn't know what Ghost can do, creating authentic scary mystery.

**Play Format**: Physical books (or PDFs) with numbered entries. "Turn to entry 47" style gameplay creates tactile ritual enhancing horror atmosphere.

## Core Mechanics

**Dual Books**: Separate Explorer Book and Ghost Book with different numbered entries

**Procedural Play**: Follow entry instructions, roll dice when prompted, turn to new entries based on results

**Dice Resolution**: Simple d6 or d10 rolls determining success/failure

**Escalation**: Encounters intensify as game progresses

**Win Conditions**: Explorer escapes or Ghost kills them

## Setting and Themes

**Haunted Location**: Any abandoned dangerous place (asylum, factory, mansion, cave system)

**Explorer**: Interloper who entered forbidden place, now trying to escape alive

**Ghost**: Murderous supernatural entity hunting intruder

**Horror Isolation**: Two-player format creates intimate horror experience

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated procedural book design could create compelling horror RPG experiences. Validated two-player horror as viable format. Showed Choose-Your-Own-Adventure structure could serve sophisticated adult gaming.
