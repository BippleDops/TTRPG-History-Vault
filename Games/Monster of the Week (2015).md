# Monster of the Week

**Publisher**: Evil Hat Productions (Generic Games originally)
**Designer**: Michael Sands
**Year**: 2015 (Evil Hat revised edition)
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: urban-fantasy, monster-hunting, investigation
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Monster of the Week, designed by Michael Sands with Evil Hat revised edition 2015, creates Buffy/Supernatural/X-Files monster-hunting using PbtA where hunters investigate mysteries, gather clues through Investigate a Mystery move, and defeat weekly monsters using playbooks (The Chosen, The Professional, The Spooky, etc.) representing hunter archetypes, with Countdown structure tracking monster's plan progression creating urgency, becoming one of PbtA's most commercially successful games demonstrating investigation and episodic monster-hunting viable with Apocalypse World framework.

**Monster-of-the-Week Format**: Episodic TV structure—each mystery self-contained

**Hunter Playbooks**: The Chosen (Buffy), Professional (Angel), Spooky (Willow), etc.

**Investigation Move**: Core mechanic gathering clues

**Countdown**: Monster's plan advancing creating urgency

**Harm**: Dangerous but heroic hunters endure

**Commercial Success**: One of best-selling PbtA games

**Impact**: Established investigation PbtA. Demonstrated monster-hunting + PbtA works brilliantly.

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA 2d6+stat**: Standard Apocalypse World resolution

**Playbooks** (11):
- The Chosen (prophesied hero)
- The Crooked (criminal background)
- The Divine (supernatural mission)
- The Expert (experienced researcher)
- The Flake (conspiracy theorist)
- The Initiate (secret society)
- The Monstrous (part-monster hunter)
- The Mundane (normal person caught up)
- The Professional (trained operative)
- The Spell-Slinger (wizard)
- The Spooky (natural psychic)

**Investigate a Mystery**: Core Move gathering clues through questions
- What happened here?
- What sort of creature?
- What can hurt it?
- Where did it go?
- What will it do next?

**Countdown**: Mystery's timeline (6 stages from omen to apocalypse)

**Harm**: Damage track (0-7 harm, 4+ unstable, 7+ dead)

**Luck**: Spendable resource for hunters

**Use Magic**: Spell-casting with risks

**Big Magic**: Ritual casting for major effects

## Reception

Praised for Investigate move elegance, faithful genre emulation, playbook variety, accessibility. Became go-to monster-hunting RPG recommendation.

## Legacy

Established investigation PbtA template. Demonstrated episodic monster-hunting worked with Apocalypse World. Commercial success confirmed PbtA mainstream viability. Continues as popular ongoing title.
