# Downfall

**Publisher**: Self-published
**Designer**: Caroline Hobbs
**Year**: 2012
**System**: Downfall System
**Genre**: tragedy, civilization-collapse, GMless
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Downfall, designed by Caroline Hobbs in 2012, enables players to collaboratively create civilization, define its three Pillars (core values/institutions sustaining it), then systematically destroy those Pillars through play, chronicling society's inevitable collapse as characters navigate deteriorating world, making choices that erode Pillars (even when trying to preserve them), until civilization falls completely—creating tragic arc examining how societies destroy themselves through their own values taken to extremes or corrupted.

**Pillar Mechanics**: Three Pillars (e.g., "Military Might," "Religious Devotion," "Technological Progress") track civilization's integrity. Scene outcomes erode Pillars. When all Pillars fall, civilization collapses.

**Collaborative Creation**: Group builds civilization from scratch—culture, values, geography, institutions

**Inevitable Doom**: Mechanics guarantee collapse; question is how and who survives

**GMless**: All players share authority

## Core Mechanics

**Civilization Creation**: Collaboratively design society with three defining Pillars

**Pillar Tracks**: Each Pillar has integrity track; damage reduces it toward zero

**Character Creation**: Protagonists representing different society sectors

**Scene Structure**: Scenes challenge Pillars; outcomes damage them

**Freefall**: When Pillar reaches zero, enters Freefall (rapid collapse)

**Endgame**: When all Pillars fail, civilization falls; epilogue determines character fates

## Setting and Themes

**Variable Civilization**: Any society—historical, fantasy, sci-fi, contemporary

**Three Pillars**: Core values that civilization depends on

**Collapse**: Systematic societal breakdown

**Themes**:
- How civilizations destroy themselves
- Values becoming corrupted
- Individual vs. collective good
- Inevitable decline
- Survival after collapse

## Cultural Impact

Influenced civilization-building/collapse games. Demonstrated how mechanics could model societal dynamics. Pillar mechanics appeared in various forms in other games.
