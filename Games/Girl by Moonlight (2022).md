# Girl by Moonlight

**Publisher**: Evil Hat Productions
**Designer**: Andrew Gillis
**Year**: 2022
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: magical-girls, anime, tragedy
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Girl by Moonlight, designed by Andrew Gillis in 2022, brings magical girl anime to PbtA with four settings (String of Pearls street-level magical girls, Alien Neon Babylon cyberpunk, Grand Season epic fantasy, Thousand Arrows wuxia) where chosen heroes transform to fight evil while managing mundane lives, relationships, and gradual corruption through Shadows (darkness accumulating from magical girl duties), creating Madoka Magica-style tragic magical girl stories examining cost of heroism, identity duality, and inevitable corruption.

**Four Settings**: Complete magical girl settings with different tones—
- String of Pearls: Contemporary magical girls fighting local evils
- Alien Neon Babylon: Cyberpunk noir magical girls
- Grand Season: Epic fantasy chosen heroes
- Thousand Arrows: Wuxia martial heroes

**Transformation**: Mechanics for magical girl transformations and powered forms

**Mundane Life**: Balancing heroics with school/relationships/normal existence

**Shadows**: Corruption track from magical duties—eventual transformation into villain

**Tragic Arc**: Inspired by Madoka—magical girl duties corrupt you toward becoming enemy

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA 2d6+stat**: Standard framework

**Dual Forms**: Mundane and transformed with different capabilities

**Transformation Moves**: Specific mechanics for magical girl transformations

**Shadows**: Corruption accumulating from magical duties

**Mundane Life Moves**: Handling school, family, relationships

**Burnout**: Eventual corruption into Shadow (villain)

**Playbooks**: Different magical girl archetypes per setting

**Team Dynamics**: Magical girl team bonds and conflicts

## Setting

**Four Complete Settings**: Each with distinct tone, threats, themes

**Magical Girl Tropes**: Transformation sequences, friendship power, gradual darkness

**Themes**: Cost of heroism, corruption through duty, identity duality, friendship, sacrifice, becoming what you fight

## Cultural Impact

Brought magical girl anime to Western PbtA gaming. Demonstrated anime genres viable in tabletop. Showed tragic magical girl (Madoka influence) was compelling gaming territory.

## Reception

Praised for faithful magical girl tone, four distinct settings, Shadow mechanics. Magical girl fans appreciated respectful adaptation. Some found it niche but enthusiastic following.
