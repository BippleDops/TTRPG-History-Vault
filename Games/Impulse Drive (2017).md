# Impulse Drive

**Publisher**: Self-published (Adrian Thoen)
**Designer**: Adrian Thoen
**Year**: 2017
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: space-opera, science-fiction, crew-based
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Impulse Drive, designed by Adrian Thoen in 2017, creates Firefly-style space opera using PbtA where players portray spaceship crew (pilot, engineer, mechanic, muscle, etc.) taking jobs, navigating sector politics, managing ship upgrades, and staying ahead of debts/enemies—demonstrating PbtA works for crew-based space adventures with collaborative ship creation, sector worldbuilding, and episodic mission structure creating accessible space opera alternative to complex games like Traveller.

**Crew Focus**: Emphasizes ensemble cast dynamics like Firefly/Expanse

**Ship Creation**: Collaborative spaceship design with stats, quirks, problems

**Sector Worldbuilding**: Group creates local space region with factions, threats

**Jobs**: Mission-based episodic structure

**Debt and Heat**: Economic and political pressure creating complications

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA 2d6+stat**: Standard framework

**Playbooks**: Pilot, Engineer, Muscle, Scoundrel, Speaker, etc.—crew roles

**Ship Stats**: Collaborative ship with mechanical properties

**Sector Creation**: Mapping local space with factions and locations

**Job Structure**: Taking contracts, executing missions, dealing with complications

**Debt Tracking**: Financial obligations creating pressure

## Setting

**Generic Space Opera**: Intentionally flexible—works for any space setting

**Crew Life**: Focus on working crew rather than military or explorers

**Economic Pressure**: Always need next job to pay bills/debts

**Factional Politics**: Navigating competing interests

**Themes**: Found family, survival, loyalty, economic desperation

## Cultural Impact

Provided accessible space opera PbtA option. Demonstrated crew-based play worked with engine. Firefly-style gaming without licensed game complexity.

## Reception

Praised for accessibility, crew dynamics, ship creation. Some wanted more tactical space combat. Solid following among space opera fans.
