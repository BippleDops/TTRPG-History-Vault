# Root: The Tabletop Roleplaying Game

**Publisher**: Magpie Games
**Designer**: Brendan Conway, Mark Diaz Truman, et al.
**Year**: 2020
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: fantasy, woodland-animals, political, warfare
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Root: The Tabletop Roleplaying Game, designed by Magpie Games in 2020, adapts Cole Wehrle's acclaimed Root board game to PbtA RPG where players portray woodland animals (mice, foxes, rabbits, birds) navigating political warfare between Marquisate (cat overlords), Eyrie Dynasty (bird aristocracy), Woodland Alliance (revolutionary animals), and Vagabonds (wandering adventurers)—creating asymmetric factional play with political intrigue, military campaigns, and personal stories set in charming-but-brutal animal kingdom.

**Licensed Adaptation**: Successfully adapted board game to RPG format

**Playbooks**: Vagabond roles (Arbiter, Champion, Chronicler, Exile, Harrier, Ranger, Ronin, Scoundrel, Seeker, Tinker)

**Faction Play**: Integration with board game's factional warfare

**Clearing-Based**: Adventures occur in forest clearings with factional control

**Charming Brutality**: Cute woodland animals in serious political warfare

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA Framework**: 2d6+stat standard resolution

**Vagabond Playbooks**: Character roles for wandering adventurers

**Faction Integration**: How RPG play intersects with board game faction mechanics

**Clearing Control**: Political dominance of forest locations

**Equipment and Items**: Material resources and crafting

**Reputation**: Standing with different factions

## Setting

**The Woodland**: Forest kingdom from Root board game

**Factions**: Marquisate, Eyrie, Woodland Alliance, Vagabonds—political entities at war

**Cute Animals, Serious Conflicts**: Anthropomorphic animals in political/military struggles

**Themes**: Revolution, imperialism, political change, found family, warfare's cost

## Cultural Impact

Successful licensed PbtA adaptation. Demonstrated board-game-to-RPG conversions could work. Showed animal protagonists could carry serious themes. Expanded Root's multimedia presence.

## Reception

Praised for faithful adaptation, charming aesthetics with serious themes. Root fans appreciated RPG option. Some found faction integration complex. Commercial success for licensed PbtA.
