# Kagematsu

**Publisher**: Self-published
**Designer**: Danielle Lewon
**Year**: 2010
**System**: Kagematsu System
**Genre**: chambara, romance, tragedy
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Kagematsu, designed by Danielle Lewon in 2010, creates chambara stories where one player portrays Kagematsu (wandering ronin who will save village), while others portray village women who must convince him to help through romantic/emotional appeals, with gendered play deliberately highlighting power imbalances—women must use limited social power (seduction, emotional manipulation) to motivate armed male stranger to save their homes, creating uncomfortable but thoughtful exploration of gender dynamics in historical and genre contexts.

**Controversial Design**: Explicitly requires gender role-play—one player (regardless of actual gender) plays male Kagematsu, others (regardless of actual gender) play women with limited agency, creating deliberate discomfort examining gendered power.

**Key Mechanic**: Women compete for Kagematsu's favor through romantic gestures. Success means Kagematsu helps village; failure means village perishes. Women's choices literally determine community survival but only through influencing man with actual power.

## Core Mechanics

**Asymmetric Roles**: One Kagematsu player, 3-4 women players

**Favor Tracking**: Kagematsu assigns favor to women based on romantic appeals

**Resolution**: Whether Kagematsu saves village depends on accumulated favor and final choice

**Gendered Mechanics**: Deliberately different player experiences based on assigned gender role

**Competitive Romance**: Women compete for Kagematsu's attention while village fate hangs

## Setting and Themes

**Feudal Japan**: Historical setting with rigid gender hierarchies

**Chambara Genre**: Wandering ronin saves village trope

**Gender Power Dynamics**: Women's limited agency and necessary manipulation

**Tragedy**: Potential for village destruction, personal heartbreak

**Historical Accuracy**: Reflects actual historical gender restrictions

## Cultural Impact

Sparked extensive discussion about gendered mechanics and uncomfortable play. Demonstrated games could examine difficult social dynamics through deliberately constrained play. Influenced thinking about asymmetric player experiences.

## Controversy

Praised for thoughtfully examining gender power; criticized for potentially reinforcing stereotypes or creating uncomfortable play. Designer intended provocative examination, not endorsement, of gender dynamics.
