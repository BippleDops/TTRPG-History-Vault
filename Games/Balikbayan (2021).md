# Balikbayan: Returning Home

**Publisher**: Hella Positive Roleplay

## Overview

Balikbayan: Returning Home, designed by Jammi Tong in 2021, explores Filipino diaspora experiences through No Dice No Masters framework where players portray Filipino-Americans returning to Philippines for family reunion/funeral/wedding, navigating cultural connections and disconnections, language barriers, family expectations, identity questions, and what "home" means when caught between cultures, creating thoughtful examination of diaspora identity through gentle mechanics emphasizing family bonds and cultural navigation.

**Cultural Specificity**: Explicitly Filipino/Filipino-American experiences

**Balikbayan**: Tagalog term for overseas Filipino returning home

**Family Drama**: Extended family dynamics, generational differences

**Cultural Navigation**: Language code-switching, cultural expectations, identity questions

**Gentle Exploration**: Sensitive handling of identity and belonging themes

## Core Mechanics

**Belonging Outside Belonging**: Diceless GMless with Weak/Strong moves and token economy

**Playbooks**: Family roles and diaspora positions

**Cultural Moves**: Navigating Filipino cultural expectations

**Language Play**: Code-switching between English/Tagalog

**Family Bonds**: Relationships with extended family members

**Home Questions**: What does "home" mean? Where do you belong?

## Setting

**Philippines**: Contemporary or recent past

**Diaspora**: Filipino-Americans negotiating cultural identity

**Family Gathering**: Reunion, funeral, wedding, balikbayan box delivery

**Themes**: Cultural identity, belonging, family, diaspora, language, home

## Cultural Impact

Brought Filipino diaspora narratives to tabletop gaming. Demonstrated cultural-specific stories had audiences. Influenced diaspora and cultural identity gaming. Part of movement toward diverse cultural representation.

## Reception

Praised for cultural authenticity, gentle handling of complex identity questions, beautiful production. Filipino diaspora community especially appreciated representation. Critics noted importance of specific cultural narratives.
