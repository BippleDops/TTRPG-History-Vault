# Wicked Ones

**Publisher**: Bandit Camp
**Designer**: Ben Nielson, Victor Costa
**Year**: 2021
**System**: Forged in the Dark
**Genre**: fantasy, monster-protagonists, dungeon-building
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Wicked Ones, designed by Ben Nielson and Victor Costa in 2021, inverts traditional dungeon-crawling by casting players as monsters building dungeons and defending them from adventurers, using Forged in the Dark framework where goblin warrens, vampire lairs, and dragon hoards are constructed through downtime projects while monsters raid nearby lands for resources, creating inverse power fantasy where you're Evil Overlord managing monster crew, building defenses, and occasionally fighting heroes who invade YOUR home.

**Dungeon-Building**: Downtime projects construct dungeon rooms, traps, treasures—your lair grows through play

**Monster Playbooks**: Goblin, Ogre, Vampire, Skeleton, Fey, etc.—classic monsters as protagonists

**Raids**: Missions where monsters attack nearby lands for resources/prisoners

**Defending Lair**: Heroes periodically invade; you defend home turf

**Tier Advancement**: Dungeon and crew advance through tiers (from pathetic warren to legendary stronghold)

## Core Mechanics

**Forged in the Dark Foundation**: Action rolls, position/effect, stress, crew advancement

**Dungeon Phases**: 
- Raids (get resources)
- Downtime (build dungeon, recover)
- Invasions (defend against heroes)

**Lair Construction**: Rooms, traps, minions, treasures built through projects

**Threat Level**: Nearby civilizations' awareness/response to monsters

**Monster Crews**: Types (Raiders, Cultists, Hoarders) with special abilities

**Minions**: NPC monsters serving player monsters

## Setting

**Generic Fantasy**: Deliberately archetypal—works with any fantasy world

**Monster Perspective**: Playing creatures usually portrayed as disposable enemies

**Dungeon as Home**: Your lair is base, home, project—defending it is personal

**Nearby Lands**: Human/elf/dwarf territories to raid

**Themes**: Evil overlord fantasy, dungeon-building satisfaction, monster community, defending what's yours

## Cultural Impact

Popularized monster protagonist gaming. Demonstrated Forged in the Dark flexibility for fantasy. Dungeon-building scratched creative itch. Inverse dungeon-crawling became recognized genre.

## Reception

Praised for clever premise, dungeon-building satisfaction, monster perspective novelty. Some wanted more complex dungeon design. Commercial success showed demand for monster gaming.

## Legacy

Influenced monster protagonist designs. Showed FitD works for fantasy dungeon settings. Dungeon-building mechanics appeared in other games.
