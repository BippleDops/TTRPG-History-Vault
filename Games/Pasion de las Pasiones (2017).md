# Pasión de las Pasiones

**Publisher**: Magpie Games
**Designer**: Claudia Cangini
**Year**: 2017
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: telenovela, romance, drama
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Pasión de las Pasiones, designed by Claudia Cangini in 2017, creates telenovela stories using PbtA framework where players portray dramatic characters (passionate lovers, scheming villains, suffering heroines) in over-the-top romantic melodrama, using playbooks like El Hombre Misterioso (The Mysterious Man) and La Sufrida (The Suffering Woman), capturing telenovela aesthetics—amnesia, secret twins, dramatic revelations, passionate declarations—through mechanics encouraging melodramatic escalation and relationship drama.

**Telenovela Tropes**: Embraces genre conventions (amnesia, evil twins, hidden parentage, passionate love)

**Playbooks**: Archetypal telenovela roles with appropriately dramatic moves

**Relationships**: Tracked mechanically driving drama

**Melodrama Moves**: Encouraging over-the-top declarations and dramatic moments

**Light Fun**: Unlike many indie games' heavy themes, this is joyful melodrama

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA Framework**: Standard 2d6+stat

**Telenovela Playbooks**: Genre-specific archetypes

**Relationship Dynamics**: Tracking connections between characters

**Dramatic Reveals**: Mechanics for shocking revelations

**Passion Tracks**: Emotional intensity mechanical representation

**Episode Structure**: Organized like telenovela episodes with cliffhangers

## Setting

**Any Telenovela Setting**: Modern, historical, wealthy estates, humble origins—whatever fits genre

**Melodrama**: Everything is heightened, emotional, dramatic

**Themes**: Passionate love, scheming rivalry, family drama, hidden secrets

## Cultural Impact

Demonstrated PbtA worked for lighthearted melodrama. Brought telenovela aesthetics to English-language gaming. Showed romance/drama gaming could be fun and light.

## Reception

Praised for joyful tone, genre-accurate tropes, fun accessible play. Niche appeal but enthusiastic following.
