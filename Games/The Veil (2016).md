# The Veil

**Publisher**: Samjoko Publishing
**Designer**: Fraser Simons
**Year**: 2016
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: cyberpunk, transhumanism, mindscape
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

The Veil, designed by Fraser Simons in 2016, creates cyberpunk gaming where stats are emotional states (Angry, Peaceful, Joyful, Scared, Sad, Powerful) rather than traditional attributes, characters navigate both physical reality and digital/mental mindscapes (The Veil—neural interface overlay), and transhumanist themes (identity, consciousness, what makes us human) drive narratives, using PbtA framework with innovations emphasizing psychological/emotional aspects of cyberpunk over typical combat/hacking focus.

**Emotional State Stats**: Revolutionary approach using emotions as character statistics

**The Veil**: Neural overlay blurring physical/digital reality

**Mindscape**: Mental/digital spaces where consciousness operates

**Transhumanist Focus**: Identity, consciousness uploading, what defines humanity

**Playbooks**: Apparatus (cyborg), Attached (AI-bonded), Catabolist (tech scavenger), Dying (terminal illness), Empath, Executor, Honed (assassin), Honorbound, Onomastic (reality hacker), Seeker, Wayward

## Core Mechanics

**Emotional Stats**: Mad, Peaceful, Joyful, Scared, Sad, Powerful—roll using current emotional state

**State Shifts**: Emotions change based on experiences

**Mindscape Mechanics**: Rules for navigating digital/mental spaces

**Giri and Obligations**: Japanese-inspired duty and social obligation tracking

**Questions**: Character creation through answering questions rather than stat allocation

**Reality Layers**: Physical reality, augmented overlay (The Veil), pure digital

## Setting

**Near Future**: Cyberpunk world with neural interfaces ubiquitous

**The Veil**: Digital overlay everyone experiences reality through

**Transhumanism**: Consciousness uploading, AI, human-machine fusion

**Themes**: Identity, consciousness, reality perception, emotional truth, what makes us human

## Cultural Impact

Emotional state stats influenced how designers thought about character definition. Demonstrated cyberpunk PbtA viability. Transhumanist focus differentiated from typical cyberpunk combat emphasis.

## Reception

Praised for emotional stat innovation, transhumanist themes, mindscape mechanics. Some found emotional stats confusing. Strong following among cyberpunk and transhumanism fans.

## Legacy

Emotional stats influenced other emotional/psychological designs. Showed PbtA cyberpunk worked differently than Shadowrun-style. Transhumanist gaming gained sophisticated option.
