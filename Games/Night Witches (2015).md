# Night Witches

**Publisher**: Bully Pulpit Games
**Designer**: Jason Morningstar
**Year**: 2015
**System**: Powered by the Apocalypse
**Genre**: historical, military, WWII, aviation
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Night Witches, designed by Jason Morningstar in 2015, chronicles Soviet 588th Night Bomber Regiment—all-women unit flying dangerous nighttime bombing missions in outdated biplanes against Nazis during WWII—using Powered by the Apocalypse framework adapted for military historical play where characters balance Duty (to Motherland, regiment, mission) against survival, accumulate Marks (missions survived) toward potential war's end or death, and navigate gendered discrimination within Soviet military while performing extraordinary heroism, creating deeply researched historical gaming honoring real women pilots' experiences.

**Historical Authenticity**:
- Based on actual 588th Regiment
- Real pilot names included
- Authentic equipment (Po-2 biplanes)
- Historical missions and targets
- Soviet military culture details
- Gender dynamics accurately portrayed

**Duty Mechanic**: Track Duty to various entities—conflicts arise when duties contradict

**Marks**: Missions survived tracking progression toward war's end (May 1945)

**Gender in Military**: Mechanics addressing discrimination, harassment, proving worth

**PbtA Historical**: Demonstrated Apocalypse World framework worked for historical military gaming

## Core Mechanics

**PbtA Foundation**: 2d6+stat with standard PbtA result ranges

**Duty Tracks**: Owe Duty to Motherland, Regiment, Mission—track separately

**Marks**: Each mission survived earns Mark. Accumulate toward war's end.

**Missions**: Structured bombing runs with specific dangers (flak, fighters, weather, equipment failure)

**Section Creation**: Group creates their section within regiment collaboratively

**Gendered Experiences**: Moves addressing discrimination, harassment, isolation

**Between Missions**: Downtime for relationships, letter-writing, dealing with trauma

**Historical Timeline**: Game follows actual war chronology 1942-1945

## Setting

**Soviet Union 1942-1945**: Eastern Front of WWII

**588th Night Bomber Regiment**: Real all-women bomber unit

**Po-2 Biplanes**: Obsolete fabric-wood aircraft (nicknamed "sewing machines")

**Night Bombing**: Dangerous missions in darkness with primitive navigation

**Male Harassment**: Discrimination within Soviet military despite service

**Themes**: Women's contributions to war, duty vs survival, discrimination despite heroism, found family in regiment, historical tragedy

## Cultural Impact

**ENnie Gold 2016**: Won Best Game

**Historical Gaming Standards**: Raised bar for research and authenticity

**Women's History**: Centered women's experiences in WWII gaming

**PbtA Historical**: Demonstrated engine's flexibility for historical military

**Educational Use**: Some educators used for teaching WWII women's roles

**Mainstream Coverage**: Gaming press and history outlets covered release

## Reception

Praised for historical depth, honoring real women pilots, emotional power, excellent PbtA adaptation for military. Some found it emotionally heavy. Widely acclaimed as masterpiece of historical gaming.

## Legacy

Established historical PbtA as viable genre. Honored 588th Regiment women's heroism. Influenced military gaming and women's history representation. Confirmed Morningstar as master of historical games.
