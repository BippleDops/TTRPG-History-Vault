## Overview

*Chainmail* is a set of medieval miniatures wargame rules written by Gary Gygax and Jeff Perren and published by Guidon Games in 1971. Its short "Fantasy Supplement" added wizards, heroes, dragons and other fantastic creatures to the man-to-man combat rules, which is where much of the vocabulary later used in [[Dungeons & Dragons (1974)]] first appeared in print. The original D&D rules assumed players owned *Chainmail* and referred to it for combat resolution, offering the d20-based "alternative combat system" as an option. A second edition appeared in 1972 and TSR published a third edition in 1975 after acquiring the rights.
