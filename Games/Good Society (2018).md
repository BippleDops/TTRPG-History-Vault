# Good Society

**Publisher**: Storybrewers Roleplaying
**Designer**: Hayley Gordon, Vee Hendro
**Year**: 2018
**System**: Good Society System
**Genre**: regency-romance, Jane-Austen, social-drama
**Status**: in-print

## Overview

Good Society, designed by Hayley Gordon in 2018, creates Jane Austen-style regency romance where players collaboratively build genteel English community with established social hierarchies, navigate courtship and scandal through Resolve mechanic (building tension toward dramatic confessions), write actual letters between characters, and rotate Facilitator role each scene, creating faithful Austen-inspired stories emphasizing social dynamics, reputation, romantic entanglements, and personal growth within rigid class structures.

**Austen Faithful**: Captures Pride and Prejudice/Sense and Sensibility tone perfectly

**No Dice**: Uses token-based Resolve system instead

**Letter Writing**: Players write actual letters between sessions—beautiful integration of medium

**Rotating Facilitator**: No permanent GM; everyone takes turns facilitating scenes

**Social Drama**: Emphasis on manners, reputation, courtship, scandal

**Playbook-Less**: Create characters through collaborative discussion rather than fixed archetypes

## Core Mechanics

**Resolve Tokens**: Build Resolve through tension, spend for dramatic moments (confessions, confrontations)

**Letter Writing**: Between sessions, write letters in-character deepening relationships

**Facilitator Rotation**: Each scene, different player facilitates

**Desire and Duty**: Characters pulled between what they want and what's proper

**Reputation**: Social standing tracked and affected by scandal/success

**Courtship**: Structured romantic progression

**Secret Desires**: Hidden wants creating dramatic tension

## Setting

**Regency England**: Early 19th century English countryside and London

**Social Hierarchy**: Class structure central to dynamics

**Manners and Propriety**: Social rules constraining behavior

**Marriage Market**: Courtship as economic and social negotiation

**Themes**: Love vs. duty, class barriers, social expectations, personal growth, reputation, scandal

## Cultural Impact

Brought regency romance to gaming successfully. Demonstrated Austen-style social drama viable as RPG genre. Letter-writing mechanic widely praised. Resolve system influenced token economies. ENnie Award recognition validated regency gaming.

## Reception

Praised for faithful Austen adaptation, elegant Resolve mechanics, letter-writing brilliance, rotating facilitator. Austen fans embraced enthusiastically. Gentle gameplay attracted non-traditional gamers.

## Legacy

Established regency romance as recognized RPG genre. Letter-writing mechanics influenced other games. Demonstrated social drama without combat could sustain campaigns. Resolve tokens appeared in derivative designs.
